## Day by day

*Days in this plan start around 09:30 and end by 22:00, at a balanced pace (the defaults). For another rhythm, tell the concierge, e.g. "we wake up early, start at 07:00", "we stay out late" or "a more relaxed pace, please".*

### Day 1 – Sat 2026‑11‑21: Indre By
*Weather*: partly cloudy, 3‑7 °C • Walking: 1.3 km
- **12:00 Arrive in Copenhagen** – Flight with SAS (see Getting there and back).
- **13:00 Riz Raz** – All‑you‑can‑eat vegetarian buffet; good spot to try Danish open‑sandwiches (smørrebrød). Low‑budget. [More info](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Copenhagen/Indre_By).
- **16:30 Ny Carlsberg Glyptotek** – Founded by brewer Carl Jacobsen, opened 1897; houses ancient Mediterranean sculpture and French Impressionist paintings plus a subtropical winter garden. Open Tue‑We, Fri‑Sun 10:00‑17:00; Thu 10:00‑21:00. ticket €16.72. [Source](https://www.openstreetmap.org/relation/1322262).
- **19:30 Fodkold** – Hot‑dog stand; try the classic Danish red sausage (rød pølse). Low‑budget. [Source](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Copenhagen/Indre_By).

### Day 2 – Sun 2026‑11‑22: Indre BY
*Weather*: partly cloudy, 1‑3 °C • Walking: 2.9 km
- **10:00 Botanical Garden (University of Copenhagen)** – Historic Palm House and plant collections; free entry. Open Oct‑Mar 08:30‑16:00; Apr‑Sep 08:30‑18:00. [Source](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/3098803).
- **11:30 Rosenborg Castle** – Early‑17th‑century royal residence now housing the Crown Jewels. Open Mo‑Su 10:00‑17:00. ticket price: see the website. [Source](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Copenhagen/Indre_By).
- **12:30 Fresh Bagel** – Low‑budget bagel shop; quick bite near Rosenborg Castle. [Source](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Copenhagen/Indre_By).
- **13:45 Rundetårn (Round Tower)** – Built 1649 as an astronomical observatory; features a helical ramp to the observation platform with city views. Open May‑Sep Mo‑Su 10:00‑20:00; Oct‑Apr Mo, Thu‑Su 10:00‑18:00; Tue‑We 10:00‑21:00. ticket price: see the website. [Source](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/1011492033).
- **14:45 Amalienborg Palace** – Current royal residence of four identical Rococo palaces; watch the daily Changing of the Guard. Open 11:00‑16:00. ticket price: see the website. [Source](http://kongehuset.dk/english/palaces/amalienborg).
- **19:20 Depart from Copenhagen** – Flight with Finnair (see Getting there and back).
