## Day by day

*Days in this plan start around 09:30 and end by 22:00, at a balanced pace (the defaults). For another rhythm, tell the concierge, e.g. "we wake up early, start at 07:00", "we stay out late" or "a more relaxed pace, please".*

### Day 1 – Wed 2027-03-10: Central Reykjavik
*Weather:* snow, 0‑4 °C • Walk ≈ 2.7 km
- **09:15 Arrive in Reykjavik** – Flight with Finnair, see *Getting there and back*.
- **11:00 Perlan** – A natural‑history museum on Öskjuhlíð hill featuring a planetarium, ice cave and panoramic deck. Open 10:00‑21:00. ticket €33.2. [source](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/25795245)
- **13:30 Bæjarins beztu pylsur** – Iconic hot‑dog stand; try the famous Icelandic lamb hot‑dog. Open 24/7. *low* budget tier. [source](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Reykjav%C3%ADk)
- **18:30 Sægreifinn** – Seafood shack known for lobster soup and grilled fish. Winter 11:30‑22:00, Summer 11:30‑23:00. *low* budget tier. [source](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Reykjav%C3%ADk)

### Day 2 – Thu 2027-03-11: Central Reykjavik
*Weather:* light snow, -2‑2 °C • Walk ≈ 1.2 km
- **10:00 Harpa** – Striking concert hall with coloured‑glass façade, home to the Icelandic Symphony Orchestra. Open daily 10:00‑00:00. No ticket fee. [source](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Reykjav%C3%ADk)
- **11:15 Icelandic Phallological Museum** – Quirky collection of mammal penises, opened 1997. Open 10:00‑19:00. No ticket fee. [source](https://www.openstreetmap.org/node/1511563888)
- **12:30 The Culture House (Safnahúsið)** – Exhibition space of the National Museum, hosts rotating art shows. Open 10:00‑17:00. No ticket fee. [source](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/94560552)
- **13:45 Kaffismiðja Íslands** – Cozy café; great for coffee and Icelandic pastries. Open Mon‑Fri 08:30‑17:00, Sat 10:00‑17:00. *low* budget tier. [source](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Reykjav%C3%ADk)
- **18:30 Austur-Indíafjelagið** – One of Reykjavik’s few Indian restaurants; warm indoor option. *mid* budget tier. [source](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Reykjav%C3%ADk)

### Day 3 – Fri 2027-03-12: Central Reykjavik
*Weather:* snow, -3‑0 °C • Walk ≈ 4.7 km
- **10:00 Hallgrímskirkja** – Iconic Lutheran church with a towering spire; a national symbol completed in 1986. Open 09:00‑20:00. ticket €10. [source](https://www.openstreetmap.org/relation/6184378)
- **12:00 Kjarvalsstaðir (Reykjavik Art Museum)** – Permanent collection of painter Jóhannes Kjarval plus temporary shows. Open 10:00‑17:00. No ticket fee listed. [source](https://www.openstreetmap.org/node/971164356)
- **13:45 Múlakaffi** – Office‑café style; order the hearty Icelandic kjötsúpa or fresh fish. *low* budget tier. [source](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Reykjav%C3%ADk)
- **18:30 Messinn** – Friendly spot serving generous portions of fresh fish. Open 11:00‑15:00 & 17:00‑22:00. *mid* budget tier. [source](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Reykjav%C3%ADk)

### Day 4 – Sat 2027-03-13: Central Reykjavik
*Weather:* snow, -1‑2 °C • Walk ≈ 0.7 km
- **10:30 National Museum of Iceland** – Presents Icelandic history from settlement to today; founded 1863. Open 10:00‑17:00. No ticket fee. [source](https://www.openstreetmap.org/relation/6180675)
- **12:30 The Icelandic Bar** – Traditional fare such as lamb shank and skyr dessert; set menus start around 4000 kr. *mid* budget tier. [source](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Reykjav%C3%ADk)
- **19:00 Free dinner near centre** – Simple snack; no low‑budget restaurant left.

### Day 5 – Sun 2027-03-14: Central Reykjavik
*Weather:* light snow, -2‑3 °C • Walk ≈ 1.2 km
- **10:00 Reykjavik Art Museum – Hafnarhús** – Contemporary exhibitions; ticket €14.7 (included in Reykjavik Art Museum pass). Open Thu 10:00‑20:00, Fri‑Wed 10:00‑17:00. [source](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Reykjav%C3%ADk)
- **12:00 Free lunch near centre** – Quick bite.
- **17:15 Dill** – High‑end New Nordic tasting menu; reservation recommended. *high* budget tier. [source](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Reykjav%C3%ADk)
- **20:45 Depart from Reykjavik** – Flight with Wizz Air, SAS; see *Getting there and back*.

## Food to try
- **Hákarl** – National dish of Iceland consisting of fermented shark. [source](https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Q1703796)
- **þorramatur** – Selection of traditional Icelandic food. [source](https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Q335626)
- **Vínarterta** – Icelandic cake traditionally layered with prune jam. [source](https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Q16430337)
- **Rúgbrauð** – Icelandic rye bread baked underground using geothermal heat. [source](https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Q1344219)
- **Laufabrauð** – Thin, leaf‑shaped fried rye flatbread, traditionally made for Þorrablót. [source](https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Q1515205)
- **Flatkaka** – Traditional Icelandic rye flatbread. [source](https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Q1427307)
- **Slatur** – Icelandic dish made from sheep offal, often served as a sausage or pudding. [source](https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Q7538980)
- **Ábrystir** – Icelandic dessert resembling a custard or flan. [source](https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Q3480481)
