Day 1 – Sat 2027-05-15: Old Town

Weather: overcast, 9‑18 °C Walking: ~0.7 km

  • 13:23 Arrive in Tallinn – Viking Line ferry arrival. (see Getting there)
  • 14:15 Toompea Castle – Medieval castle on Toompea hill, now housing the Estonian Parliament. It shows layers of Danish, German and Russian influence. Open 10:00–16:00. Ticket: ticket price: see the website. source
  • 15:45 St. Nicholas Church – 16th‑century church home to the Museum of St. Nicholas and the famous Danse Macabre altarpiece. Open 10:00–17:00. Ticket €3.5. source
  • 17:00 Dinner at Loca – Low‑price fast‑food joint serving pizza, falafel and hummus. Budget‑tier: low. Open 11:00–07:00. source
  • 19:11 Depart from Tallinn – Tallink Grupp AS ferry back to Helsinki.

Food to try

  • Verivorst – Pig's blood sausage, traditionally served with lingonberry jam and mulgikapsad. source
  • Mustamakkara – Finnish black‑pudding‑like blood sausage, a common winter dish similar to verivorst. source
  • Mulgikapsad – Sauerkraut stew often served alongside blood sausage. source
  • Hapukoor – Sour 20 %‑fat milk dressing used in salads, the Estonian equivalent of Russian smetana. source
  • Kartulisalat – Traditional Estonian potato salad, usually dressed with sour cream. source
  • Kohuke – Flavoured milk‑curd sweet covered in chocolate, a popular snack sold in supermarkets. source
