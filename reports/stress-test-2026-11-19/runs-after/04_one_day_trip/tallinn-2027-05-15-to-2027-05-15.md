## Food to try
- **Verivorst** – Pig's blood sausage, traditionally served with lingonberry jam and mulgikapsad. [source](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Estonia#Eat)
- **Mustamakkara** – Finnish black‑pudding‑like blood sausage, a common winter dish similar to verivorst. [source](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Estonia#Eat)
- **Mulgikapsad** – Sauerkraut stew often served alongside blood sausage. [source](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Estonia#Eat)
- **Hapukoor** – Sour 20 %‑fat milk dressing used in salads, the Estonian equivalent of Russian smetana. [source](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Estonia#Eat)
- **Kartulisalat** – Traditional Estonian potato salad, usually dressed with sour cream. [source](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Estonia#Eat)
- **Kohuke** – Flavoured milk‑curd sweet covered in chocolate, a popular snack sold in supermarkets. [source](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Estonia#Eat)
