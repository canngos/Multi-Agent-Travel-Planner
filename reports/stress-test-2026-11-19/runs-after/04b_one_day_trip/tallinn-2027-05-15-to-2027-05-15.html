Day 1 – Sat 2027-05-15: Tallinn Old Town

Weather: overcast, 9‑18 °C Walking: ~0.7 km

  • 13:25 Arrive in Tallinn – Ferry with Viking Line (see Getting there and back).
  • 13:30 Lunch – Kohvik Energia – Inexpensive cafeteria offering soups, sandwiches, salads and pastries (price €1‑10). Recommended low‑budget spot. source
  • 15:00 Town Hall Square – Central UNESCO‑listed square surrounded by historic buildings; great for photos and atmosphere. source
  • 15:45 Town Hall – 14‑15th‑century town hall museum (free entry). Open Mo‑Sa 10:00‑18:00; closed Sun. [ticket free] source
  • 17:00 Take tram to harbour – Tram (≈5 min) from Old Town to Tallink harbour; arrive ~17:05.
  • 19:14 Depart from Tallinn – Ferry with Tallink Grupp AS (see Getting there and back).

Food to try

  • verivorst – Estonia's national pig's blood sausage, similar to black pudding, usually served with lingonberry jam. source
  • mulgikapsad – Sauerkraut stew often served alongside verivorst. source
  • hapukoor – 20 %‑fat sour cream used as a dressing for salads and other dishes. source
  • kartulisalat – Estonian style potato salad, typically served with meat dishes. source
  • kohuke – Chocolate‑covered curd snack, widely available in Estonian supermarkets. source
  • mustamakkara – Finnish‑style black‑blood sausage, similar to verivorst, popular in winter. source
