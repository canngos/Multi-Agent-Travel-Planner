### Day 1 – Sat 2027-05-15: Tallinn Old Town
**Weather:** overcast, 9‑18 °C **Walking:** ~0.7 km

- **13:25 Arrive in Tallinn** – Ferry with Viking Line (see Getting there and back).
- **13:30 Lunch – Kohvik Energia** – Inexpensive cafeteria offering soups, sandwiches, salads and pastries (price €1‑10). Recommended low‑budget spot. [source](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Tallinn)

- **17:00 Take tram to harbour** – Tram (≈5 min) from Old Town to Tallink harbour; arrive ~17:05.
- **19:14 Depart from Tallinn** – Ferry with Tallink Grupp AS (see Getting there and back).

## Food to try
- **verivorst** – Estonia's national pig's blood sausage, similar to black pudding, usually served with lingonberry jam. [source](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Estonia#Eat)
- **mulgikapsad** – Sauerkraut stew often served alongside verivorst. [source](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Estonia#Eat)
- **hapukoor** – 20 %‑fat sour cream used as a dressing for salads and other dishes. [source](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Estonia#Eat)
- **kartulisalat** – Estonian style potato salad, typically served with meat dishes. [source](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Estonia#Eat)
- **kohuke** – Chocolate‑covered curd snack, widely available in Estonian supermarkets. [source](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Estonia#Eat)
- **mustamakkara** – Finnish‑style black‑blood sausage, similar to verivorst, popular in winter. [source](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Estonia#Eat)
