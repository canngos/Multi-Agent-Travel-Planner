## Day by day
*Days in this plan start around 09:30 and end by 22:00, at a balanced pace (the defaults). For another rhythm, tell the concierge, e.g. "we wake up early, start at 07:00", "we stay out late" or "a more relaxed pace, please".*

### Day 1 – Mon 2027‑04‑05: In‑flight
- **20:25 Depart from Helsinki** – Flight with Finnair & China Eastern Airlines (see Getting there and back).

### Day 2 – Tue 2027‑04‑06: Asakusa
- Weather: light drizzle, 15‑22 °C – no rain expected.
- **20:55 Arrive in Tokyo** – Same flight as above.
- **21:30 Sushi Kanesaka** – Two‑sticker Michelin‑star sushi dinner; try the seasonal omakase. *Budget tier*: high. [Details](http://www.sushi-kanesaka.com/)

### Day 3 – Wed 2027‑04‑07: Asakusa / Ueno (4.4 km walk)
- Weather: light drizzle, 9‑18 °C.
- **10:00 Mitsubishi Ichigokan Museum** – The museum, opened 2014, recreates the historic 1894 Mitsubishi Ichigokan building and showcases Meiji‑Era Western art. Hours: Sa‑Su, Tu‑Th 10:00‑18:00; Fr, We 10:00‑20:00; Mo off. Ticket: *ticket price: see the website*.[source](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/306116935)
- **12:30 Tokyo Station Gallery** – Contemporary exhibitions inside the Neo‑Renaissance Tokyo Station building. Hours: Tu‑Th 10:00‑18:00, Fr 10:00‑20:00, Sa‑Su 10:00‑18:00. Ticket: *ticket price: see the website*.[source](https://www.openstreetmap.org/node/3164340661)
- **13:30 Lunch near Ueno** – Grab a quick bite at a street‑food stall in Ueno.
- **15:00 National Museum of Modern Art, Tokyo** – Japan’s first national museum of modern art, displaying works from the Meiji era onward. Hours: Sa‑Th 10:00‑17:00; Fr 10:00‑20:00. Ticket: *ticket price: see the website*.[source](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/43934324)
- **16:30 Kyū-Iwasaki-Tei Garden** – Former Iwasaki family estate with Western‑style house, Japanese house and historic billiard house, illustrating Meiji‑era architecture. Ticket: *ticket price: see the website*.[source](https://opentripmap.com/en/card/W577424968)
- **19:30 Dinner near Asakusa** – Casual izakaya dinner nearby (no specific venue).

### Day 4 – Thu 2027‑04‑08: Asakusa (2.0 km walk)
- Weather: mainly clear, 7‑17 °C.
- **09:30 Sensō‑ji** – Tokyo’s oldest Buddhist temple, founded 628 and rebuilt after WWII; famous Kaminarimon gate and Nakamise‑dōri shopping street. Hours: 06:00‑17:00 (main hall); grounds open all day. Ticket: free entry.[source](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Tokyo/Asakusa)
- **12:00 Edo Tokyo Museum** – Interactive models and exhibits depicting everyday life in Edo‑period Tokyo. Hours: Su, Tu‑Fr 09:30‑17:30; Sa 09:30‑19:30; Mo off. Ticket: *ticket price: see the website*.[source](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/76187056)
- **15:00 Lunch near Asakusa** – Street‑food on Nakamise‑dōri (onigiri, tempura).
- **19:00 Dinner near Asakusa** – Kaiten‑sushi plate at a local conveyor‑belt shop.

### Day 5 – Fri 2027‑04‑09: Harajuku / Shibuya (7.0 km walk)
- Weather: heavy drizzle, 11‑18 °C.
- **10:00 Meiji Shrine** – Grand Shinto shrine erected 1920 to honor Emperor Meiji; set in a forested approach in Harajuku. Ticket: free entry.[source](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Tokyo/Harajuku)
- **12:30 Blumare** – Low‑budget pizza & pasta spot near Harajuku; good for a casual lunch. *Budget tier*: low.[source](https://www.ginzalion.jp/shop/brand/blumare/shop90.html)
- **13:00 Tokyo Tower** – 332.9 m lattice tower completed 1958, iconic symbol of Tokyo with observation decks. Hours: Mo‑Su 09:00‑23:00. Ticket: *ticket price: see the website*.[source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tokyo_Tower)
- **16:00 Shibuya Crossing** – World‑famous pedestrian scramble.
- **19:00 Ginza Shiseido Parlour** – Swank Japanese‑Western dining; try the lobster‑abalone curry. *Budget tier*: high.

### Day 6 – Sat 2027‑04‑10: Mitaka / Odaiba (20.5 km walk)
- Weather: rain, 16‑19 °C – rain expected.
- **10:00 Ghibli Museum** – Whimsical museum showcasing Studio Ghibli’s animation legacy; opened 2001. Hours: 10:00‑18:00 (closed Tuesdays). Ticket: *ticket price: see the website*.[source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ghibli_Museum)
- **13:30 Lunch near Mitaka** – Grab a bento from a convenience store (rain‑proof).
- **15:00 teamLab Planets** – Immersive water‑filled digital art experience; shoes removed. Hours: Mo‑Su 09:00‑22:00. Ticket: *ticket price: see the website*.[source](https://planets.teamlab.art/tokyo/)
- **19:00 Dinner near teamLab Planets** – Local izakaya serving warm ramen.

### Day 7 – Sun 2027‑04‑11: Shiodome
- Weather: overcast, 15‑24 °C.
- **02:00 Depart from Tokyo** – Flight with Korean Air & China Southern Airlines (see Getting there and back).
