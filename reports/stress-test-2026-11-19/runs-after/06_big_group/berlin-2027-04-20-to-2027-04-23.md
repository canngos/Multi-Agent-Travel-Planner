## Day by day
*Days in this plan start around 09:30 and end by 22:00, at a balanced pace (the defaults). For another rhythm, tell the concierge, e.g. "we wake up early, start at 07:00", "we stay out late" or "a more relaxed pace, please".*

### Day 1 – Tue 2027-04-20: Mitte
*Weather:* heavy drizzle, 4‑12 °C, rainy – **walk 3.1 km**
- **08:05 Arrive in Berlin** – Flight arrival (Finnair). [source](https://kiwi.com/u/3bhxrxfc9h)
- **10:00 German Historical Museum** – Covers German history from pre‑history to the present. *Hours:* 10:00‑18:00. *Ticket:* ticket €8. [source](https://www.openstreetmap.org/node/3203167561)
- **12:30 Witty's Currywurst** – Organic currywurst and fries. *Budget:* low. [source](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Berlin/Mitte)
- **14:00 Neues Museum** – Archaeological collection, famous for the bust of Nefertiti. *Hours:* 10:00‑18:00. *Ticket:* ticket €12. [source](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/18578139)
- **16:00 Bode Museum** – Sculpture, Byzantine art and numismatics on Museum Island. *Hours:* Tue‑Fri 10:00‑17:00, Sat‑Sun 10:00‑18:00. *Ticket:* ticket €10. [source](https://www.openstreetmap.org/relation/4211594)
- **18:00 DDR Museum** – Interactive exhibition of everyday life in former East Germany. *Hours:* 09:00‑21:00. *Ticket:* ticket €13.5. [source](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/175149971)
- **20:30 Bombay** – Classic Indian dishes. *Budget:* mid. [source](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Berlin/Mitte)

### Day 2 – Wed 2027-04-21: Mitte
*Weather:* overcast, 2‑13 °C – **walk 3.5 km**
- **10:00 Brandenburg Gate** – 18th‑century neoclassical monument, symbol of German reunification. *Hours:* 24/7. *Ticket:* free entry. [source](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Berlin/Mitte)
- **12:30 Curry Wolf am Brandenburger Tor** – Classic currywurst stand near the Gate. *Budget:* low. [source](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Berlin/Mitte)
- **14:00 Memorial to the Murdered Jews of Europe** – Holocaust memorial with underground information centre. *Hours:* 24/7. *Ticket:* free entry. [source](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/172234733)
- **15:30 Topography of Terror** – Museum documenting Nazi institutions and shows a remaining wall stretch. *Hours:* 10:00‑20:00. *Ticket:* free entry. [source](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/24368681)
- **19:00 Die Berliner Republik** – Traditional German fare and beer. *Budget:* mid. [source](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Berlin/Mitte)

### Day 3 – Thu 2027-04-22: Friedrichshain
*Weather:* overcast, 1‑16 °C – **walk 5.6 km**
- **10:00 East Side Gallery** – Open‑air gallery of murals on a 1.3 km stretch of the former Berlin Wall. *Hours:* open all day. *Ticket:* free entry. [source](https://www.museumsportal-berlin.de/en/museums/east-side-gallery)
- **12:30 Dada Falafel** – Falafel sandwiches, vegan‑friendly. *Budget:* low. *Hours:* 10:00‑02:00. [source](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Berlin/Mitte)
- **14:00 St. Hedwig's Cathedral** – Berlin’s largest Catholic cathedral, Baroque‑Neoclassical, survived WWII. *Hours:* Mo‑Sa 10:00‑17:00, Sun 13:00‑17:00. *Ticket:* ticket price: see the website. [source](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/58608090)
- **16:30 Infotafel Berliner Mauer** – On‑site plaque giving information about the Berlin Wall. *Ticket:* ticket price: see the website. [source](https://opentripmap.com/en/card/N1489358328)
- **19:30 Weilands** – Healthy salads and soups. *Budget:* mid. *Hours:* 10:00‑21:30. [source](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Berlin/Mitte)

### Day 4 – Fri 2027-04-23: Mitte
*Weather:* overcast, 4‑15 °C – **walk 0.6 km**

## Food to try
- **currywurst** – fast food dish of German origin. [source](https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Q176063)
- **döner** – the döner is Germany's most loved fast food. [source](https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Q181055)
- **bratwurst** – type of German sausage made from pork or, less commonly, beef, veal, or any combination of these meats. 
- **pretzel** – German baked pastry shaped into a knot. [source](https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Q160525)
- **spaghettieis** – German ice cream dish made to resemble a plate of spaghetti. [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spaghettieis)
- **sauerkraut** – cabbage that has been fermented by lactic acid bacteria. [source](https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Q154166)
