Food to try

  • Wiener schnitzel – Breaded veal schnitzel, a classic Viennese specialty. – source
  • Tafelspitz – Boiled beef served with potatoes, horseradish and apple sauce, traditionally a Sunday dish in Vienna. – source
  • Apfelstrudel – A layered pastry filled with spiced apples, a beloved Austrian dessert. – source
  • Kaiserschmarren – Fluffy shredded pancake sprinkled with powdered sugar and served with fruit compote. – source
  • Sachertorte – Rich chocolate cake with a thin apricot glaze, a famous Viennese confection. – source
  • Germknödel – Steamed sweet yeast dumpling filled with plum jam, topped with butter and poppy seeds. – source
