## Day by day

*Days in this plan start around 09:30 and end by 22:00, at a balanced pace (the defaults). For another rhythm, tell the concierge, e.g. "we wake up early, start at 07:00", "we stay out late" or "a more relaxed pace, please".*

### Day 1 – Thu 2027-05-06: Innere Stadt
*Weather:* light drizzle, 16‑26 °C – **walk 6.2 km**
- **08:25 Arrive in Vienna** – flight with airBaltic (see Getting there and back).
- **10:00 Burggarten** – historic park created for the royal family after the city fortifications were destroyed by Napoleon. Wheelchair‑friendly paths. Ticket: ticket price: see the website. [source](https://opentripmap.com/en/card/W8063831)
- **11:30 Volksgarten** – public garden laid out in 1821 on the site of former city fortifications, part of the Hofburg complex. Ticket: ticket price: see the website. [source](https://opentripmap.com/en/card/W8063768)
- **13:00 Rathauspark** – 40 000 m² city‑hall park created in the 1860s at Emperor Franz Joseph I’s request. Ticket: ticket price: see the website. [source](https://opentripmap.com/en/card/R7735480)
- **13:45 Palmenhaus** – mid‑range, child‑friendly restaurant; try a Wiener schnitzel. Budget tier: mid. [source](http://www.palmenhaus.at/)
- **14:30 Augarten** – the oldest Baroque park in Vienna, 52.2 ha of French‑style gardens. Wheelchair‑friendly. Ticket: ticket price: see the website. [source](https://opentripmap.com/en/card/W8044066)
- **18:00 Le Bol** – fine French‑Provencal cuisine, good for an early dinner. Budget tier: mid. [source](http://www.lebol.at)

### Day 2 – Fri 2027-05-07: Innere Stadt
*Weather:* drizzle, 14‑21 °C – **walk 4.0 km**
- **09:30 Stadtpark** – large municipal park stretching from the Ringstraße to Heumarkt, with statues of famous artists and the historic Kursalon. Ticket: ticket price: see the website. [source](https://opentripmap.com/en/card/W551031461)
- **11:00 House of Music (Haus der Musik)** – interactive museum of sound opened in 2000, housed in the former palace of Archduke Charles. Ticket €19. Hours: Mo‑Su 10:00‑22:00. [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Haus_der_Musik)
- **13:30 Inigo** – modern restaurant with diverse menu and vegetarian options; suitable for families. Budget tier: mid. [source](http://inigo.at/restaurant/)
- **15:00 Grete‑Rehor‑Park** – modest park with a small playground, named after politician Grete Rehor. Free entry. [source](https://opentripmap.com/en/card/W8083418)
- **18:00 Plachutta** – high‑end restaurant famous for Tafelspitz (boiled beef). Budget tier: high. [source](https://www.plachutta-wollzeile.at/)

### Day 3 – Sat 2027-05-08: Innere Stadt
*Weather:* overcast, 11‑21 °C – **walk 2.1 km**
- **10:30 Mozarthaus Vienna** – Mozart’s former residence (1784‑1787), museum of his life and work. Ticket €16. Hours: Mo‑Su 10:00‑19:00. [source](https://www.mozarthausvienna.at/en/plan-your-visit)
- **12:30 St. Stephen's Cathedral (Stephansdom)** – iconic Romanesque‑Gothic cathedral whose construction began in the 12th century; wheelchair‑accessible ramp available. Ticket: ticket price: see the website. Hours: Mo‑Sa 06:00‑22:00; Su 07:00‑22:00. [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/St._Stephen%27s_Cathedral,_Vienna)
- **13:30 Figlmüller** – classic schnitzel house, famous for a giant Wiener schnitzel. Budget tier: mid. [source](http://www.figlmueller.at/en/figlmueller-wollzeile-en.html)
- **14:30 Girardipark** – connected green spaces (Girardipark & Esperantopark) in the 1st district, stroller‑friendly. Ticket: ticket price: see the website. [source](https://opentripmap.com/en/card/R2906770)
- **18:30 Artner am Franziskanerplatz** – upscale wine‑focused venue with creative Austrian‑inspired dishes. Budget tier: high. [source](http://www.artner.co.at/)

## Food to try
- **Wiener schnitzel** – Breaded veal schnitzel, a classic Viennese specialty. – [source](https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Q6497852)
- **Tafelspitz** – Boiled beef served with potatoes, horseradish and apple sauce, traditionally a Sunday dish in Vienna. – [source](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Austria#Eat)
- **Apfelstrudel** – A layered pastry filled with spiced apples, a beloved Austrian dessert. – [source](https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Q59170)
- **Kaiserschmarren** – Fluffy shredded pancake sprinkled with powdered sugar and served with fruit compote. – [source](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Austria#Eat)
- **Sachertorte** – Rich chocolate cake with a thin apricot glaze, a famous Viennese confection. – [source](https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Q131442)
- **Germknödel** – Steamed sweet yeast dumpling filled with plum jam, topped with butter and poppy seeds. – [source](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Austria#Eat)
