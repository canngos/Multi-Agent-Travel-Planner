Food to try

  • Pickled herring (sill) – Pickled herring is eaten with bread or potatoes for summer lunch or as a starter on the smörgåsbord. source
  • Gravlax – Many forms of salmon, especially cured salmon (gravlax), are a traditional Swedish dish. source
  • Swedish meatballs (köttbullar) – Meatballs are the internationally most famous Swedish dish, served with potatoes, brown sauce and lingonberry jam. source
  • Hash (pytt i panna) – Hash consists of diced meat, onions and potatoes, fried together and served with sliced beetroots and a fried or boiled egg. source
  • Pea soup (ärtsoppa) – Pea soup with diced pork, followed by thin pancakes, is traditionally eaten on Thursdays. source
  • Renskav – Sliced, sautéed reindeer meat, typically eaten with wild mushrooms, lingonberries and potatoes. source
