## Food to try
- **Pickled herring (sill)** – Pickled herring is eaten with bread or potatoes for summer lunch or as a starter on the smörgåsbord. [source](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Sweden#Eat)
- **Gravlax** – Many forms of salmon, especially cured salmon (gravlax), are a traditional Swedish dish. [source](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Sweden#Eat)
- **Swedish meatballs (köttbullar)** – Meatballs are the internationally most famous Swedish dish, served with potatoes, brown sauce and lingonberry jam. [source](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Sweden#Eat)
- **Hash (pytt i panna)** – Hash consists of diced meat, onions and potatoes, fried together and served with sliced beetroots and a fried or boiled egg. [source](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Sweden#Eat)
- **Pea soup (ärtsoppa)** – Pea soup with diced pork, followed by thin pancakes, is traditionally eaten on Thursdays. [source](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Sweden#Eat)
- **Renskav** – Sliced, sautéed reindeer meat, typically eaten with wild mushrooms, lingonberries and potatoes. [source](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Sweden#Eat)
