### Day 1 – Fri 2027‑01‑15: Central (walk ≈ 5.8 km)  
*Weather:* overcast, –3 °C to –7 °C.  

- **11:30 Arrive in Longyearbyen** – Flight with Finnair, SAS (see Getting there and back).  
- **12:00 Funktionærmessen Restaurant** – Light lunch with French‑inspired Arctic dishes. *High‑budget* restaurant.  
- **14:00 Galleri Svalbard** – Contemporary art gallery hosting works by Kåre Tveter and historic expedition maps. Free entry. *Ticket:* ticket price: see the website. [[source]](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Longyearbyen)  
- **15:00 Gruve 1A Amerikagruva** – Historic coal mine opened in 1906, closed 1958; shows Longyearbyen’s mining heritage. Open all day. *Ticket:* ticket price: see the website. [[source]](https://opentripmap.com/en/card/W686576331)  
- **16:30 24‑Hour Sundial** – Quirky sundial that works 24 hours in summer, a popular photo spot. Free entry. [[source]](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Longyearbyen)  
- **17:30 WildPhoto Travel** – Photography studio offering Arctic landscape tours and northern‑lights tips. Free entry. [[source]](https://www.openstreetmap.org/node/5228487121)  
- **19:30 Huset** – Fine‑dining dinner with Arctic ingredients (e.g., reindeer, seal). *High‑budget* restaurant.  

### Day 2 – Sat 2027‑01‑16: Central (walk ≈ 0.8 km)  
*Weather:* overcast, –3 °C to –8 °C.  

- **09:00 Arctic Husky Travellers** – Husky‑sledding tour (≈3 h) with lunch included. *Ticket:* ticket price: see the website.  
- **12:30 Barentz Pub & Spiseri** – Mid‑budget lunch; try the local pizza. [[source]](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Longyearbyen)  
- **13:30 Svalbard Museum** – Shows Svalbard’s natural and cultural history, from early whalers to modern research. Indoor, open 10:00‑17:00. *Ticket:* ticket price: see the website. [[source]](https://opentripmap.com/en/card/N3526633670)  
- **15:45 North Pole Expedition Museum** – Exhibits on polar aviation and exploration, featuring historic airships. Indoor, open 09:00‑17:00. *Ticket:* ticket price: see the website. [[source]](https://opentripmap.com/en/card/Q18796945)  
- **18:30 Vinterhagen Restaurant** – High‑budget dinner with Arctic seafood (e.g., salmon, whale).  
- **20:00 Northern Lights observation** – Find a dark spot near the town centre; clear sky expected.  

### Day 3 – Sun 2027‑01‑17: Hotel  
*Weather:* snow, rainy, –1 °C to –8 °C (rainy).

## Food to try  

- **Lutefisk** – Traditional fish dish of some Nordic countries. – [source](https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Q890197)  
- **Fårikål** – Lamb stew, Norway’s national dish. – [source](https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Q890360)  
- **Krumkake** – Norwegian waffle rolled into a cone after baking. – [source](https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Q694022)  
- **Lefse** – Soft Norwegian flatbread, often rolled with butter and sugar. – [source](https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Q888720)  
- **Smalahove** – Smoked, salted and boiled sheep’s head, served with swede and potatoes. – [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Smalahove)  
- **Pinnekjøtt** – Cured and steamed lamb ribs, traditionally served at Christmas. – [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pinnekj%C3%B8tt)
