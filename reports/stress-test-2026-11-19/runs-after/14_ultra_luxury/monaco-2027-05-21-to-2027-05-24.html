Day 1 – Fri 2027‑05‑21: Monte‑Carlo

  • Weather: partly cloudy, 15‑25 °C, no rain. Walk: 1.7 km.
  • 09:10 Arrive in Monaco – Flight with Finnair (see Getting there and back).
  • 11:00 Prince's Palace of Monaco – The official residence of the Sovereign Prince, originally a 12th‑century Genoese fortress. Open 10:00‑18:00. ticket €10. source
  • 12:30 Chez Roger – Local spot; try the socca (thin chickpea pancake, €3). Low‑budget. source
  • 13:30 Oceanographic Museum of Monaco – Founded by Prince Albert I (1910), marine‑science museum with historic vessels. Open 10:00‑20:00. ticket €19. source
  • 14:30 Musée des Timbres et des Monnaies – Monaco’s stamp and coin collection, displaying postal history from 1640. Open 09:30‑17:00. ticket €3. source
  • 18:30 Beefbar – Premium beef cuts, extensive wine list. Mid‑budget. source

Day 2 – Sat 2027‑05‑22: Monte‑Carlo

  • Weather: clear, 18‑25 °C, no rain. Walk: 1.7 km.
  • 09:30 Port Hercules – Monaco’s deep‑water harbour, home to super‑yachts. No ticket needed. source
  • 12:00 Café de Paris – Iconic people‑watching café, Mediterranean bites. Mid‑budget. source
  • 13:30 Opéra de Monaco – Garnier‑style opera house built 1879, offers guided tours. Open Tue‑Sat 10:00‑17:30. source
  • 15:00 Monte Carlo Casino – Historic gaming rooms and atrium. Entry free, gaming rooms require ID. Open 14:00‑04:00. source
  • 16:00 Port Hercules (second visit) – Enjoy the harbour ambience.
  • 19:30 Louis XV – Three‑star Michelin seafood tasting menu at Hôtel de Paris. High‑budget. source

Day 3 – Sun 2027‑05‑23: Monte‑Carlo

  • Weather: overcast, 18‑27 °C, no rain. Walk: 3.8 km.
  • 10:00 Japanese Garden – Zen garden with waterfalls and tea house, designed by Yasuo Beppu. Free entry. Open Apr‑Oct 09:00‑18:45. source
  • 11:30 Nouveau Musée National – Villa Sauber – Contemporary art exhibitions in a Belle Epoque villa. Ticket €6. Open 10:00‑18:00. source
  • 13:00 Maison des pâtes Saint‑Charles – Freshly made pasta with regional sauces. Low‑budget. source
  • 15:00 Jardin Exotique – Cliffside garden of succulents with panoramic views. Ticket €6. Open May‑Sep 09:00‑19:00. source
  • 17:30 Yacht Club de Monaco – Exclusive harbour club, admire the super‑yachts. Open 24 h. Ticket price: see the website. source
  • 19:30 Lo Sfizio Street – Terrace pizza with local toppings. Low‑budget. source

Day 4 – Mon 2027‑05‑24: Monte‑Carlo

  • Weather: overcast, 19‑28 °C, no rain. Walk: 0.7 km.
  • 10:00 Monaco Top Cars Collection – Displays historic and modern racing cars, reflecting Prince Albert II’s motoring passion. Ticket €10. Open Sep‑May 10:00‑18:00. source
  • 12:00 Jardin Exotique (re‑visit) – Succulent plants and views. Ticket €6. source
  • 13:30 Mickey’s Pizza – Classic wood‑oven pizza, quick bite. Low‑budget. source
  • 19:10 Depart from Monaco – Flight with Finnair (see Getting there and back).

Food to try

  • Socca – Thin, crispy chickpea pancake traditionally cooked on a hot stone, served hot from the oven. source
  • Pissaladière – Rustic tart topped with caramelized onions, anchovies, and olives, often enjoyed warm as a snack. source
  • Barbajuan – Crisp fried pastry filled with Swiss chard, ricotta, leeks, garlic and herbs. source
  • Bouillabaisse – Hearty Provençal fish stew made with assorted Mediterranean fish, tomatoes, saffron and garlic. source
  • Stocafi – Stew of dried cod cooked with tomatoes, olives, garlic and herbs, typical for family gatherings. source
