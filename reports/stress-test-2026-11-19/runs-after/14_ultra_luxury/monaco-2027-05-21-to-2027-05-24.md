## Day by day

*Days in this plan start around 09:30 and end by 22:00, at a balanced pace (the defaults). For another rhythm, tell the concierge, e.g. "we wake up early, start at 07:00", "we stay out late" or "a more relaxed pace, please".*

### Day 1 – Fri 2027‑05‑21: Monte‑Carlo
- **Weather**: partly cloudy, 15‑25 °C, no rain.  Walk: 1.7 km.
- **09:10 Arrive in Monaco** – Flight with Finnair (see Getting there and back).
- **11:00 Prince's Palace of Monaco** – The official residence of the Sovereign Prince, originally a 12th‑century Genoese fortress. Open 10:00‑18:00. ticket €10. [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prince%27s_Palace_of_Monaco)
- **12:30 Chez Roger** – Local spot; try the **socca** (thin chickpea pancake, €3). Low‑budget. [source](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Monaco)
- **13:30 Oceanographic Museum of Monaco** – Founded by Prince Albert I (1910), marine‑science museum with historic vessels. Open 10:00‑20:00. ticket €19. [source](https://musee.oceano.org)
- **14:30 Musée des Timbres et des Monnaies** – Monaco’s stamp and coin collection, displaying postal history from 1640. Open 09:30‑17:00. ticket €3. [source](https://www.mtm-monaco.mc/)
- **18:30 Beefbar** – Premium beef cuts, extensive wine list. Mid‑budget. [source](http://beefbar.com/)

### Day 2 – Sat 2027‑05‑22: Monte‑Carlo
- **Weather**: clear, 18‑25 °C, no rain.  Walk: 1.7 km.
- **09:30 Port Hercules** – Monaco’s deep‑water harbour, home to super‑yachts. No ticket needed. [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Port_Hercules)
- **12:00 Café de Paris** – Iconic people‑watching café, Mediterranean bites. Mid‑budget. [source](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Monaco)
- **13:30 Opéra de Monaco** – Garnier‑style opera house built 1879, offers guided tours. Open Tue‑Sat 10:00‑17:30. [source](http://www.opera.mc/)
- **15:00 Monte Carlo Casino** – Historic gaming rooms and atrium. Entry free, gaming rooms require ID. Open 14:00‑04:00. [source](http://www.casinomontecarlo.com)
- **16:00 Port Hercules (second visit)** – Enjoy the harbour ambience.
- **19:30 Louis XV** – Three‑star Michelin seafood tasting menu at Hôtel de Paris. High‑budget. [source](https://www.montecarlosbm.com/en/restaurant-monaco/le-louis-xv-alain-ducasse-hotel-de-paris)

### Day 3 – Sun 2027‑05‑23: Monte‑Carlo
- **Weather**: overcast, 18‑27 °C, no rain.  Walk: 3.8 km.
- **10:00 Japanese Garden** – Zen garden with waterfalls and tea house, designed by Yasuo Beppu. Free entry. Open Apr‑Oct 09:00‑18:45. [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Japanese_Garden,_Monaco)
- **11:30 Nouveau Musée National – Villa Sauber** – Contemporary art exhibitions in a Belle Epoque villa. Ticket €6. Open 10:00‑18:00. [source](http://www.nmnm.mc/)
- **13:00 Maison des pâtes Saint‑Charles** – Freshly made pasta with regional sauces. Low‑budget. [source](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Monaco)
- **15:00 Jardin Exotique** – Cliffside garden of succulents with panoramic views. Ticket €6. Open May‑Sep 09:00‑19:00. [source](http://www.jardin-exotique.mc)
- **17:30 Yacht Club de Monaco** – Exclusive harbour club, admire the super‑yachts. Open 24 h. Ticket price: see the website. [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yacht_Club_de_Monaco)
- **19:30 Lo Sfizio Street** – Terrace pizza with local toppings. Low‑budget. [source](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Monaco)

### Day 4 – Mon 2027‑05‑24: Monte‑Carlo
- **Weather**: overcast, 19‑28 °C, no rain.  Walk: 0.7 km.
- **10:00 Monaco Top Cars Collection** – Displays historic and modern racing cars, reflecting Prince Albert II’s motoring passion. Ticket €10. Open Sep‑May 10:00‑18:00. [source](https://www.mtcc.mc/)
- **12:00 Jardin Exotique (re‑visit)** – Succulent plants and views. Ticket €6. [source](http://www.jardin-exotique.mc)
- **13:30 Mickey’s Pizza** – Classic wood‑oven pizza, quick bite. Low‑budget. [source](https://www.mickeyspizza-monaco.com/)
- **19:10 Depart from Monaco** – Flight with Finnair (see Getting there and back).

## Food to try
- **Socca** – Thin, crispy chickpea pancake traditionally cooked on a hot stone, served hot from the oven. [source](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Monaco#Eat)
- **Pissaladière** – Rustic tart topped with caramelized onions, anchovies, and olives, often enjoyed warm as a snack. [source](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Monaco#Eat)
- **Barbajuan** – Crisp fried pastry filled with Swiss chard, ricotta, leeks, garlic and herbs. [source](https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Q2294743)
- **Bouillabaisse** – Hearty Provençal fish stew made with assorted Mediterranean fish, tomatoes, saffron and garlic. [source](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Monaco#Eat)
- **Stocafi** – Stew of dried cod cooked with tomatoes, olives, garlic and herbs, typical for family gatherings. [source](https://esim.holafly.com/travel-tips/monaco-food)
