## Day by day
*Days in this plan start around 09:30 and end by 22:00, at a balanced pace (the defaults). For another rhythm, tell the concierge, e.g. "we wake up early, start at 07:00", "we stay out late" or "a more relaxed pace, please".*

### Day 1 – Fri 2027-06-04: Mariehamn centre
- **Weather:** drizzle, 14‑18 °C (not rainy). No walking distance specified.
- **16:00 Arrive in Mariehamn** – Flight with Finnair, see *Getting there and back*.
- **19:30 Cafe Julius** – Try the Ålandspannkaka, a local sweet pancake. Low‑budget. [source](https://julle.ax/fi/)

### Day 2 – Sat 2027-06-05: Western Mariehamn
- **Weather:** light rain, 12‑16 °C (rainy). **Walk:** 2.6 km.
- **10:00 Åland Maritime Museum** – Explore Åland's seafaring heritage; tickets €16.5. Open 10:00‑17:00. *Summary:* The museum preserves the islands’ maritime history, showing ship models and the famous Pommern barque. [source](https://sjofartsmuseum.ax)
- **11:45 Pommern (museum ship)** – Visit the historic four‑masted barque built in Glasgow 1903, now a museum ship. Ticket price: see the website. Open 10:00‑17:00. [source](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/155085207)
- **13:00 Hesburger Mariehamn** – Quick fast‑food lunch. Low‑budget. [source](https://www.hesburger.com/restaurants?tid=167)
- **14:30 Tullarns äng Nature Area** – Walk the marked trails in the protected forest meadow; free entry. [source](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Mariehamn)
- **19:00 Pizza24 machine** – Grab a hot pizza from the 24/7 vending machine. Low‑budget. [source](https://www.pizza24.ax/)

### Day 3 – Sun 2027-06-06: Mariehamn
- **Weather:** rain, 11‑15 °C (rainy). No walking distance specified.
- **09:50 Depart from Mariehamn** – Flight with Finnair, see *Getting there and back*.

## Food to try
- **kalakukko** – traditional food from the Finnish region of Savonia. [source](https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Q612661)
- **Karelian pasty** – traditional pasties from Karelia. [source](https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Q598113)
- **mämmi** – Finnish Easter dessert. [source](https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Q1630666)
- **Runeberg's torte** – Finnish pastry flavored with almonds and arrack or rum. [source](https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Q1817903)
- **semla** – traditional Scandinavian sweet roll associated with Shrove Tuesday. [source](https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Q1139319)
- **rice pudding** – dish made from rice with milk. [source](https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Q19029)
