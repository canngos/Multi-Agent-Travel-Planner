## Day by day
*Days in this plan start around 09:30 and end by 22:00, at a balanced pace (the defaults). For another rhythm, tell the concierge, e.g. "we wake up early, start at 07:00", "we stay out late" or "a more relaxed pace, please".*

### Day 1 – Thu 2027-06-03: Centre
Weather: overcast, 20‑24 °C – walk ~7.8 km
- **09:10 Arrive in Barcelona** – Flight with Norwegian Air Sweden. [source](https://kiwi.com/u/pktbnyt9mf)
- **10:45 Palau de la Música Catalana** – Modernista concert hall, UNESCO site. *Built 1905‑1908, designed by Lluís Domènech i Montaner.* Hours 09:00‑15:30. ticket price: see the website. [source](https://www.palaumusica.cat/)
- **13:30 Alkimia** – Michelin‑starred restaurant serving inventive Catalan cuisine (high‑budget). Consider the tasting menu. [source](http://www.alkimia.cat/?lang=en)
- **15:00 Museu Frederic Marès** – Sculpture and everyday‑object collection. *Shows the personal collection of sculptor Frederic Marès, spanning Romanesque to Renaissance.* Hours 10:00‑19:00. ticket price: see the website. [source](http://www.museumares.bcn.cat/)
- **16:30 Barcelona Cathedral** – Gothic cathedral with historic cloister. *Built 13th‑15th centuries; cloister contains the Well of the Geese.* Hours 09:30‑18:30 (weekdays). ticket price: see the website. [source](https://www.catedralbcn.org/)
- **17:30 Picasso Museum** – Extensive early‑Picasso collection. *Opened 1963, housed in five medieval palaces.* Hours 09:00‑21:00 (Thu‑Sat). ticket price: see the website. [source](https://museupicassobcn.cat)
- **20:45 La Taberna del Cura** – Mid‑budget tapas bar, ~0.5 km from the stay. First courses €4‑12, mains €12‑20. [source](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Barcelona/Gr%C3%A0cia)

### Day 2 – Fri 2027-06-04: Eixample
Weather: rain, 16‑25 °C – walk ~4.7 km
- **09:45 Sagrada Família** – Gaudí's iconic basilica (UNESCO). *Construction since 1882, designed by Antoni Gaudí.* ticket €26. [source](https://sagradafamilia.org/en/sagrada-familia-ticket)
- **12:00 La Rosa del Desierto** – Mid‑budget Moroccan dishes, ~€18 per person. [source](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Barcelona/Gr%C3%A0cia)
- **15:45 Can Xurrades** – Mid‑budget Catalan dishes; try the Ous Estrellats and strawberries with salt & pepper. [source](https://www.facebook.com/canxurradesbarcelona/)
- **17:30 Casa Batlló** – Colourful modernista masterpiece. *Redesigned 1904 by Gaudí, façade like a dragon’s back.* Hours 08:30‑22:30. ticket €15. [source](https://www.casabatllo.es/en/online-tickets)
- **19:30 D.O. vins i platillos** – High‑budget wine bar offering exquisite tapas. [source](http://www.dovinsiplatillos.com/)

### Day 3 – Sat 2027-06-05: Gràcia
Weather: light drizzle, 16‑21 °C – walk ~4.2 km
- **09:30 Park Güell** – Gaudí's whimsical park with city views. *Public park with mosaic benches, originally a private garden city.* Hours 08:00‑21:30. ticket price: see the website. [source](https://www.parkguell.cat/)
- **13:45 Kibuka** – Japanese sushi bar, light lunch. Mid‑budget. [source](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Barcelona/Gr%C3%A0cia)
- **15:00 Casa Vicens** – Gaudí's first major work, oriental‑Mudejar style. *Built 1883‑1885, marks start of Gaudí’s modernista style.* Hours 10:00‑20:00. ticket price: see the website. [source](https://casavicens.org/)
- **16:30 Casa Martí Trias i Domènech** – Architectural work in Gràcia, historic heritage. *Only house built during Güell’s Park Güell urbanisation plan, constructed 1905.* [source](https://opentripmap.com/en/card/Q47170945)
- **17:15 Can Muntaner de Dalt** – Noucentista farmhouse, listed heritage building. *Predates Park Güell, rural house with decorative plasterwork.* [source](https://opentripmap.com/en/card/Q96099488)
- **18:00 Sol i Lluna** – Mid‑budget Mediterranean dinner. [source](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Barcelona/Gr%C3%A0cia)

## Food to try
- **paella** – rice dish from the Valencian Community, Spain. [source](https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Q212121)
- **gazpacho** – cold soup from Spain and Portugal. [source](https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Q202677)
- **churro** – fried‑dough pastry. [source](https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Q727605)
- **aioli** – Provençal sauce made of garlic, olive oil, usually egg yolks, and seasonings. [source](https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Q283231)
- **chorizo** – pork sausage originating in the Iberian Peninsula. [source](https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Q23374)
- **Spanish omelette** – egg, potato and onion dish. [source](https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Q281751)
