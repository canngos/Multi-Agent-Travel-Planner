### Day 2 – Sun 2027-04-04: Paris
*Weather:* drizzle, 11‑18 °C – **walk 0.7 km**
- **09:30 Louvre Museum** – World‑famous national art museum housed in the former Louvre Palace (12th‑13th century). Open 09:00‑18:00. Ticket €9. [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Louvre)
- **12:00 Musée national Eugène Delacroix** – Former townhouse of the Romantic painter, showcasing his works and personal items. Open 09:30‑17:30. Ticket: see the website. [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mus%C3%A9e_national_Eug%C3%A8ne_Delacroix)
- **13:45 Lunch** – Bakery croissant and coffee.
- **19:00 Dinner** – Classic French bistro (no specific venue listed).

### Day 3 – Mon 2027-04-05: Paris
*Weather:* drizzle, 7‑15 °C – **walk 0.8 km**
- **09:30 Musée d'Orsay** – Housed in the former Gare d'Orsay railway station (1898‑1900), it displays French art from 1848‑1914, including the largest Impressionist collection. Ticket €14. [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mus%C3%A9e_d%27Orsay)
- **12:00 Musée de l'Orangerie** – Situated in the Tuileries Garden, famous for Monet’s Water Lilies murals and other Impressionist works. Ticket €11. Open 09:00‑18:00. [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mus%C3%A9e_de_l%27Orangerie)
- **13:45 Lunch** – Fresh croissant at a nearby bakery.
- **14:30 Fondation Custodia** – Collection of European Old Master drawings and prints in the 18th‑century Hôtel Turgot. Ticket: see the website. [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fondation%20Custodia)
- **19:00 Dinner** – Light dinner at a local café.

### Day 4 – Tue 2027-04-06: Paris

- **13:30 Lunch** – Final lunch; enjoy a croissant or quiche from a bakery.

## Food to try
- **Ratatouille** – A classic French vegetable stew made with eggplant, zucchini, peppers, tomatoes, and herbs. [source](https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Q183095)
- **Croissant** – A buttery, flaky pastry shaped into a crescent, iconic of French bakeries. [source](https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Q207832)
- **Bouillabaisse** – A stew of various fish, shellfish and seafood simmered with aromatic vegetables, herbs, fennel, saffron and tomatoes, served with rouille and toasted bread. [source](https://www.196flavors.com?p=135171)
- **Foie gras** – A rich, buttery delicacy made from the fattened liver of a duck or goose, often served as a terrine or mousse. [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Foie_gras)
- **Crêpe** – A thin French pancake made from flour, eggs and milk, served sweet with sugar or savory with fillings like ham and cheese. [source](https://www.abakingjourney.com/basic-french-crepes-recipe)
