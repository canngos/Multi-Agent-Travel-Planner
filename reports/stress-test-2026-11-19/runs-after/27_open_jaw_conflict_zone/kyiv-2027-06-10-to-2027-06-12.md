### Day 1 – Thu 2027-06-10: Kyiv (arrival)
**Weather:** light drizzle, 14.8‑25.7 °C
- **11:40 Arrival** – Flight lands at Chișinău; you’ll need a ground transfer to Kyiv. [source](https://kiwi.com/u/s6t75scptc)
- **18:30 Maidan Nezalezhnosti (Independence Square)** – Central square, historic site of the Orange Revolution and Euromaidan protests. Great for an evening stroll. [source](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Kyiv)
- **20:30 Kureni** – Upscale Ukrainian cuisine; try the tasting menu for a modern take on classic dishes. *Budget tier:* mid. [source](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Kyiv)

### Day 2 – Fri 2027-06-11: Kyiv
**Weather:** light drizzle, 16.7‑27.5 °C
**Walking:** ~2.8 km
- **09:30 Saint Sophia Cathedral** – 11th‑century UNESCO World Heritage cathedral, iconic for its mosaics and frescoes. Open 09:00‑16:00. *Ticket:* see the website. [source](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Kyiv)
- **13:30 Puzata Khata** – Traditional Ukrainian cafeteria‑style lunch (borscht, varenyky, etc.). *Budget tier:* low. [source](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Kyiv)
- **16:30 St. Volodymyr's Cathedral** – Neo‑Byzantine 19th‑century cathedral with rich frescoes. [source](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Kyiv)
- **20:00 Celentano** – Italian dishes and pizza, popular with locals. Open Mon‑Fri 09:00‑22:00, Sat‑Sun 10:00‑22:00. *Budget tier:* low. [source](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Kyiv)

### Day 3 – Sat 2027-06-12: Kyiv (departure)
**Weather:** rain, 12.3‑26.4 °C
- **11:31 Depart from Kyiv** – FlixBus‑eu long‑distance bus back to Helsinki (multiple transfers). [source](https://global.flixbus.com)
