## Day by day

*Days in this plan start around 09:30 and end by 22:00, at a balanced pace (the defaults). For another rhythm, tell the concierge, e.g. "we wake up early, start at 07:00", "we stay out late" or "a more relaxed pace, please".*

### Day 1 – Mon 2027-05-17: Stare Podgórze
- **Weather:** heavy drizzle, 9‑11 °C – walking distance ≈ 3.9 km
- **08:05 Arrive in Krakow** – Flight with Finnair (see Getting there and back).
- **10:30 Town Hall Tower** – The remaining tower of Kraków's medieval town hall, now a museum offering panoramic views over the Old Town from its observation deck. Open Mar‑Oct Mon 10:30‑14:00, Tue‑Sun 10:30‑18:00; ticket €2.27. [source](https://www.muzeumkrakowa.pl/branches/town-hall-tower)
- **12:30 Polakowski** – Low‑budget Polish cafeteria; try the pierogi. (low‑budget)
- **13:30 St Mary's Basilica** – A striking Gothic brick church on the Main Market Square, famous for its wooden altarpiece by Veit Stoss and the hourly hejnał trumpet call from its towers. Open Mon‑Sat 12:00‑18:00, Sun 14:00‑18:00; ticket €2.27. [source](http://www.mariacki.com/)
- **15:00 Barbican** – A massive fortified gate from the 15th‑century city walls, one of the best‑preserved medieval defensive structures in Europe. Open Mo‑Su 10:30‑18:00. Ticket price: see the website. [source](https://muzeumkrakowa.pl/oddzialy/barbakan)
- **18:30 Mały Śledź** – Low‑budget spot, good pierogi and cheap beer. (low‑budget)

### Day 2 – Tue 2027-05-18: Stare Podgórze
- **Weather:** light rain, 8‑14 °C – walking distance ≈ 2.2 km
- **10:00 Old Synagogue** – One of Europe's oldest surviving synagogues, now a museum presenting the history and culture of Kraków's historic Jewish community. Open Mo 10:00‑14:00, Tue‑Su 10:00‑17:00. [source](https://muzeumkrakowa.pl/oddzialy/stara-synagoga)
- **12:30 Judah Food Market** – Street‑food market; try pierogi and zapiekanki. (low‑budget)
- **13:30 Galicia Jewish Museum** – A modern museum dedicated to the Jewish heritage of Galicia, featuring personal testimonies, artifacts, and interactive exhibits. Open Mo‑Su 10:00‑18:00. [source](https://galiciajewishmuseum.org/)
- **15:30 Oskar Schindler's Enamel Factory** – The former enamel factory that saved many Jewish lives during WWII, now a museum documenting the Nazi occupation and Schindler's rescue efforts. Open Mo 10:00‑15:00, Tue‑Su 09:00‑19:00. [source](https://muzeumkrakowa.pl/oddzialy/fabryka-emalia-oskara-schindlera)
- **19:00 Bagelmama** – Bagel shop, good for a relaxed dinner. (low‑budget)

### Day 3 – Wed 2027-05-19: Stare Podgórze
- **Weather:** light drizzle, 7‑19 °C – walking distance ≈ 1.8 km
- **09:30 Royal Castle (Wawel Castle)** – The royal residence on Wawel Hill, the seat of Polish kings for centuries, showcasing Renaissance and Baroque architecture and a rich collection of art and historic artifacts. Open Apr‑Oct daily 09:30‑17:00, Nov‑Mar Tue‑Sun 09:30‑17:00. [source](http://www.wawel.krakow.pl/en/)
- **12:00 Wawel Cathedral** – A millennium‑old cathedral on Wawel Hill, the coronation site of Polish monarchs, featuring a blend of Gothic, Renaissance and Baroque styles and the famous Sigismund Chapel. Open Mon‑Sat 09:00‑16:00, Sun 12:30‑16:00; ticket €5.68. [source](https://www.katedra-wawelska.pl/en/)
- **13:45 Kuchnia u Doroty** – Mid‑budget Polish kitchen; try pierogi with fresh fillings. (mid‑budget)
- **15:00 Cloth Hall (Sukiennice)** – A historic 14th‑century trading hall rebuilt in the Renaissance style, now housing souvenir shops on the ground floor and the 19th‑century Polish Art Gallery on the upper floor. Gallery open Tue‑Fri 09:00‑17:00, Sat 10:00‑18:00, Sun 10:00‑16:00. [source](https://mnk.pl/oddzial/galeria-sztuki-polskiej)
- **18:55 Depart from Krakow** – Flight with Finnair (see Getting there and back).

## Food to try
- **Barszcz czerwony (borscht)** – A spicy and slightly sour beet soup, served hot, often poured over dumplings or with a fried pâté roll. – [source](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Poland#Eat)
- **Żurek** – A sour‑rye soup served with traditional Polish sausage and a hard‑boiled egg, common in restaurants. – [source](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Poland#Eat)
- **Pierogi** – Filled dumplings often served alongside soups, with fillings such as cheese and onion, meat, or forest fruits. – [source](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Poland#Eat)
- **Bigos** – A “hunter’s stew” of various meats and vegetables on a base of pickled cabbage, thick and hearty. – [source](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Poland#Eat)
- **Kotlet schabowy** – Breaded pork chop, a classic Polish main similar to a schnitzel. – [source](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Poland#Eat)
- **Obwarzanek** – A doughy ring halfway between a bagel and a pretzel, sold everywhere for about 3 zł. –
- **Zapiekanka** – A toasted baguette half topped with mushrooms, cheese and sauces, a popular street food sold at Okrąglak in Plac Nowy. –
- **Oscypek** – Smoked sheep’s cheese pressed into spindle shapes, usually pan‑fried and served with jam, seasonal from the Tatra Mountains. –
