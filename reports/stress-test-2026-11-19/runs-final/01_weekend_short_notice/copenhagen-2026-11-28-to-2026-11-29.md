### Day 1 – Saturday 2026‑11‑28: De Gamles By
*Weather*: overcast, 2‑4 °C *Walking*: ~4 km

- **12:00 Arrive in Copenhagen** – Flight with SAS (see *Getting there and back*).
- **13:00 Riz Raz** – Low‑budget vegetarian buffet (Mediterranean). Budget tier: low. Open 11:30‑00:00. [source](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Copenhagen/Indre_By)
- **13:30 Check‑in at Sleep in Heaven** – Drop luggage and freshen up.
- **14:30 Rosenborg Castle** – Renaissance castle built 1606‑1624, former summerhouse of Christian IV. Open 10:00‑17:00 (Sat). Ticket: *ticket price: see the website*. [source](https://www.kongernessamling.dk/rosenborg/)
- **15:45 Round Tower** – 17th‑century observatory with a helical ramp to the viewing platform. Open 10:00‑18:00 (Sat). Ticket: *ticket price: see the website*. [source](https://www.rundetaarn.dk/)
- **18:00 Bøf og Ost** – Mid‑budget French‑Danish cuisine; mains from 149 kr. Open 11:30‑01:00. Budget tier: mid. [source](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Copenhagen/Indre_By)

### Day 2 – Sunday 2026‑11‑29: De Gamles By
*Weather*: drizzle, 2‑4 °C *Walking*: ~0.8 km

- **10:00 National Museum of Denmark** – Covers Danish history from the Viking Age to modern times. Open 10:00‑17:00 (Sun). Ticket: *ticket price: see the website*. [source](https://www.nationalmuseet.dk/)
