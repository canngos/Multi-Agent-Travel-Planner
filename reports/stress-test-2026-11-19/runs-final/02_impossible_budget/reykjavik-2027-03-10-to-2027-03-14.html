Food to try

  • Hákarl – National dish of Iceland consisting of fermented shark. source
  • Skyr – A yoghurt‑like cheese, low in fat and high in protein, available in flavored and unflavored varieties. source
  • Hangikjöt – Smoked lamb, a staple of Icelandic cuisine. source
  • Harðfiskur – Dried fish pieces eaten as a snack with butter (also good with coleslaw). source
  • Svið – Singed sheep's head, traditionally served as a delicacy. source
  • Slátur – Consists of lifrarpylsa (sausage from sheep offal) and blóðmör (blood sausage). source
  • Rúgbrauð – Icelandic rye bread, called "thunder bread", baked underground using geothermal heat and sweeter than regular rye breads. source
