## Day by day
*Days in this plan start around 09:30 and end by 22:00, at a balanced pace (the defaults). For another rhythm, tell the concierge, e.g. "we wake up early, start at 07:00", "we stay out late" or "a more relaxed pace, please".*

### Day 1 – Wed 2027-03-10: Skuggahverfi
*Weather:* snow, 0.8‑4.1 °C – **Typical**.
*Walking:* ~1.4 km
- **10:50 Arrive in Reykjavik** – Flight with SAS (see Getting there and back).
- **12:30 Bæjarins beztu pylsur** – Try the famous Icelandic hot‑dog with crispy fries. *Budget tier:* low. [source](http://www.bbp.is)
- **14:00 Harpa Concert Hall** – Open daily 10:00‑00:00. The glass façade, inspired by basalt formations, was unveiled in 2011 and hosts concerts and conferences. *Ticket:* ticket price: see the website. [source](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Reykjav%C3%ADk)
- **15:30 The Culture House (Safnahúsið)** – Built in 1906, this central exhibition space hosts rotating cultural displays. Open 10:00‑17:00. *Ticket:* ticket price: see the website. [source](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/94560552)
- **17:00 The Icelandic Phallological Museum** – Home to the world’s largest collection of mammalian penises (300+ specimens). Open 10:00‑19:00. *Ticket:* ticket price: see the website. [source](https://www.openstreetmap.org/node/1511563888)
- **19:30 Sægreifinn** – Seafood restaurant; try the lobster soup. *Budget tier:* low. Open winter 11:30‑22:00. [source](http://www.saegreifinn.is/)

### Day 2 – Thu 2027-03-11: Skuggahverfi
*Weather:* light snow, -2.4‑1.9 °C – **Typical**.
*Walking:* ~1.2 km
- **10:00 Reykjavik Art Museum – Hafnarhús** – Located by the old harbour, it showcases rotating exhibitions of Icelandic artists such as Erró. Open Thu 10:00‑22:00. *Ticket:* ticket price: see the website. [source](https://www.openstreetmap.org/node/688998258)
- **12:30 The Icelandic Bar** – Sample traditional lamb shank and skyr dessert. *Budget tier:* mid. [source](http://www.icelandicbar.is)
- **14:30 Reykjavík 871±2 (The Settlement Exhibition)** – Explores the founding of Reykjavik around 870 AD, built around archaeological remains of the first houses. Open 10:00‑17:00. *Ticket:* ticket price: see the website. [source](https://www.openstreetmap.org/node/1264724303)
- **15:45 National Gallery of Iceland** – Presents 20th‑ and 21st‑century Icelandic art; founded in 1884 in Copenhagen before moving to Reykjavik. Open 10:00‑17:00. *Ticket:* ticket price: see the website. [source](https://www.openstreetmap.org/node/688998282)
- **19:30 Caruso** – Mid‑range Italian dishes; good for a relaxed dinner. *Budget tier:* mid. Open Mon‑Thu 11:30‑22:00. [source](http://www.caruso.is)

### Day 3 – Fri 2027-03-12: Skuggahverfi
*Weather:* snow, -3.0‑0.2 °C – **Typical**.
*Walking:* ~3.7 km
- **10:30 Hallgrímskirkja** – Iconic modernist church completed in 1986; its tower offers panoramic city views. Open 09:00‑20:00. *Ticket:* ticket price: see the website. [source](https://www.openstreetmap.org/relation/6184378)
- **12:30 Kaffismiðja Íslands** – Cozy café serving coffee and a skyr pastry. *Budget tier:* low. Open Mon‑Fri 08:30‑17:00; Sat 10:00‑17:00. [source](http://www.kaffismidja.is)
- **13:00 Perlan** – Perched on Öskjuhlíð hill, the building houses a panoramic observation deck and interactive natural‑history exhibits about Iceland’s geology. Open 10:00‑21:00. *Ticket:* ticket price: see the website. [source](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Reykjav%C3%ADk)
- **19:30 Múlakaffi** – Low‑budget café with Icelandic home‑cooking, a good spot after the day’s sights. *Budget tier:* low. [source](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Reykjav%C3%ADk)

### Day 4 – Sat 2027-03-13: Skuggahverfi
*Weather:* snow, -0.4‑2.0 °C – **Typical**.
*Walking:* ~1.0 km
- **10:00 National Museum of Iceland** – Founded 1863, it chronicles Icelandic history from settlement to modern times. Open 10:00‑17:00. *Ticket:* ticket price: see the website. [source](https://www.openstreetmap.org/relation/6180675)
- **12:00 Kolaportið** – Weekend market offering affordable local snacks, Icelandic chocolate, licorice and free hakarl samples. Open Sat‑Sun 11:00‑17:00. *Budget tier:* low. [source](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Reykjav%C3%ADk)
- **18:00 Bæjarins beztu pylsur** – Cheap hot‑dog stand for a low‑budget dinner. Open 24/7. *Budget tier:* low. [source](http://www.bbp.is)

## Food to try
- **Hákarl** – National dish of Iceland consisting of fermented shark. [source](https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Q1703796)
- **Skyr** – A yoghurt‑like cheese, low in fat and high in protein, available in flavored and unflavored varieties. [source](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Iceland#Eat)
- **Hangikjöt** – Smoked lamb, a staple of Icelandic cuisine. [source](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Iceland#Eat)
- **Harðfiskur** – Dried fish pieces eaten as a snack with butter (also good with coleslaw). [source](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Iceland#Eat)
- **Svið** – Singed sheep's head, traditionally served as a delicacy. [source](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Iceland#Eat)
- **Slátur** – Consists of lifrarpylsa (sausage from sheep offal) and blóðmör (blood sausage). [source](https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Q335626)
- **Rúgbrauð** – Icelandic rye bread, called "thunder bread", baked underground using geothermal heat and sweeter than regular rye breads. [source](https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Q1344219)
