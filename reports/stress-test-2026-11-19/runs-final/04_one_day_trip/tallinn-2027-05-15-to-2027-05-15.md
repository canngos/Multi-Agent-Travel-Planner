### Day 1 – Sat 2027‑05‑15: Tallinn Old Town
**Weather:** overcast, 9‑18 °C • **Walking:** ~0.9 km

- **12:45 Arrive in Tallinn** – Ferry with Tallink Grupp AS. [source](https://en.tallink.com/book-a-cruise?voyageType=SHUTTLE)
- **14:30 Loca** – Quick, cheap lunch – pizza, falafel or soup. *Budget tier:* low. Opens 11:00–07:00. [source](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Tallinn)
- **15:30 Toompea Castle** – Medieval fortress that now houses the Estonian Parliament (Riigikogu). Open 10:00‑16:00. *Ticket:* "ticket price: see the website". [source](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Tallinn)
- **16:15 Alexander Nevsky Cathedral** – Russian‑Orthodox basilica built 1894‑1900, famous for its onion domes. Open 08:00‑19:00. *Ticket:* free entry. [source](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Tallinn)
- **16:45 Kiek in de Kök** – 15th‑century artillery tower now a museum of medieval defenses. Open 11:00‑18:00 (May‑Sep & Oct‑Apr). *Ticket:* "ticket price: see the website". [source](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Tallinn)
- **19:14 Depart from Tallinn** – Ferry with Tallink Grupp AS back to Helsinki. [source](https://en.tallink.com/book-a-cruise?voyageType=SHUTTLE)

## Food to try
- **mulgikapsad** – cabbage‑based dish in Estonian cuisine – [source](https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Q16411925)
- **kiluvõileib** – Estonian open‑faced sprat sandwich – [source](https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Q12366986)
- **kama** – traditional Estonian, Finnish and Slavic finely milled flour mixture – [source](https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Q134213)
- **egg butter** – mixture of butter and chopped hard boiled eggs, eaten in Finland and Estonia – [source](https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Q3351904)
