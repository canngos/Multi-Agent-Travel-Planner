Day 1 – Sat 2027‑05‑15: Tallinn Old Town

Weather: overcast, 9‑18 °C | Walking: ~1.1 km

  • 13:25 Arrive in Tallinn – ferry with Viking Line (see Getting there and back).
  • 15:00 Toompea Castle – medieval fortress on Toompea hill, originally built by the Danes (13th‑15th c.) and now the seat of the Estonian Parliament. Open 10:00–16:00. Ticket: see the website. source
  • 15:45 Alexander Nevsky Cathedral – classic onion‑domed Russian Orthodox church built 1894‑1900, opposite Toompea Castle. Free entry. Open 08:00‑18:00. source
  • 16:30 Aed – nice lunch with organic Estonian dishes (mid‑budget). Open Mon‑Sat 12:00‑00:00, Sun 12:00‑18:00. Recommended to try the seasonal menu; vegetarian/vegan options are available. source
  • 19:30 Scotland Yard – relaxed English‑style pub for dinner. Open Mon‑Wed 12:00–23:00, Thu 12:00–00:00, Fri‑Sat 12:00–02:00, Sun 12:00–23:00. Good for a casual meal after the day’s sights. source
  • 23:15 Depart from Tallinn – flight with Finnair back to Helsinki (see Getting there and back).
