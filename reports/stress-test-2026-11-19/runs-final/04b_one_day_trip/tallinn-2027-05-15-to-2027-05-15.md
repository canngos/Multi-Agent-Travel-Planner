### Day 1 – Sat 2027‑05‑15: Tallinn Old Town
**Weather:** overcast, 9‑18 °C | **Walking:** ~1.1 km

- **13:25 Arrive in Tallinn** – ferry with Viking Line (see *Getting there and back*).
- **15:00 Toompea Castle** – medieval fortress on Toompea hill, originally built by the Danes (13th‑15th c.) and now the seat of the Estonian Parliament. Open 10:00–16:00. Ticket: *see the website*. [source](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Tallinn)
- **15:45 Alexander Nevsky Cathedral** – classic onion‑domed Russian Orthodox church built 1894‑1900, opposite Toompea Castle. Free entry. Open 08:00‑18:00. [source](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Tallinn)
- **16:30 Aed** – nice lunch with organic Estonian dishes (mid‑budget). Open Mon‑Sat 12:00‑00:00, Sun 12:00‑18:00. Recommended to try the seasonal menu; vegetarian/vegan options are available. [source](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Tallinn)
- **19:30 Scotland Yard** – relaxed English‑style pub for dinner. Open Mon‑Wed 12:00–23:00, Thu 12:00–00:00, Fri‑Sat 12:00–02:00, Sun 12:00–23:00. Good for a casual meal after the day’s sights. [source](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Tallinn)
- **23:15 Depart from Tallinn** – flight with Finnair back to Helsinki (see *Getting there and back*).

## Food to try
- **Mulgikapsad** – cabbage‑based stew, a traditional Estonian comfort dish. – [source](https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Q16411925)
- **kiluvõileib** – open‑faced sprat sandwich, often served with butter and boiled egg. – [source](https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Q12366986)
- **kama** – finely milled mixture of grains, traditionally mixed with milk or kefir. – [source](https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Q134213)
- **Sepik** – dense Estonian wheat bread, usually served with butter or cheese. – [source](https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Q24735647)
- **egg butter** – butter blended with chopped hard‑boiled eggs, a classic spread. – [source](https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Q3351904)
