Day 3 – Wed 2027-04-07: Tokyo Station area

  • Weather: light drizzle, 9‑18 °C, walking ≈10.4 km
  • 10:00 Mitsubishi Ichigokan Museum – Art museum in a faithful 1894 building replica. Open Tue‑Thu 10:00‑18:00, Fri & Wed 10:00‑20:00. Ticket price: see the website. source
  • 12:30 Tokyo Station Gallery – Exhibition space inside historic Tokyo Station, showing photography, design and contemporary art. Open Tue‑Thu 10:00‑18:00, Fri 10:00‑20:00, Sat‑Sun 10:00‑18:00. Ticket price: see the website.
  • 13:30 Lunch near museum – Light meal at a station café.
  • 14:30 Kaminarimon – Iconic outer gate of Sensō‑ji; original built 941, present structure 1960. Open 24/7, free entry. source
  • 18:30 Sushi Kanesaka – Two‑Michelin‑star sushi; dinner menu €20 000 per person (high tier). Reservations essential. source

Day 4 – Thu 2027-04-08: Shibuya / Harajuku

  • Weather: mainly clear, 7‑17 °C, walking ≈9.1 km
  • 10:00 Meiji Shrine – Shinto shrine dedicated to Emperor Meiji & Empress Shōken, set in a forested precinct. Open sunrise‑sunset (Apr 5 10:00‑17:50). Free entry. source
  • 11:00 Metro Chiyoda Line to Suginami – Approx 25 min ride.
  • 12:30 Lunch near Harajuku – Try a crepe stand or ramen shop.
  • 13:30 Suginami Animation Museum – Exhibits on anime history & production, free entry. Indoor. source
  • 14:30 Harajuku Takeshita‑dōri – Famous shopping street, lively atmosphere. Open 10:00‑20:00.
  • 17:00 Metro to Shinjuku – Transfer for dinner.
  • 18:30 Dinner near Shinjuku – Explore izakaya alleys for small plates (mid‑budget, no specific venue listed).

Day 5 – Fri 2027-04-09: Chiyoda / Odaiba

  • Weather: heavy drizzle, 11‑18 °C, walking ≈10.9 km
  • 10:00 National Museum of Modern Art, Tokyo (MOMAT) – Japan's first modern art museum, works from Meiji to present. Open Sa‑Th 10:00‑17:00, Fri 10:00‑20:00. Ticket price: see the website. source
  • 12:30 Lunch near museum – Museum café set menu.
  • 13:30 teamLab Borderless – Immersive digital art museum with interactive installations. Open Mo‑Su 08:30‑21:00. Ticket price: see the website. source
  • 15:30 Sensō‑ji – Tokyo's oldest Buddhist temple (founded 628), dedicated to Kannon. Requires ~30 min metro ride from Odaiba. Ticket price: see the website. source
  • 18:30 Dinner near Odaiba – Seaside izakaya with views of the Rainbow Bridge.

Day 6 – Sat 2027-04-10: Roppongi

  • Weather: rain, 15‑19 °C, rainy day
  • 10:00 National Art Center Tokyo – Large exhibition space, free entry. Open Su‑Mo, We‑Th 10:00‑18:00, Fr‑Sa 10:00‑20:00, Tue closed. source
  • 12:30 Lunch near Roppongi – Upscale café, try a bento box.
  • 13:00 Metro to Tokyo Station – Hibiya Line to Hibiya, then walk.
  • 14:30 Anime Tokyo Station (Anime Museum) – Free museum inside Tokyo Station showcasing anime history (≈500 000 items). Free entry. source
  • 18:00 Dinner near Roppongi – High‑end sushi bar or rooftop bar (high tier).
