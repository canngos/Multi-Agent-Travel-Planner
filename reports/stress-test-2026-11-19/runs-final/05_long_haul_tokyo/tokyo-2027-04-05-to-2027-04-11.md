## Day by day

*Days in this plan start around 09:30 and end by 22:00, at a balanced pace (the defaults). For another rhythm, tell the concierge, e.g. "we wake up early, start at 07:00", "we stay out late" or "a more relaxed pace, please".*

- **20:25 Depart from Helsinki** – Flight with Finnair & China Eastern (see Getting there and back).

### Day 2 – Tue 2027-04-06: Arrival
- **20:55 Arrive in Tokyo** – Flight with Finnair & China Eastern (see Getting there and back).

### Day 3 – Wed 2027-04-07: Tokyo Station area
- **Weather:** light drizzle, 9‑18 °C, walking ≈10.4 km
- **10:00 Mitsubishi Ichigokan Museum** – Art museum in a faithful 1894 building replica. Open Tue‑Thu 10:00‑18:00, Fri & Wed 10:00‑20:00. Ticket price: see the website. [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mitsubishi%20Ichigokan%20Museum%2C%20Tokyo)
- **12:30 Tokyo Station Gallery** – Exhibition space inside historic Tokyo Station, showing photography, design and contemporary art. Open Tue‑Thu 10:00‑18:00, Fri 10:00‑20:00, Sat‑Sun 10:00‑18:00. Ticket price: see the website. 
- **13:30 Lunch near museum** – Light meal at a station café.
- **14:30 Kaminarimon** – Iconic outer gate of Sensō‑ji; original built 941, present structure 1960. Open 24/7, free entry. [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kaminarimon)
- **18:30 Sushi Kanesaka** – Two‑Michelin‑star sushi; dinner menu €20 000 per person (high tier). Reservations essential. [source](http://www.sushi-kanesaka.com/)

### Day 4 – Thu 2027-04-08: Shibuya / Harajuku
- **Weather:** mainly clear, 7‑17 °C, walking ≈9.1 km
- **10:00 Meiji Shrine** – Shinto shrine dedicated to Emperor Meiji & Empress Shōken, set in a forested precinct. Open sunrise‑sunset (Apr 5 10:00‑17:50). Free entry. [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Meiji_Shrine)
- **11:00 Metro Chiyoda Line to Suginami** – Approx 25 min ride.
- **12:30 Lunch near Harajuku** – Try a crepe stand or ramen shop.
- **13:30 Suginami Animation Museum** – Exhibits on anime history & production, free entry. Indoor. [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Suginami_Animation_Museum)
- **14:30 Harajuku Takeshita‑dōri** – Famous shopping street, lively atmosphere. Open 10:00‑20:00.
- **17:00 Metro to Shinjuku** – Transfer for dinner.
- **18:30 Dinner near Shinjuku** – Explore izakaya alleys for small plates (mid‑budget, no specific venue listed).

### Day 5 – Fri 2027-04-09: Chiyoda / Odaiba
- **Weather:** heavy drizzle, 11‑18 °C, walking ≈10.9 km
- **10:00 National Museum of Modern Art, Tokyo (MOMAT)** – Japan's first modern art museum, works from Meiji to present. Open Sa‑Th 10:00‑17:00, Fri 10:00‑20:00. Ticket price: see the website. [source](https://www.momat.go.jp/)
- **12:30 Lunch near museum** – Museum café set menu.
- **13:30 teamLab Borderless** – Immersive digital art museum with interactive installations. Open Mo‑Su 08:30‑21:00. Ticket price: see the website. [source](https://www.teamlab.art/e/tokyo/)
- **15:30 Sensō‑ji** – Tokyo's oldest Buddhist temple (founded 628), dedicated to Kannon. Requires ~30 min metro ride from Odaiba. Ticket price: see the website. [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sens%C5%8D-ji)
- **18:30 Dinner near Odaiba** – Seaside izakaya with views of the Rainbow Bridge.

### Day 6 – Sat 2027-04-10: Roppongi
- **Weather:** rain, 15‑19 °C, rainy day
- **10:00 National Art Center Tokyo** – Large exhibition space, free entry. Open Su‑Mo, We‑Th 10:00‑18:00, Fr‑Sa 10:00‑20:00, Tue closed. [source](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/103425086)
- **12:30 Lunch near Roppongi** – Upscale café, try a bento box.
- **13:00 Metro to Tokyo Station** – Hibiya Line to Hibiya, then walk.
- **14:30 Anime Tokyo Station (Anime Museum)** – Free museum inside Tokyo Station showcasing anime history (≈500 000 items). Free entry. [source](https://en.japantravel.com/guide/anime-museums-in-tokyo/70797)
- **18:00 Dinner near Roppongi** – High‑end sushi bar or rooftop bar (high tier).

### Day 7 – Sun 2027-04-11: Departure
- **Weather:** overcast, 15‑24 °C
- **02:10 Depart from Tokyo** – Flight with Peach Aviation & China Southern (see Getting there and back).
