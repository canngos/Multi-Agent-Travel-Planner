Day 3 – Wed 2027-04-07: Asakusa

  • Weather: light drizzle, 9‑18 °C
  • Walk: 0.4 km
  • 09:30 Kaminarimon (Sensō‑ji Gate) – Tokyo's oldest Buddhist temple, founded in 628 and dedicated to Kannon. Open 06:00‑17:00, free entry. source
  • 10:30 Dembō‑in Temple – Quiet sub‑temple with a garden; entrance fee ¥300. Ticket: ticket price: see the website. source
  • 12:30 Lunch near Asakusa – Try a local noodle shop.
  • 14:00 Hōzōmon (Inner Gate) – Inner gate rebuilt in 1964, famous for a giant straw sandal. Open 06:00‑17:00, free entry. source
  • 15:30 Gojūnotō Pagoda – Five‑story pagoda that may contain Buddha’s ashes; rebuilt in 1973. Open 06:00‑17:00, free entry. source
  • 18:30 Sukiyabashi Jiro – Three‑Michelin‑star sushi venue; reservation required. Order the chef’s omakase for an unforgettable experience. Budget tier: high. source

Day 4 – Thu 2027-04-08: Odaiba

  • Weather: mainly clear, 7‑17 °C
  • Walk: 4.4 km
  • 09:30 teamLab Borderless – Immersive digital‑art museum where projections create ever‑changing rooms. Open 08:30‑21:00. Ticket: ticket price: see the website. source
  • 12:30 Lunch near Odaiba – Grab a quick bite at a mall food court.
  • 15:00 Mori Art Museum – Contemporary art museum in Roppongi Hills, rotating international exhibitions. Open 10:00‑22:00 (Wed‑Mon) / 10:00‑17:00 (Tue). Ticket: ticket price: see the website. source
  • 17:30 Sushi Kanesaka – Two‑Michelin‑star omakase sushi; chef selects seasonal nigiri. Budget tier: high. source
  • 21:00 Botejyu – Interactive okonomiyaki shop where you cook at the table. Mid‑budget. source

Day 5 – Fri 2027-04-09: Roppongi

  • Weather: heavy drizzle, 11‑18 °C
  • 10:30 Mitsubishi Ichigokan Museum – Reconstructed 1894 Meiji‑era office building displaying European art (Renoir, Monet). Open 10:00‑20:00 (Fri, Wed) / 10:00‑18:00 (others). Ticket: ticket price: see the website. source
  • 12:30 Lunch near Roppongi – Grab a bento from a convenience store.
  • 15:00 Tokyo Anime Center (DNP Plaza) – Hub for anime fans with exhibitions, live radio and merch fairs. Open 11:00‑20:00, free entry. source
  • 19:00 Dinner near Roppongi – Casual izakaya for drinks and small plates.

Day 6 – Sat 2027-04-10: Chiyoda

  • Weather: rain, 16‑19 °C
  • Walk: 1.6 km
  • 10:30 National Museum of Modern Art, Tokyo (MOMAT) – 20th‑century Japanese and international modern art collection. Open 10:00‑17:00 (Sat‑Thu) / 10:00‑20:00 (Fri). Ticket: ticket price: see the website. source
  • 12:30 Lunch near MOMAT – Cafe inside the museum offers light meals.
  • 15:00 Bank of Japan Currency Museum – Historical exhibit on Japanese money, ancient coins and notes. Open 09:30‑16:30 (Tue‑Sun). Ticket: ticket price: see the website. source
  • 19:00 Dinner near MOMAT – Warm ramen bowl.
