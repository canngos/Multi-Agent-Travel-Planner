## Day by day

### Day 2 – Tue 2027-04-06: Arrival
- **Weather:** light drizzle, 15‑21 °C
- **20:55 Arrive in Tokyo** – Same flight as above.
- **21:30 Dinner near hotel** – Explore a nearby izakaya for a relaxed first night.

### Day 3 – Wed 2027-04-07: Asakusa
- **Weather:** light drizzle, 9‑18 °C
- **Walk:** 0.4 km
- **09:30 Kaminarimon (Sensō‑ji Gate)** – Tokyo's oldest Buddhist temple, founded in 628 and dedicated to Kannon. Open 06:00‑17:00, free entry. [source](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Tokyo/Asakusa)
- **10:30 Dembō‑in Temple** – Quiet sub‑temple with a garden; entrance fee ¥300. Ticket: *ticket price: see the website*. [source](http://www.senso-ji.jp/guide/guide13.html)
- **12:30 Lunch near Asakusa** – Try a local noodle shop.
- **14:00 Hōzōmon (Inner Gate)** – Inner gate rebuilt in 1964, famous for a giant straw sandal. Open 06:00‑17:00, free entry. [source](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Tokyo/Asakusa)
- **15:30 Gojūnotō Pagoda** – Five‑story pagoda that may contain Buddha’s ashes; rebuilt in 1973. Open 06:00‑17:00, free entry. [source](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Tokyo/Asakusa)
- **18:30 Sukiyabashi Jiro** – Three‑Michelin‑star sushi venue; reservation required. Order the chef’s omakase for an unforgettable experience. Budget tier: **high**. [source](http://www.sushi-jiro.jp/eng-index.html)

### Day 4 – Thu 2027-04-08: Odaiba
- **Weather:** mainly clear, 7‑17 °C
- **Walk:** 4.4 km
- **09:30 teamLab Borderless** – Immersive digital‑art museum where projections create ever‑changing rooms. Open 08:30‑21:00. Ticket: *ticket price: see the website*. [source](https://www.teamlab.art/e/tokyo/)
- **12:30 Lunch near Odaiba** – Grab a quick bite at a mall food court.
- **15:00 Mori Art Museum** – Contemporary art museum in Roppongi Hills, rotating international exhibitions. Open 10:00‑22:00 (Wed‑Mon) / 10:00‑17:00 (Tue). Ticket: *ticket price: see the website*. [source](https://www.mori.art.museum/jp/index.html)
- **17:30 Sushi Kanesaka** – Two‑Michelin‑star omakase sushi; chef selects seasonal nigiri. Budget tier: **high**. [source](http://www.sushi-kanesaka.com/)
- **21:00 Botejyu** – Interactive okonomiyaki shop where you cook at the table. Mid‑budget. [source](http://www.botejyu.co.jp)

### Day 5 – Fri 2027-04-09: Roppongi
- **Weather:** heavy drizzle, 11‑18 °C
- **10:30 Mitsubishi Ichigokan Museum** – Reconstructed 1894 Meiji‑era office building displaying European art (Renoir, Monet). Open 10:00‑20:00 (Fri, Wed) / 10:00‑18:00 (others). Ticket: *ticket price: see the website*. [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mitsubishi%20Ichigokan%20Museum%2C%20Tokyo)
- **12:30 Lunch near Roppongi** – Grab a bento from a convenience store.
- **15:00 Tokyo Anime Center (DNP Plaza)** – Hub for anime fans with exhibitions, live radio and merch fairs. Open 11:00‑20:00, free entry. [source](https://us.trip.com/travel-guide/attraction/tokyo/tokyo-anime-center-in-dnp-plaza-20905486)
- **19:00 Dinner near Roppongi** – Casual izakaya for drinks and small plates.

### Day 6 – Sat 2027-04-10: Chiyoda
- **Weather:** rain, 16‑19 °C
- **Walk:** 1.6 km
- **10:30 National Museum of Modern Art, Tokyo (MOMAT)** – 20th‑century Japanese and international modern art collection. Open 10:00‑17:00 (Sat‑Thu) / 10:00‑20:00 (Fri). Ticket: *ticket price: see the website*. [source](https://www.momat.go.jp/)
- **12:30 Lunch near MOMAT** – Cafe inside the museum offers light meals.
- **15:00 Bank of Japan Currency Museum** – Historical exhibit on Japanese money, ancient coins and notes. Open 09:30‑16:30 (Tue‑Sun). Ticket: *ticket price: see the website*. [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Currency_Museum_of_the_Bank_of_Japan)
- **19:00 Dinner near MOMAT** – Warm ramen bowl.

### Day 7 – Sun 2027-04-11: Departure
- **Weather:** overcast, 16‑25 °C
- **02:10 Depart from Tokyo** – Flight with Peach Aviation & China Southern (see *Getting there and back*).

## Food to try
- **sushi** – portioned prepared vinegared rice topped or rolled with other ingredients. [source](https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Q46383)
- **sashimi** – Japanese dish of thinly sliced raw fish or meat. [source](https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Q190715)
- **ramen** – Japanese noodle soup of Chinese origin. [source](https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Q234646)
- **tempura** – battered deep‑fried fish or vegetable, of Portuguese origin. [source](https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Q328709)
- **miso** – fermented soybean paste. [source](https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Q235169)
- **tofu** – soy‑based food used as a protein source. [source](https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Q177378)
- **onigiri** – Japanese rice ball, often wrapped in seaweed. [source](https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Q13393)
