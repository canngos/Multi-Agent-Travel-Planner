Food to try

  • currywurst – A bratwurst sliced and covered in spiced ketchup dusted with curry powder, a Berlin specialty sold for around €3. source
  • döner kebab – Flatbread filled with lamb or chicken meat, vegetables and sauces, a popular Turkish‑inspired fast food found across Berlin for about €6. source
  • pretzel – German baked pastry shaped into a knot, commonly enjoyed as a snack or with beer. source
  • sauerkraut – Fermented cabbage that accompanies many German meat dishes. source
  • spaghettieis – Vanilla ice‑cream pressed through a ricer to look like spaghetti, topped with strawberry sauce and chocolate shavings. source
  • marzipan – Confection made from almond meal and sugar, a sweet treat especially popular in northern Germany. source
