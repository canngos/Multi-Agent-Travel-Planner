## Day by day
*Days in this plan start around 09:30 and end by 22:00, at a balanced pace (the defaults). For another rhythm, tell the concierge, e.g. "we wake up early, start at 07:00", "we stay out late" or "a more relaxed pace, please".*

### Day 1 – Tue 2027-04-20: Mitte
*Weather:* heavy drizzle, 4‑12 °C – **rainy** – approx. 4.5 km walk.
- **08:05 Arrive in Berlin** – Flight arrival (see Getting there and back).
- **10:00 German Historical Museum** – The Deutsches Historisches Museum presents German history from pre‑history to the present inside the 17th‑century Zeughaus. Hours: 10:00‑18:00. ticket €8. [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/German_Historical_Museum)
- **12:15 St. Hedwig's Cathedral** – Baroque cathedral built 1747‑1773, survived WWII and was restored after 1952. Hours: 10:00‑17:00 (Mon‑Sat), 13:00‑17:00 (Sun). ticket price: see the website. [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/St._Hedwig%27s_Cathedral)
- **13:30 Ishin Japanese Deli** – Sushi set and other Japanese snacks; low‑budget Japanese deli. [source](http://www.ishin.de/)
- **14:30 Neue Wache** – Central memorial built 1818, now houses the Federal Republic’s war cenotaph; a quiet indoor space for reflection. ticket price: see the website. [source](https://opentripmap.com/en/card/W24240381)
- **16:30 Berlin Wall Memorial** – Outdoor site with a preserved 60‑m section of the Wall, documentation centre (10:00‑18:00 Tue‑Sun) and viewing tower. Free entry. Hours: 08:00‑22:00. [source](https://www.walkofberlin.com/post/berlin-wall-memorial-bernauer-strasse)
- **18:30 Lucky Star** – All‑you‑can‑eat hot‑pot, mid‑budget dinner (€12.80 per person). [source](http://www.restaurant-luckystar.de)

### Day 2 – Wed 2027-04-21: Mitte
*Weather:* overcast, 2‑13 °C – **dry** – approx. 3.2 km walk.
- **09:30 East Side Gallery** – Open‑air mural stretch of the Wall, over 100 artists painted the longest remaining segment. Free entry, open 24/7. [source](https://www.ticketlens.com/en/p268072866/berlin/east-side-gallery)
- **11:00 Memorial to the Murdered Jews of Europe** – Vast field of concrete slabs commemorating six million Holocaust victims; underground information centre open 10:00‑19:00 Tue‑Sun. Free entry. [source](http://www.stiftung-denkmal.de/en)
- **12:30 Curry Wolf am Brandenburger Tor** – Classic currywurst stand near the Gate; budget-friendly. [source](https://curry-wolf.de/de/currywurst-standorte/brandenburger-tor)
- **13:30 Brandenburg Gate** – Iconic 1791 neoclassical gate, symbol of German reunification. Free entry, open 24/7. [source](http://www.visitberlin.de/de/ort/brandenburger-tor)
- **19:00 Dada Falafel** – Low‑budget falafel sandwiches; prices from €3. [source](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Berlin/Mitte)

### Day 3 – Thu 2027-04-22: Mitte
*Weather:* overcast, 1‑16 °C – **dry** – approx. 1.3 km walk.
- **09:30 DDR Museum** – Interactive exhibits on everyday life in East Germany; hands‑on objects and reconstructed interiors. Hours: 09:00‑21:00. ticket €13.5. [source](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/175149971)
- **12:00 Witty's Currywurst** – Organic currywurst with fries; a budget Berlin classic. [source](http://wittys-berlin.de/)
- **15:00 Bombay** – North‑Indian restaurant offering classic dishes; mid‑budget dinner. [source](http://www.bombay-berlin.de/)

## Food to try
- **currywurst** – A bratwurst sliced and covered in spiced ketchup dusted with curry powder, a Berlin specialty sold for around €3. [source](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Germany#Eat)
- **döner kebab** – Flatbread filled with lamb or chicken meat, vegetables and sauces, a popular Turkish‑inspired fast food found across Berlin for about €6. [source](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Germany#Eat)
- **pretzel** – German baked pastry shaped into a knot, commonly enjoyed as a snack or with beer. [source](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Germany#Eat)
- **sauerkraut** – Fermented cabbage that accompanies many German meat dishes. [source](https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Q154166)
- **spaghettieis** – Vanilla ice‑cream pressed through a ricer to look like spaghetti, topped with strawberry sauce and chocolate shavings. [source](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Germany#Eat)
- **marzipan** – Confection made from almond meal and sugar, a sweet treat especially popular in northern Germany. [source](https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Q106252)
