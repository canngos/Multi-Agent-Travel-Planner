## Day by day

*Days in this plan start around 09:30 and end by 22:00, at a balanced pace (the defaults). For another rhythm, tell the concierge, e.g. "we wake up early, start at 07:00", "we stay out late" or "a more relaxed pace, please".*

### Day 1 – Thu 2027-05-06: Innere Stadt
*Weather:* light drizzle, 15.6‑26.2 °C – not rainy. *Walking:* ~1.2 km
- **08:25 Arrive in Vienna** – Flight arrival (see *Getting there*).
- **10:00 Haus der Musik** – Interactive sound museum opened in 2000 as Austria’s first museum of sound, housed in the former palace of Archduke Charles. Hours: Mo‑Su 10:00‑22:00. ticket €19. [source](https://www.openstreetmap.org/node/10138397168)
- **12:00 Palmenhaus** – Mid‑range lunch; recommended to try a Wiener schnitzel with a side salad. [source](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Vienna/Innere_Stadt)
- **13:15 Albertina** – Founded in 1805, it holds one of the world’s largest print rooms with works by Dürer, Michelangelo, Picasso and a modern art collection. Hours: PH,Th,Sa‑Tu 10:00‑18:00; We,Fr 10:00‑21:00. ticket price: see the website. [source](https://www.openstreetmap.org/node/60438337)
- **15:00 Burggarten** – Historic public park created early 19th century on former imperial fortifications; nice for a stroller walk. Free entry. [source](https://opentripmap.com/en/card/W8063831)
- **18:30 Le Bol** – Mid‑range dinner; seasonal dish and a glass of Austrian wine are recommended. [source](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Vienna/Innere_Stadt)

### Day 2 – Fri 2027-05-07: Innere Stadt
*Weather:* drizzle, 14.0‑21.3 °C – not rainy. *Walking:* ~3.2 km
- **09:30 Volksgarten** – Morning walk in the rose garden attached to the Hofburg Palace; laid out in 1821 over former fortifications and famous for its roses. Free entry. [source](https://opentripmap.com/en/card/W8063768)
- **10:30 Natural History Museum Vienna** – Founded in 1889, it houses extensive mineral collections and the Venus of Willendorf. Hours: Th‑Mo 09:00‑18:30; We 09:00‑21:00. ticket price: see the website. [source](https://www.openstreetmap.org/relation/11187)
- **12:30 Artner am Franziskanerplatz** – High‑budget lunch with creative Austrian dishes and an extensive wine list. [source](http://www.artner.co.at/)
- **13:45 Museum of Art History (Kunsthistorisches Museum)** – Opened in 1891, showcases imperial collections of fine arts, ancient artifacts and the world’s largest Old Master paintings. Hours: Tu‑We, Fr‑Su 10:00‑18:00; Th 10:00‑21:00. ticket price: see the website. [source](https://www.openstreetmap.org/relation/20165)
- **18:30 Chattanooga** – Low‑budget dinner, quick bites suitable for a child. [source](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Vienna/Innere_Stadt)

### Day 3 – Sat 2027-05-08: Innere Stadt
*Weather:* overcast, 11.1‑21.5 °C – not rainy. *Walking:* ~1.2 km
- **10:00 Mozarthaus Vienna** – The only museum located in a residence where Mozart actually lived, illustrating his life and work. Hours: Mo‑Su 10:00‑19:00. ticket price: see the website. [source](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/169186435)
- **11:45 Stadtpark** – Large municipal park with the iconic Johann Strauss monument; great for a relaxed stroll. Free entry. [source](https://opentripmap.com/en/card/W551031461)
- **12:45 Inigo** – Mid‑range lunch; family‑friendly menu with vegetarian options. [source](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Vienna/Innere_Stadt)
- **18:30 Figlmüller** – Famous for its giant Wiener schnitzel, served with potato salad. [source](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Vienna/Innere_Stadt)

### Day 4 – Sun 2027-05-09: Leopoldstadt
*Weather:* overcast, 10.6‑21.2 °C – not rainy. *Walking:* ~2.1 km
- **09:30 Augarten** – Vienna’s oldest Baroque park (52 ha) with historic artillery collections; excellent for a stroller walk. Free entry. [source](https://opentripmap.com/en/card/W8044066)
- **11:30 Schachtelwirt** – Low‑budget lunch; simple Austrian fare suitable for a child. [source](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Vienna/Innere_Stadt)
- **15:30 Plachutta** – Dinner; try the classic Tafelspitz (boiled beef). [source](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Vienna/Innere_Stadt)
- **20:15 Depart from Vienna** – Flight departure (see *Getting there*).

## Food to try
- **Wiener schnitzel** – Breaded veal cutlet fried to a golden crisp, Austria's signature dish. [source](https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Q6497852)
- **Apfelstrudel** – Layered pastry filled with spiced apples, popular Austrian dessert. [source](https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Q59170)
- **Sachertorte** – Rich chocolate cake with a smooth apricot glaze, a Viennese classic. [source](https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Q131442)
- **Vienna sausage** – Small thin pork or beef sausage traditionally served with mustard. [source](https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Q327316)
- **goulash** – Hearty stew of meat and paprika, popular in Central Europe. [source](https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Q205762)
