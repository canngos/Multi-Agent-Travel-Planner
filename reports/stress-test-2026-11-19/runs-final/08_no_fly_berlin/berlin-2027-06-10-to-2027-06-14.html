Day 2 – Fri 2027‑06‑11: Museum Island

  • 12:54 Arrive in Berlin – Bus with FlixBus‑eu.
  • 13:20 Witty's Currywurst – Quick organic currywurst & fries. Low budget. source
  • 14:30 Altes Museum – Neoclassical museum built 1825‑1830, designed by Karl Friedrich Schinkel; houses the royal antiquities collection. Open Tue‑Fri 10:00‑17:00, Sat‑Sun 10:00‑18:00. Ticket: “ticket price: see the website”. source
  • 16:00 Neues Museum – Open‑air museum opened 1855, restored after war damage; famous for the Nefertiti bust. Open Tue‑Sun 10:00‑18:00. Ticket: “ticket price: see the website”. source
  • 17:00 Alte Nationalgalerie – Completed 1876, displays 19th‑century paintings and sculptures, part of the Museum Island UNESCO ensemble. Open Tue‑Sun 10:00‑18:00. Ticket: “ticket price: see the website”. source
  • 20:00 Die Berliner Republik – Traditional German dishes (e.g., schnitzel) in a relaxed setting. Mid budget. source

  • 10:00 Bode Museum – Open‑air museum (1904) with sculpture, Byzantine art and numismatics. Open Tue‑Fri 10:00‑17:00, Sat‑Sun 10:00‑18:00. Ticket: “ticket price: see the website”. source

Food to try

  • Currywurst – A grilled sausage covered in spiced ketchup and curry powder, a Berlin staple typically sold at street stalls for around €3. source
  • Döner kebab – Flatbread filled with seasoned lamb or chicken, salad and sauces, popular from Turkish stands throughout Berlin, usually costing about €6. source
  • Spaghettieis – A whimsical ice‑cream sundae where vanilla ice cream is pressed through a ricer to look like spaghetti, topped with strawberry sauce and white‑chocolate shavings. source
  • Pretzel – German baked pastry shaped into a knot, traditionally salty and often enjoyed as a snack or with butter. source
  • Sauerkraut – Fermented cabbage that accompanies many German meat dishes. source
