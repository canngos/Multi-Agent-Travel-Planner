### Day 2 – Fri 2027‑06‑11: Museum Island
- **12:54 Arrive in Berlin** – Bus with FlixBus‑eu.
- **13:20 Witty's Currywurst** – Quick organic currywurst & fries. *Low* budget. [source](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Berlin/Mitte)
- **14:30 Altes Museum** – Neoclassical museum built 1825‑1830, designed by Karl Friedrich Schinkel; houses the royal antiquities collection. Open Tue‑Fri 10:00‑17:00, Sat‑Sun 10:00‑18:00. *Ticket:* “ticket price: see the website”. [source](https://www.openstreetmap.org/relation/3619)
- **16:00 Neues Museum** – Open‑air museum opened 1855, restored after war damage; famous for the Nefertiti bust. Open Tue‑Sun 10:00‑18:00. *Ticket:* “ticket price: see the website”. [source](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/18578139)
- **17:00 Alte Nationalgalerie** – Completed 1876, displays 19th‑century paintings and sculptures, part of the Museum Island UNESCO ensemble. Open Tue‑Sun 10:00‑18:00. *Ticket:* “ticket price: see the website”. [source](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/313670733)
- **20:00 Die Berliner Republik** – Traditional German dishes (e.g., schnitzel) in a relaxed setting. *Mid* budget. [source](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Berlin/Mitte)

### Day 3 – Sat 2027‑06‑12: Museum Island & Kreuzberg
- **10:00 Bode Museum** – Open‑air museum (1904) with sculpture, Byzantine art and numismatics. Open Tue‑Fri 10:00‑17:00, Sat‑Sun 10:00‑18:00. *Ticket:* “ticket price: see the website”. [source](https://www.openstreetmap.org/relation/4211594)
- **13:00 Bombay** – Mid‑budget Indian restaurant, good for a curry lunch. [source](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Berlin/Mitte)
- **14:30 German Historical Museum** – Presents German history from the Middle Ages to today in a former Prussian barracks. Open 10:00‑18:00 daily. *Ticket:* ticket €8. [source](https://www.openstreetmap.org/node/3203167561)
- **17:30 Humboldt Forum** – Modern museum inside the reconstructed Berlin Palace, hosting world‑culture exhibitions. Open Mon, Wed‑Sun 10:30‑18:30 (Tue closed). *Ticket:* ticket €14. [source](https://www.openstreetmap.org/node/8413352835)
- **21:00 Kopps** – Upscale vegan restaurant re‑interpreting German dishes; mains from €17.50. *Mid* budget. [source](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Berlin/Mitte)
- **23:30 Berghain** – World‑renowned techno club in a former power plant; legendary sound system and door policy. *Ticket:* ticket €27.5. [source](https://www.technoairlines.com/blog/how-to-get-into-berghain)

### Day 4 – Sun 2027‑06‑13: Jewish Museum & Mitte
- **11:00 Jewish Museum Berlin** – Designed by Daniel Libeskind; explores two millennia of Jewish history through striking architecture. Open 10:00‑18:00 daily. *Ticket:* “ticket price: see the website”. [source](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/302942554)
- **13:15 Curry Wolf am Brandenburger Tor** – Quick currywurst stand near Brandenburg Gate. *Low* budget. [source](https://curry-wolf.de/de/currywurst-standorte/brandenburger-tor)
- **19:30 Brasserie Desbrosses** – High‑budget French brasserie offering classic cuisine in a historic setting. *High* budget. [source](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Berlin/Mitte)

## Food to try
- **Currywurst** – A grilled sausage covered in spiced ketchup and curry powder, a Berlin staple typically sold at street stalls for around €3. [source](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Berlin)
- **Döner kebab** – Flatbread filled with seasoned lamb or chicken, salad and sauces, popular from Turkish stands throughout Berlin, usually costing about €6. [source](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Berlin)
- **Spaghettieis** – A whimsical ice‑cream sundae where vanilla ice cream is pressed through a ricer to look like spaghetti, topped with strawberry sauce and white‑chocolate shavings. [source](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Berlin)
- **Pretzel** – German baked pastry shaped into a knot, traditionally salty and often enjoyed as a snack or with butter. [source](https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Q160525)
- **Sauerkraut** – Fermented cabbage that accompanies many German meat dishes. [source](https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Q154166)
