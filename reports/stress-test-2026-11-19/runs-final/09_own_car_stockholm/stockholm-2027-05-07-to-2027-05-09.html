Ticket: ticket price: see the website (source). - 12:15 Royal Armoury – Livrustkammaren, Sweden's oldest museum (founded 1628) displaying royal costumes and armor. Free entry. Mid‑budget. - 14:30 Nobel Museum – Interactive stories of Nobel laureates in the historic Stock Exchange building. Hours: Fri 11:00‑21:00, Sat‑Sun 10:00‑18:00. Ticket: ticket price: see the website (source). - 15:30 Hallwyl Museum – Preserved early‑20th‑century mansion showing aristocratic life and Swedish decorative arts. Hours: Tue/Thu‑Fri 12:00‑16:00, Wed 12:00‑19:00, Sat‑Sun 11:00‑17:00. Ticket: ticket price: see the website (source). - 19:00 Döden i grytan – Award‑winning neighbourhood Italian restaurant; try the house pasta. Mid‑budget.

Weather: overcast, 14 °C max, 4 °C min – ≈ 12 km walking. - 10:00 Swedish Centre for Architecture and Design (ArkDes) – National museum for architecture & design, opened 1998, shares a modern building with the Moderna Museet. Free entry. Hours: Tue 10:00‑22:00, Wed‑Sun 10:00‑18:00. (source). - 11:45 Vasa Museum – Houses the 17th‑century warship Vasa, salvaged in 1961. Hours: 08:30‑18:00 daily. Ticket: ticket price: see the website (source). - 16:30 Skansen – World’s first open‑air museum (founded 1891) showcasing historic Swedish architecture, crafts and native wildlife. Hours: 10:00‑18:00 daily. (source). - 17:30 Tennstopet – Famous for Surströmming; try the traditional serving. Mid‑budget (open Sun 13:00‑01:00). - 19:40 Depart from Stockholm – car ferry with Viking Line (see Getting there and back).
