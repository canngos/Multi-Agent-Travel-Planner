### Day 2 – Sat 2027‑05‑08: Gamla stan
**Weather:** overcast, 14 °C max, 3 °C min – **≈ 9 km** walking.
- **06:52 Arrive in Stockholm** – ferry arrival.
- **09:00 Peppar** – Cajun & Creole spot, good breakfast options. *Mid‑budget*.
- **10:00 Royal Palace** – Stockholm Palace, built after the 1697 fire on the site of medieval Tre Kronor. Designed by Nicodemus Tessin the Younger. Open daily 10:00‑17:00 (May‑Sep). Ticket: *ticket price: see the website* ([source](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Stockholm/Gamla_stan)).
- **12:15 Royal Armoury** – Livrustkammaren, Sweden's oldest museum (founded 1628) displaying royal costumes and armor. Free entry. Hours: May‑Jun 11:00‑17:00 etc. ([source](http://livrustkammaren.se/en/)).
- **13:45 Mäster Anders** – French/Swedish menu; try the grilled salmon. *Mid‑budget*.
- **14:30 Nobel Museum** – Interactive stories of Nobel laureates in the historic Stock Exchange building. Hours: Fri 11:00‑21:00, Sat‑Sun 10:00‑18:00. Ticket: *ticket price: see the website* ([source](http://www.nobelmuseum.se/)).
- **15:30 Hallwyl Museum** – Preserved early‑20th‑century mansion showing aristocratic life and Swedish decorative arts. Hours: Tue/Thu‑Fri 12:00‑16:00, Wed 12:00‑19:00, Sat‑Sun 11:00‑17:00. Ticket: *ticket price: see the website* ([source](https://hallwylskamuseet.se/)).
- **19:00 Döden i grytan** – Award‑winning neighbourhood Italian restaurant; try the house pasta. *Mid‑budget*.

### Day 3 – Sun 2027‑05‑09: Djurgården & Skeppsholmen
**Weather:** overcast, 14 °C max, 4 °C min – **≈ 12 km** walking.
- **10:00 Swedish Centre for Architecture and Design (ArkDes)** – National museum for architecture & design, opened 1998, shares a modern building with the Moderna Museet. Free entry. Hours: Tue 10:00‑22:00, Wed‑Sun 10:00‑18:00. ([source](https://arkdes.se/)).
- **11:45 Vasa Museum** – Houses the 17th‑century warship Vasa, salvaged in 1961. Hours: 08:30‑18:00 daily. Ticket: *ticket price: see the website* ([source](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/27288534)).
- **14:00 Restaurang Ät Gott** – Diner with traditional Swedish dishes, affordable lunch. *Low‑budget*.
- **15:15 ABBA The Museum** – Interactive pop‑music exhibits about the Swedish group ABBA. Hours: 10:00‑18:00. Ticket: *ticket price: see the website* ([source](https://abbathemuseum.com)).
- **16:30 Skansen** – World’s first open‑air museum (founded 1891) showcasing historic Swedish architecture, crafts and native wildlife. Hours: 10:00‑18:00 daily. ([source](https://www.skansen.se)).
- **17:30 Tennstopet** – Famous for Surströmming; try the traditional serving. *Mid‑budget* (open Sun 13:00‑01:00). 
- **19:40 Depart from Stockholm** – car ferry with Viking Line (see *Getting there and back*).

## Food to try
- **Surströmming** – Fermented herring with a strong connection to northern Sweden. [source](https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Q778396)
- **Gravlax** – Nordic raw salmon cured in salt, sugar, and dill. [source](https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Q252630)
- **Janssons frestelse** – Swedish potato dish. [source](https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Q1197873)
- **Lutefisk** – Traditional fish dish of some Nordic countries. [source](https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Q890197)
- **Cinnamon roll** – Sweet pastry. [source](https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Q254979)
- **Semla** – Traditional Scandinavian sweet roll associated with Shrove Tuesday. [source](https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Q1139319)
