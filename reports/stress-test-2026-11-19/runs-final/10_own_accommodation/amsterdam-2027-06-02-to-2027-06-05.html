Food to try

  • Stamppot – Hearty mash of potatoes with vegetables, often served with endive and bacon. source
  • Haring – Raw, lightly salted herring served with chopped onion, usually eaten from a bun. source
  • Stroopwafel – Two thin waffle‑like wafers with a caramel syrup filling, sold fresh at markets. source
  • Poffertjes – Small, slightly leavened pancakes served with butter and powdered sugar. source
  • Pannenkoeken – Larger Dutch pancakes, sweet or savoury with toppings such as apple, syrup, cheese or bacon. source
  • Kroket – Deep‑fried snack with a meat ragout filling, a popular Dutch fast‑food bite. source
