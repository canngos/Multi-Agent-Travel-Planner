## Day by day
*Days in this plan start around 09:30 and end by 22:00, at a balanced pace (the defaults). For another rhythm, tell the concierge, e.g. "we wake up early, start at 07:00", "we stay out late" or "a more relaxed pace, please".*

### Day 1 – Wed 2027-06-02: Museumplein area
*Weather:* rain, 14‑20 °C – **rainy**. **Walk:** ≈5.2 km.
- **09:45 Arrive in Amsterdam** – Flight arrival (see Getting there and back).
- **12:00 Rijksmuseum** – The national museum of Dutch art and history, home to masterpieces by Rembrandt and Vermeer. Open 09:00‑17:00. Ticket €26.5. [source](https://www.rijksmuseum.nl/)
- **13:15 CTaste** – “Dining in the dark” with a surprise menu. Mid‑budget. [source](http://www.ctaste.nl)
- **13:45 Van Gogh Museum** – Hosts the world’s largest collection of Van Gogh works, including *Sunflowers* and *The Bedroom*. Open 09:00‑18:00 (Sat‑Thu) / 09:00‑22:00 (Fri). Ticket price: see the website. [source](https://www.vangoghmuseum.nl/)
- **15:30 Stedelijk Museum Amsterdam** – Modern and contemporary art, featuring Kandinsky, Warhol and others. Open 10:00‑18:00. Ticket price: see the website. [source](https://www.stedelijk.nl/)
- **19:30 Long Pura** – Indonesian rijsttafel (rice table) dinner, mid‑budget. Open 18:00‑23:00. [source](http://www.restaurant-longpura.com/)

### Day 2 – Thu 2027-06-03: Jordaan & Dam Square
*Weather:* rain, 12‑18 °C – **rainy**. **Walk:** ≈3.5 km.
- **09:30 Anne Frank House** – Historic hidden annex where Anne Frank wrote her diary. Open 09:00‑22:00 (online tickets only). Ticket price: see the website. [source](https://www.annefrank.org/)
- **11:30 Royal Palace of Amsterdam** – 17th‑century city hall turned royal palace, lavish state rooms. Open 10:00‑17:00. Ticket price: see the website. [source](https://www.paleisamsterdam.nl/)
- **12:30 Frites Huys** – Classic Dutch fries with a range of sauces. Low‑budget snack. [source](https://www.openstreetmap.org/node/2385772433)
- **15:00 Eye Filmmuseum** – Cutting‑edge cinema museum on the IJ river, with film exhibitions and interactive installations. Open 10:00‑19:00. Ticket price: see the website. [source](https://www.eyefilm.nl/)
- **19:00 Kinnaree** – Thai restaurant; recommended: green curry. Mid‑budget. [source](http://www.restaurantkinnaree.nl)

### Day 3 – Fri 2027-06-04: Science district (Oosterdok)
*Weather:* light rain, 13‑18 °C – **rainy**. **Walk:** ≈6.9 km.
- **09:30 NEMO Science Museum** – Interactive science museum in a striking green ship‑shaped building. Open 09:30‑17:30. Ticket €21.5. [source](https://www.nemosciencemuseum.nl)
- **12:30 Burgermeester** – Low‑budget burger joint; try the “mini trio” of three small burgers. Open 12:00‑23:00. [source](http://www.burgermeester.eu/)
- **14:30 Canal Belt (Grachtengordel)** – UNESCO World Heritage canal network, perfect for a short bike or stroll. Free entry. [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Canals_of_Amsterdam)
- **16:30 Vondelpark** – Large city park ideal for a relaxed bike ride. Open 24/7. Free entry. [source](https://eurolandmarks.com/attractions/netherlands/amsterdam/vondelpark)
- **19:00 La Piazza** – Italian pizza place, open late. Open 11:00‑24:00. [source](https://www.lapiazza.nl/)

## Food to try
- **Stamppot** – Hearty mash of potatoes with vegetables, often served with endive and bacon. [source](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Netherlands#Eat)
- **Haring** – Raw, lightly salted herring served with chopped onion, usually eaten from a bun. [source](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Netherlands#Eat)
- **Stroopwafel** – Two thin waffle‑like wafers with a caramel syrup filling, sold fresh at markets. [source](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Netherlands#Eat)
- **Poffertjes** – Small, slightly leavened pancakes served with butter and powdered sugar. [source](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Netherlands#Eat)
- **Pannenkoeken** – Larger Dutch pancakes, sweet or savoury with toppings such as apple, syrup, cheese or bacon. [source](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Netherlands#Eat)
- **Kroket** – Deep‑fried snack with a meat ragout filling, a popular Dutch fast‑food bite. [source](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Netherlands#Eat)
