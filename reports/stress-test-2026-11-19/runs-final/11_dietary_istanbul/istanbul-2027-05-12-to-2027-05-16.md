## Day by day

*Days in this plan start around 09:30 and end by 22:00, at a balanced pace (the defaults). For another rhythm, tell the concierge, e.g. "we wake up early, start at 07:00", "we stay out late" or "a more relaxed pace, please".*

### Day 1 – Wed 2027-05-12: Sultanahmet
*Weather*: overcast, 18‑28 °C – **walk 0.6 km**
- **14:50 Arrive in Istanbul** – Flight with airBaltic.  
- **16:30 Hagia Sophia** – Originally built as a Christian basilica in 537 AD, later an Ottoman mosque and then museum; now a Grand Mosque. Open 09:00‑19:30. ticket €25. [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hagia_Sophia)
- **18:45 Mevlana Bazaar** – 19th‑century market linked to the Mevlâna Museum, showcasing Sufi heritage. Open 08:00‑22:00. Ticket info on website. [source](https://bazaarturkey.com/activities/daily-konya-mevlana-rumi-tour)
- **20:30 Dinner near Sultanahmet** – Vegan börek, pilaf and meze (plant‑based). 

### Day 2 – Thu 2027-05-13: Sultanahmet
*Weather*: light drizzle, 16‑24 °C – **walk 0.4 km**
- **09:30 Grand Bazaar** – One of the world’s largest covered markets, operating since the 15th century with over 4 000 shops. Open 09:00‑19:00. [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grand_Bazaar,_Istanbul)
- **12:00 Lunch near Sultanahmet** – Vegan döner or lentil soup from a street stall.
- **13:30 Çemberlitaş Hammam** – Historic 1584 Ottoman bath built by Sinan Pasha, featuring marble pools and tiled domes. Open 06:00‑23:30. Ticket info on website. [source](https://www.openstreetmap.org/node/663391267)
- **19:30 Dinner near Sultanahmet** – Vegan mezze platter with stuffed grape leaves and hummus.

### Day 3 – Fri 2027-05-14: Fatih
*Weather*: drizzle, 13‑18 °C – **walk 3.0 km** (rainy)
- **09:30 Chora Mosque (Kariye Museum)** – Former Byzantine church famed for exquisite mosaics; converted to mosque in the 16th century. Open 09:00‑18:00. Ticket info on website. [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Chora)
- **12:00 Lunch near Fatih** – Vegan lentil stew (mercimek çorbası) with rice.
- **13:30 Bayezid II Hamam** – Late‑15th century Ottoman bath, now a museum of Turkish bath culture, preserving original marble basins and domed chambers. Ticket info on website. [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bayezid%20II%20Hamam)
- **20:00 Dinner near Fatih** – Vegan pide with tomato and herbs.

### Day 4 – Sat 2027-05-15: Sultanahmet
*Weather*: overcast, 10‑19 °C
- **09:30 Blue Mosque (Sultan Ahmed Mosque)** – Built 1609‑1617, famous for its blue İznik tiles and six minarets; UNESCO historic site. Open 08:30‑12:15, 14:00‑16:45, 17:45‑18:30. Ticket info on website. [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blue_Mosque,_Istanbul)
- **12:00 Lunch near Sultanahmet** – Vegan köfte with bulgur salad.
- **19:30 Dinner near Sultanahmet** – Vegan baklava and herbal tea.

### Day 5 – Sun 2027-05-16: Zeyrek
*Weather*: rain, 14‑25 °C (rainy)
- **09:30 Zeyrek Çinili Hamam** – 16th‑century Ottoman bathhouse known for its historic blue‑tiled interiors. Open 08:00‑22:00 (Tue‑Sun). Ticket info on website. [source](https://zeyrekcinilihamam.com/en/)
- **12:00 Lunch near Zeyrek** – Vegan lentil and rice bowl (pilav).
- **15:35 Depart from Istanbul** – Flight with airBaltic (see Getting there and back).

## Food to try
- **Baklava** – Layered pastry from the Middle East. [source](https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Q187495)
- **Pilaf** – Rice dish. [source](https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Q485796)
- **Dolma** – Category of dishes of stuffed vegetables, fruits, seafood and flowers. [source](https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Q339193)
- **Börek** – Stuffed phyllo pastry. [source](https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Q8196212)
- **Halva** – Confections often made from nut butters or flours. [source](https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Q183599)
- **Meze** – Small dishes in the cuisine of the Near East and Balkans. [source](https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Q818674)
