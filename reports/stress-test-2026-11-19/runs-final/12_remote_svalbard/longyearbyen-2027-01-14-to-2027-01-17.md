## Day by day

*Days in this plan start around 09:30 and end by 22:00, at a balanced pace (the defaults). For another rhythm, tell the concierge, e.g. "we wake up early, start at 07:00", "we stay out late" or "a more relaxed pace, please".*

### Day 1 – Thu 2027-01-14: Sverdrupbyen
*Weather:* light snow, –5.5 °C to –0.5 °C
- **22:10 Depart from Helsinki** – Flight with SAS (see Getting there and back).

### Day 2 – Fri 2027-01-15: Elvsletta Nord
*Weather:* overcast, –8 °C to –2.6 °C · Walk 3.4 km
- **11:30 Arrive in Longyearbyen** – Flight with SAS.
- **12:15 Barentz Pub & Spiseri** – Lunch (pizza, burgers, live‑music vibe). *Mid‑budget* – [source]((https://hurtigrutensvalbard.com/no/restauranter/barentz-gastropub))
- **13:30 Svalbard Church** – Visit the world’s northernmost church, indoor coffee and gift shop. *Always open*. Ticket: *ticket price: see the website*. **[source]((https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Longyearbyen))**
- **14:30 24‑Hour Sundial** – Outdoor sundial in town centre, works all day. No ticket. **[source]((https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Longyearbyen))**
- **15:30 Arctic Husky Travellers – Husky Sledding Tour** – Guided husky‑sledding excursion (15:30‑17:30). Ticket price: see the website. **[source]((https://fullsuitcase.com/svalbard-dog-sledding-ice-caves))**
- **18:00 Gruve 1A (Amerikagruva) Mine** – Evening visit to the historic first coal mine (1906‑1958). No ticket. **[source]((https://opentripmap.com/en/card/W686576331))**
- **20:00 Stationen** – Mid‑budget dinner before aurora hunting. **[source]((https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Longyearbyen))**
- **22:30 Aurora Borealis Viewing (Longyearbyen)** – Head to a dark spot outside town for northern lights. No ticket. **[source]((https://polarforecast.com/northern-light-forecast-longyearbyen))**

### Day 3 – Sat 2027-01-16: Longyearbyen centre
*Weather:* snow, –8.1 °C to –0.7 °C · Walk 1.0 km
- **10:30 Svalbard Museum** – Indoor cultural & natural‑history exhibition, "Life in Light and Ice". Open 10:00‑17:00. Ticket: *ticket price: see the website*. **[source]((http://svalbardmuseum.no))**
- **12:30 North Pole Expedition Museum** – History of polar aviation & airships. Open 09:00‑17:00. Ticket: *ticket price: see the website*. **[source]((https://www.northpolemuseum.com/))**
- **13:30 Mix kiosk** – Quick lunch (hot dogs, burgers, sandwiches). *Low‑budget*. **[source]((https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Longyearbyen))**
- **15:00 Reindeer and Wildlife** – Spot free‑roaming reindeers, polar foxes and seabirds. No ticket. **[source]((https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Longyearbyen))**
- **19:00 Restaurant Nansen** – Formal dinner with modern Arctic cuisine. *High‑budget*. **[source]((http://www.spitsbergentravel.com/start/food/restaurant-nansen/))**

### Day 4 – Sun 2027-01-17: Longyearbyen centre
*Weather:* clear, –7 °C to –1 °C
- **09:30 University Cafeteria** – Brunch (coffee, light bites). Open 24/7. **[source]((https://www.openstreetmap.org/node/4264171350))**
- **14:40 Depart from Longyearbyen** – Flight with SAS, Ethiopian Airlines, Norwegian Air Sweden (see Getting there and back).

## Good to know
- No viable ground‑transport option between Helsinki and Longyearbyen; all travel is by air.
- Ticket prices for most attractions are not listed – check the websites before booking.
- Museums are open year‑round; verify opening hours close to travel dates.
- Polar night means continuous darkness – ideal for aurora watching.
- Before booking, check your government's official travel advice for Norway (safety, entry rules, insurance).
- Polar night: the sun does not rise on these dates, so there is only some twilight around midday. Plan outdoor sights for the brightest hours and bring lights.
