Food to try

  • Currywurst – Fast food dish of German origin consisting of pork sausage covered in spiced ketchup and curry powder. source
  • Pretzel – German baked pastry shaped into a knot. source
  • Sauerkraut – Cabbage that has been fermented by various lactic acid bacteria. source
  • Döner – Germany's most loved fast food, a flatbread filled with lamb or chicken meat and vegetables. source
  • Spaghettieis – A sundae that shapes vanilla ice cream into “noodles” and tops it with strawberry sauce and chocolate shavings. source
  • Potato salad – Dish made from boiled potatoes, often mixed with mayo or vinaigrette. source
