## Day by day
*Days in this plan start around 09:30 and end by 22:00, at a balanced pace (the defaults). For another rhythm, tell the concierge, e.g. "we wake up early, start at 07:00", "we stay out late" or "a more relaxed pace, please".*

### Day 1 – Wed 2026‑12‑30: Mitte
*Weather:* snow, max 2.3 °C / min ‑2.2 °C • Walk ≈ 1.3 km
- **08:00 Arrive in Berlin** – Flight with Finnair (see *Getting there and back*).
- **10:00 German Historical Museum** – The museum presents German and European history in the former 17th‑century Zeughaus on Unter den Linden. Open 10:00‑18:00, ticket €8. [source](https://www.openstreetmap.org/node/3203167561)
- **12:15 PalaisPopulaire** – Free contemporary‑art gallery with rotating exhibitions. Open 11:00‑18:00 (Thu 11:00‑21:00). Free entry. [source](https://www.openstreetmap.org/node/6355366085)
- **13:30 Lunch near Mitte** – Try a local Currywurst stand.
- **15:30 Brandenburg Gate** – An 18th‑century neoclassical triumphal arch that has become a symbol of German reunification and a popular spot for New‑Year’s Eve fireworks. [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brandenburg_Gate)
- **18:30 Dinner near Brandenburg Gate** – Casual meal, optional pretzel.

### Day 2 – Thu 2026‑12‑31: Mitte
*Weather:* snow, max 2.8 °C / min ‑3.7 °C (rainy) • Walk ≈ 1.8 km
- **10:00 Berlin Cathedral** – Berlin Cathedral (Berliner Dom) is the largest Protestant church in Germany, built between 1894 and 1905. Open 09:00‑18:00 (weekdays). No ticket required. [source](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/313670734)
- **12:00 DDR Museum** – Interactive look at everyday life in former East Germany. Open 09:00‑21:00, ticket €13.5. [source](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/175149971)
- **14:30 Lunch near Mitte** – Warm up with a hearty soup.
- **18:00 Dinner near Brandenburg Gate** – Early dinner before the fireworks.
- **20:00 Brandenburg Gate (New Year’s Eve fireworks)** – Join the crowd for Berlin’s famous NYE fireworks display. No ticket needed. [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brandenburg_Gate)

### Day 3 – Fri 2027‑01‑01: Mitte
*Weather:* snow, max 3.0 °C / min 1.5 °C (rainy)
- **10:00 St. Hedwig's Cathedral** – The first Catholic church built in Berlin after the Reformation, combining Neo‑Romanesque architecture with a richly decorated interior. Open 10:00‑17:00 (Mon‑Sat). No ticket. [source](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/58608090)
- **12:30 Lunch near Mitte** – Sample a spiced potato salad.
- **18:00 Dinner near Alexanderplatz** – Simple meal before the night out.
- **23:00 Berghain** – Berlin’s legendary techno club, housed in a former power plant; doors open at 23:59 Saturday night and the party runs into Monday morning. No ticket listed. [source](https://www.technoairlines.com/blog/how-to-get-into-berghain)

### Day 4 – Sat 2027‑01‑02: Mitte
*Weather:* heavy snow, max 2.5 °C / min ‑0.8 °C (rainy)
- **11:00 Lunch near Mitte** – Warm soup and a pretzel to keep you energized.
- **15:00 Early dinner near hotel** – Light fare before heading to the airport.
- **19:30 Depart from Berlin** – Flight with Finnair (see *Getting there and back*).

## Food to try
- **Currywurst** – Fast food dish of German origin consisting of pork sausage covered in spiced ketchup and curry powder. [source](https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Q176063)
- **Pretzel** – German baked pastry shaped into a knot. [source](https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Q160525)
- **Sauerkraut** – Cabbage that has been fermented by various lactic acid bacteria. [source](https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Q154166)
- **Döner** – Germany's most loved fast food, a flatbread filled with lamb or chicken meat and vegetables. [source](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Germany#Eat)
- **Spaghettieis** – A sundae that shapes vanilla ice cream into “noodles” and tops it with strawberry sauce and chocolate shavings. [source](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Germany#Eat)
- **Potato salad** – Dish made from boiled potatoes, often mixed with mayo or vinaigrette. [source](https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Q152964)
