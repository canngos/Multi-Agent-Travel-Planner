Food to try

  • Socca – A peppery chickpea flatbread baked on a cast‑iron pan and eaten hot with seasoning. source
  • Pissaladière – A Provençal onion tart topped with caramelised onions, anchovies and black olives. source
  • Barbajuan – An appetizer fritter stuffed with Swiss chard, rice, ricotta (sometimes pumpkin, meat or leeks), traditionally eaten on Monaco’s national day. source
  • Bouillabaisse – A Provençal fish stew with Mediterranean fish, shellfish, onions, tomatoes, garlic, saffron and herbs, served with garlic‑rubbed toasted bread. source
  • Fougasse (Monaco pastry) – A sweet bread or pastry topped with almonds and nuts, derived from Provençal tradition. source
