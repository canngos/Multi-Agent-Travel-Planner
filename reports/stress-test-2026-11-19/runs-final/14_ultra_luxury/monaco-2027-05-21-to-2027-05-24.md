## Day by day
*Days in this plan start around 09:30 and end by 22:00, at a balanced pace (the defaults). For another rhythm, tell the concierge, e.g. "we wake up early, start at 07:00", "we stay out late" or "a more relaxed pace, please".*

### Day 1 – Fri 2027-05-21: Monte‑Carlo (central)
Weather: partly cloudy, 15‑25 °C • Walk 1.5 km
- **09:10 Arrive in Monaco** – Flight with Finnair (see Getting there and back).
- **11:00 Prince's Palace of Monaco** – The official residence, originally a 1191 Genoese fortress, became the Grimaldi stronghold in 1297. Guided tour and panoramic port view. Hours 10:00‑18:00 (Jul‑Aug 10:00‑19:00). ticket €10. [source](https://www.palais.mc/)
- **12:30 Chez Roger** – Lunch. Try the local **Socca** and **Pissaladière**. Budget low. [source](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Monaco)
- **14:30 Monaco Top Cars Collection** – Displays vintage and Formula 1 cars in Fontvieille; showcases the princely car collection. Hours 10:00‑18:00 (Sep‑May) or 10:00‑19:00 (Jun‑Aug). Ticket info on website. [source](https://www.mtcc.mc/)
- **16:30 Musée des Timbres et des Monnaies** – Museum of stamps and coins, presenting Monaco's postal history and currency from 1640. Hours 09:30‑17:00 (Jul‑Aug 09:30‑18:00). ticket €3. [source](https://www.mtm-monaco.mc/)
- **19:30 Beefbar** – Upscale steak dinner with a fine‑wine list. Budget mid. [source](http://beefbar.com/)

### Day 2 – Sat 2027-05-22: Monte‑Carlo waterfront
Weather: clear, 17‑25 °C • Walk 0.4 km
- **11:30 Yacht Club de Monaco** – Hub for luxury yachting, founded 1953; modern clubhouse designed by Lord Foster with bars, gallery and library. Hours 11:00‑22:00. [source](https://yacht-club-monaco.mc/en/club-house/bars-restaurants)
- **12:30 Le Grill** – Rooftop lunch with panoramic Monte‑Carlo views, grilled seafood and meat. Budget high. [source](https://www.montecarlosbm.com/en/restaurant-monaco/the-grill)
- **14:30 Monte Carlo Casino** – Historic gaming and entertainment complex opened 1863, includes Opéra de Monte‑Carlo. Citizens cannot enter gaming rooms. Hours 14:00‑04:00. [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Monte_Carlo_Casino)
- **15:30 One Monte‑Carlo** – Luxury residential complex with upscale retail, epitomising Monaco’s premium accommodation market. [source](https://www.montecarlosbm.com/en/hotel-monaco/residences/one-monte-carlo)
- **20:00 Louis XV – Alain Ducasse** – Three‑Michelin‑star dinner, refined Mediterranean cuisine. Reservation required. Budget high. Hours Sat‑Sun 12:00‑13:45, Thu‑Mon 19:30‑21:45 (Tue‑Wed closed). [source](https://www.montecarlosbm.com/en/restaurant-monaco/le-louis-xv-alain-ducasse-hotel-de-paris)

### Day 3 – Sun 2027-05-23: Monaco‑Ville cultural quarter
Weather: overcast, 18‑27 °C • Walk 2.2 km
- **10:00 Oceanographic Museum of Monaco** – Founded 1910 by Prince Albert I, showcases marine science with 6,500 m² of exhibits and historic vessels. Hours 10:00‑19:00 (Apr‑Jun, Sep) or 10:00‑20:00 (Jul‑Aug). ticket €20.5. [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Musée_Océanographique)
- **13:00 Café de Paris** – Iconic café for people‑watching; drinks €6‑16. Budget mid. Hours 08:00‑02:00. [source](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Monaco)
- **14:30 Musée de la Chapelle de la Visitation** – Baroque chapel turned art museum, once housed the Barbara‑Piasecka Johnson Collection. Hours not listed. [source](https://www.monte-carlo.mc/en/tourism/musee-chapelle/)
- **19:30 L'Express** – Mid‑range dinner; local sampler plate “Farandole Monégasque”. Budget mid. [source](https://lexpress-restaurant-monaco.com/)

### Day 4 – Mon 2027-05-24: Eastern Monaco
Weather: overcast, 19‑28 °C • Walk 1.8 km
- **10:00 Japanese Garden** – Tranquil 0.7 ha garden designed by Yasuo Beppu, featuring stylised mountain, waterfall and Zen area. Hours 09:00‑18:45 (Apr‑Oct) or 09:00‑17:45 (Nov‑Mar). [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Japanese_Garden,_Monaco)
- **12:00 Nouveau Musée National – Villa Sauber** – Belle Époque villa hosting rotating modern art exhibitions; combined ticket €6 for Sauber & Villa Paloma. Hours 10:00‑18:00 during exhibitions. ticket €6. [source](https://www.nmnm.mc/)
- **13:30 Mickey’s Pizza** – Casual pizza lunch with high‑quality pies. Budget low. Hours 11:00‑14:00 & 18:00‑22:00 (Mon‑Fri). [source](https://www.mickeyspizza-monaco.com/)
- **20:55 Depart from Monaco** – Flight with Norwegian (see Getting there and back).

## Food to try
- **Socca** – A peppery chickpea flatbread baked on a cast‑iron pan and eaten hot with seasoning. [source](https://www.seemonaco.com/cuisine/guide)
- **Pissaladière** – A Provençal onion tart topped with caramelised onions, anchovies and black olives. [source](https://foodnouveau.com/pissaladiere-provencal-flatbread)
- **Barbajuan** – An appetizer fritter stuffed with Swiss chard, rice, ricotta (sometimes pumpkin, meat or leeks), traditionally eaten on Monaco’s national day. [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Barbajuan)
- **Bouillabaisse** – A Provençal fish stew with Mediterranean fish, shellfish, onions, tomatoes, garlic, saffron and herbs, served with garlic‑rubbed toasted bread. [source](https://travelfoodatlas.com/food-of-monaco-10-traditional-monegasque-dishes)
- **Fougasse (Monaco pastry)** – A sweet bread or pastry topped with almonds and nuts, derived from Provençal tradition. [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fougasse_(bread))
