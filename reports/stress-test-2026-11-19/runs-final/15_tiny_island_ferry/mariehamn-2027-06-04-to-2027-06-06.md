## Day by day
*Days in this plan start around 09:30 and end by 22:00, at a balanced pace (the defaults). For another rhythm, tell the concierge, e.g. "we wake up early, start at 07:00", "we stay out late" or "a more relaxed pace, please".*

### Day 1 – Fri 2027‑06‑04: Central Mariehamn
Weather: drizzle, 14‑18 °C – walk 2.0 km.
- **16:00 Arrive in Mariehamn** – Flight with Finnair, see Getting there and back.
- **16:45 Draken Kebab Stan** – Quick kebab lunch. Low‑budget. [info](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Mariehamn)
- **17:30 Ålandsvägen cycling route Mariehamn → Lemland** – A popular 16.1 km coastal cycle path offering gentle climbs and archipelago views. *ticket price: see the website* (no fee). [source](https://www.bikemap.net/en/l/3041732)
- **18:30 Tullarns äng nature reserve** – Protected forest and meadow, part of the EU Natura 2000 network; walk among diverse flora and an old Bomarsund cannon. *ticket price: see the website* (no fee). [source](https://visitaland.com/en/experience/sights-excursions/mariehamn/)
- **20:00 Pub Niska** – Try Åland‑style plåtbröd pizza. Mid‑budget. [Pub Niska](https://www.pubniska.com/)

### Day 2 – Sat 2027‑06‑05: Central Mariehamn
Weather: light rain, 12‑16 °C – walk 0.9 km.
- **10:30 Åland Museum (cultural history) & Åland Art Museum** – Presents Åland’s history from prehistoric times and showcases local art. Combined ticket €8. Open May‑Aug daily 10:00‑17:00. [Åland Museum](https://www.museum.ax/en)
- **12:30 Bagarstugan Café och Vin** – Order the Ålandspannkaka (€6.5) on the terrace. Low‑budget. [Bagarstugan](https://bagarstugan.ax/)
- **15:00 Mariehamn Museum (Model Town)** – Scale model of 1920s Mariehamn, free entry. Open Mo‑Su 12:00‑16:00. [Mariehamn Museum](https://www.mariehamnsmuseet.hembygd.fi/)
- **18:30 Hesburger Mariehamn** – Finnish fast‑food burgers for a casual dinner. Low‑budget. [Hesburger](https://www.hesburger.com/restaurants?tid=167)

### Day 3 – Sun 2027‑06‑06: Central Mariehamn
Weather: rain, 12‑15 °C – walk 0.9 km.
- **10:30 Café Julius** – Light lunch, try the Ålandspannkaka (€5.5). Low‑budget. [Café Julius](https://julle.ax/fi/)
- **11:30 Åland Maritime Museum** – Exhibits Åland’s seafaring heritage, flagship exhibit is the barque Pommern. Ticket €16.5. Open Jun‑Aug 10:00‑17:00, Sep‑May 11:00‑16:00. [Åland Maritime Museum](https://sjofartsmuseum.ax)
- **16:00 Depart from Mariehamn** – Flight with populAir + Finnair, see Getting there and back.
