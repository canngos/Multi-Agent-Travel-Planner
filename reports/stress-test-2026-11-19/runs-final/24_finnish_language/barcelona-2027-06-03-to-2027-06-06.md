## Day by day

*Days in this plan start around 09:30 and end by 22:00, at a balanced pace (the defaults). For another rhythm, tell the concierge, e.g. "we wake up early, start at 07:00", "we stay out late" or "a more relaxed pace, please".*

### Day 1 – Thu 2027-06-03: la Dreta de l'Eixample
*Weather*: overcast, 20‑25 °C – 2.1 km walk.
- **09:10 Arrive in Barcelona** – Flight with Norwegian Air Sweden. [source](https://kiwi.com/u/sxuuv8gt59)
- **11:30 Palau de la Música Catalana** – Marvel at the modernist concert hall’s colourful stained‑glass interior. Open 09:00‑15:30. Ticket: *price: see the website*. [source](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Barcelona/Ciutat_Vella)
- **13:00 Museu d'Història de Barcelona** – Explore Roman ruins and medieval Barcelona. Open 10:00‑19:00 (Sun 10:00‑20:00). Ticket: *price: see the website*. [source](https://www.barcelona.cat/museuhistoria/)
- **14:30 El Glop** – Classic Catalan set menu; try the seafood *paella* or *crema catalana* for dessert. Mid‑budget. [source](https://elglop.com/en/)
- **18:30 La Flauta** – Menu del día (~€10) followed by Crema Catalana. Mid‑budget. [source](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Barcelona/Eixample)

### Day 2 – Fri 2027-06-04: la Dreta de l'Eixample
*Weather*: rain, 16‑25 °C – 7.5 km walk.
- **10:00 Basilica of Santa Maria del Mar** – Gothic masterpiece, soaring interior. Open 10:00‑20:30. ticket €8. [source](https://www.santamariadelmar.barcelona/en/opening-hours-and-admission-fees)
- **10:45 Sagrada Família** – Gaudí’s iconic basilica, still under construction. Open 09:00‑18:00. ticket €26. [source](https://sagradafamilia.org/en/sagrada-familia-ticket)
- **13:00 Restaurant Macrobiòtic Zen** – Vegan lunch with fresh seasonal dishes. Mid‑budget (low). [source](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Barcelona/Eixample)
- **14:30 Palau Güell** – Gaudí’s early palace, rooftop chimneys. Apr‑Oct 10:00‑20:00, Nov‑Mar 10:00‑17:30. ticket €12. [source](https://inici.palauguell.cat/)
- **15:45 Walk along Passeig de Gràcia** – Free walk; admire modernist façades such as Casa Batlló (exterior).
- **18:30 Vinitus** – Tapas and seafood; try the house special paella. Mid‑budget. [source](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Barcelona/Eixample)

### Day 3 – Sat 2027-06-05: la Dreta de l'Eixample
*Weather*: light drizzle, 16‑22 °C – 5.0 km walk.
- **09:30 Sant Pau del Camp** – Rare Romanesque church; cloister entrance €2. ticket €2. [source](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Barcelona/Ciutat_Vella)
- **12:45 Veg World - India** – Sample a Masala Dosa, a rare find in Barcelona. Low‑budget. [source](http://www.vegworldindiabarcelona.es/)
- **20:30 Cinc Sentits** – Contemporary Catalan tasting menu; a special evening. High‑budget. [source](http://www.cincsentits.com/)

### Day 4 – Sun 2027-06-06: la Dreta de l'Eixample
*Weather*: overcast, 17‑24 °C – 2.8 km walk.
- **10:30 Picasso Museum** – Early works of Picasso before Cubism. Open 10:00‑20:00 (Sun‑Tue). ticket €13. [source](https://museupicassobcn.cat/en/node/6010)
- **12:30 Karakala** – Lebanese mezze; good for a light lunch before the flight. Mid‑budget. [source](http://www.karakala.com/)
- **19:00 Depart from Barcelona** – Flight with Vueling/Norwegian Air Sweden. [source](https://kiwi.com/u/67u7yk9tdy)
