## Day by day
*Days in this plan start around 09:30 and end by 22:00, at a balanced pace (the defaults). For another rhythm, tell the concierge, e.g. "we wake up early, start at 07:00", "we stay out late" or "a more relaxed pace, please".*

### Day 1 – Sat 2027-04-03: Quartier de l'Odéon
*Weather:* light drizzle, 3‑13 °C *Walking:* 5.5 km
- **09:40 Arrive in Paris** – Flight with Finnair (see Getting there and back).
- **11:00 Musée d'Orsay** – Major museum housed in the former Beaux‑Arts railway station, showcasing French art 1848‑1914, especially Impressionist works. Hours Tue‑Su 09:30‑18:00; Thu 09:30‑21:45. ticket €16. [source](https://thetourguy.com/travel-blog/france/paris/musee-dorsay/visiting-musee-dorsay-everything-you-need-to-know)
- **12:30 Pizza Nelly.** – Quick slice; try the croissant‑style dough for a French twist. Low budget. [source](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Paris/1st_arrondissement)
- **13:40 Musée de l'Orangerie** – Houses Monet’s Water Lilies murals and other Impressionist works. Hours Mo‑Su 09:00‑18:00; Tue closed. ticket €12.5. [source](https://www.musee-orangerie.fr/en/articles/rates-257)
- **15:20 Petit Palais** – Art Nouvelle museum built for the 1900 Exposition Universelle, now the City of Paris Museum of Fine Arts. Hours Tu‑Su 10:00‑18:00. free entry. [source](https://www.openstreetmap.org/relation/2778854)
- **17:00 Musée Rodin** – Dedicated to sculptor Auguste Rodin, opened 1919; see The Thinker in the garden. Hours Tu‑Su 10:00‑18:30. ticket price: see the website. [source](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/472159547)
- **19:30 Macéo** – Fine‑dining dinner; try the chef’s tasting menu. High budget. [source](http://maceorestaurant.com/)

### Day 2 – Sun 2027-04-04: Quartier de l'Odéon
*Weather:* drizzle, 11‑18 °C *Walking:* 2.9 km
- **09:30 Louvre Museum** – National art museum housed in the former Louvre Palace (12‑13th c.). World‑famous collection including the Mona Lisa. Hours Mo,Th,Sa‑Su 09:00‑18:00; We,Fr 09:00‑21:00. ticket price: see the website. [source](https://www.openstreetmap.org/relation/7515426)
- **12:30 L'Express Bar** – Inexpensive bar for a quick lunch. Low budget. [source](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Paris/1st_arrondissement)
- **13:30 Musée national Eugène Delacroix** – Museum in the painter’s former townhouse, presenting Romantic works. Hours Mo‑Su 09:30‑17:30; Tue closed. ticket price: see the website. [source](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/395785603)
- **15:00 Sainte‑Chapelle** – 13th‑century royal chapel renowned for its soaring stained‑glass windows. Hours Apr‑Sep 09:00‑19:00; Jan‑Mar & Oct‑Dec 09:00‑17:00. ticket €11.5. [source](https://www.openstreetmap.org/relation/3344870)
- **16:30 Walk on Île de la Cité** – Free exploration of the historic island and its narrow streets.
- **19:30 Chez Denise** – Mid‑budget bistro; try the croissant‑bread confit. Mid budget. [source](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Paris/1st_arrondissement)

### Day 3 – Mon 2027-04-05: Quartier de l'Odéon
*Weather:* drizzle, 7‑15 °C *Walking:* 4.1 km
- **10:00 Arc de Triomphe** – Monument commissioned by Napoleon in 1806, commemorating French victories. Hours Apr 02‑Jan 31 Mo‑Su 10:00‑22:30; Apr‑Sep 10:00‑23:00. ticket €12. [source](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/226413508)
- **11:30 Lunch near Arc de Triomphe** – Grab a pastry or a quick snack (croissant) from a nearby bakery.
- **19:30 La Robe et le Palais** – Cozy Basque‑style restaurant for a relaxed dinner. Mid budget. [source](http://larobeetlepalais.fr/en)

### Day 4 – Tue 2027-04-06: Quartier de l'Odéon
*Weather:* overcast, 4‑16 °C
- **09:30 Eiffel Tower** – Iconic iron lattice tower erected for the 1889 World Fair. Hours 09:30‑23:45 (Jun‑Sep 09:00‑00:45). ticket price: see the website. [source](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/5013364)
- **12:30 Lunch near Eiffel Tower** – Picnic with baguette, cheese, and a croissant from a nearby shop.
- **19:00 Depart from Paris** – Flight with Finnair (see Getting there and back).

## Food to try
- **ratatouille** – French dish – [source](https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Q183095)
- **croissant** – French pastry – [source](https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Q207832)
- **crêpe** – type of very thin pancake – [source](https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Q12200)
- **bouillabaisse** – Traditional fish stew from the Mediterranean coast of France, especially Marseille. – [source](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/France#Eat)
- **foie gras** – Rich, buttery liver pâté made from specially fattened ducks or geese. – [source](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/France#Eat)
- **escargot** – Cooked land snails, usually served in garlic‑herb butter. – [source](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/France#Eat)
- **French onion soup** – Hearty soup of caramelised onions, beef broth and melted cheese‑topped croutons. – [source](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/France#Eat)
- **frites (French fries)** – Crispy twice‑fried potatoes, a ubiquitous side across France. – [source](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/France#Eat)
