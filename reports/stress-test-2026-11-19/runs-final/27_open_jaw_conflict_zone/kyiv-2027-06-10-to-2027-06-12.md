- **16:30 National Art Museum of Ukraine** – Ukraine’s largest fine‑art collection, from icons to modern works. Open 12:00‑19:00 (Fr). Ticket: “ticket price: see the website”. – [OSM](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/62805191)  
- **22:15 Good Music Bar&Restaurant** – mid‑budget bar‑restaurant for dinner and drinks, karaoke vibes. Open 18:00‑06:00 (Sun‑Thu), 22:00‑06:00 (Fri‑Sat). – [source](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Kyiv)
