Day 1 – Thu 2027-06-03: airport

  • 22:45 Arrive in Valencia – Flight with Norwegian Air Sweden / Ryanair (see Getting there and back).

Day 2 – Fri 2027-06-04: historical centre (walk 2.2 km)

  • 10:00 Valencia Cathedral (Catedral de Santa María de València) – Gothic cathedral whose origins trace back to a Roman temple, later a Moorish mosque. Climb the Miguelete tower for city views. Ticket ticket €9. Hours: Mon‑Sat 10:00‑18:30, Sun 14:00‑18:30. source
  • 12:00 Almoina Archaeological Centre – Underground museum exposing Roman baths, Moorish courtyard and Visigothic layers uncovered in 1985. Ticket ticket €2. Hours: Tue‑Sat 09:30‑19:00. source
  • 13:30 Lunch at Tasca Ángel – Low‑budget tapas; try the grilled sardines. Budget tier: low. source
  • 15:00 Almodí (Almodí Building) – Restored 14th‑century granary hosting temporary art exhibitions; name means “bushel”. Ticket ticket €2. Hours: Mo‑Sa 09:30‑14:00, 15:00‑19:00. source
  • 16:30 Torres de Serranos – One of Valencia’s medieval gates (built 1392); climb for panoramic views. Ticket ticket €2. Hours: Mo‑Sa 10:00‑18:00, Su 10:00‑15:00. source
  • 20:30 Dinner at El Poblet Restaurante – Michelin‑starred, seasonal tasting menu (mid‑budget). Budget tier: mid. source

Day 3 – Sat 2027-06-05: City of Arts and Sciences (walk 3.6 km) – drizzle

  • 10:00 City of Arts and Sciences – Calatrava‑designed modern complex (opera house, museum, aquarium) on former Turia riverbed. Ticket ticket €46.4. Hours: Mon‑Fri 09:00‑20:00, Sat 10:00‑20:00. source
  • 12:30 Príncipe Felipe Science Museum – Whale‑shaped building with interactive science exhibits. Ticket ticket €9.4. Hours: Sat 10:00‑20:00 (summer 10:00‑21:00). source
  • 14:30 Lunch at Alquería del Pou – Mid‑budget; order the traditional Valencian paella. Budget tier: mid. source
  • 16:30 Oceanogràfic – Europe’s largest oceanarium, featuring dolphin shows and a shark tunnel. Ticket ticket €41.8. Hours: 10:00‑21:00. source
  • 19:30 Dinner at Portland Ale House – American‑style bar; enjoy a burger and a local craft beer. Budget tier: mid. source

Day 4 – Sun 2027-06-06: Silk Exchange district (walk 2.0 km) – overcast

  • 10:00 Silk Exchange (Llotja de la Seda) – Gothic UNESCO World Heritage hall (built 1482‑1533) that was the centre of Valencia’s medieval silk trade. Ticket ticket €2. Hours: Mo‑Sa 10:00‑19:00, Su 10:00‑14:00. source
  • 11:45 Museum of Ceramics and Decorative Arts (González Martí) – Rococo palace displaying historic Valencian ceramics, including pieces by Picasso. Ticket ticket €3. Hours: 10:00‑14:00, 16:00‑20:00. source
  • 13:15 Museum of Fine Arts (Museu de Belles Arts de València) – 19th‑century neoclassical building with Spanish paintings from medieval to early‑20th century (free entry). Ticket free entry. Hours: Tu‑Su 10:00‑20:00. source
  • 14:30 Snack at Bodega La Rentaora – Mid‑budget tapas bar; try a quick tapa before the flight. Budget tier: mid.
  • 18:00 Depart from Valencia – Flight with Lufthansa (see Getting there and back).
