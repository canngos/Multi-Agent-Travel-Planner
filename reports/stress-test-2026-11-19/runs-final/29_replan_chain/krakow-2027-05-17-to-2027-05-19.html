Food to try

  • borscht – Red beet soup, spicy and slightly sour, served hot and often poured over dumplings or with a fried pâté roll. source
  • pierogi – Unleavened stuffed buns, filled with cottage cheese and onion, meat, or forest fruits, commonly served alongside soup. source
  • bigos – A hearty "hunter's stew" of various meats and vegetables on a base of pickled cabbage, thick and flavorful. source
  • cabbage roll – Large cabbage leaves stuffed with a mix of grains and meats, steamed or boiled and served with white or tomato sauce. source
  • sauerkraut – Fermented cabbage, a common side accompaniment to many Polish dishes. source
