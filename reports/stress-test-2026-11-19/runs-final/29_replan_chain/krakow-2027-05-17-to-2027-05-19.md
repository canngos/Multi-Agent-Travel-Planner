## Day by day
*Days in this plan start around 09:30 and end by 22:00, at a balanced pace (the defaults). For another rhythm, tell the concierge, e.g. "we wake up early, start at 07:00", "we stay out late" or "a more relaxed pace, please".*

### Day 1 – Mon 2027-05-17: Old Town vicinity
**Weather:** heavy drizzle, 8‑11 °C (rainy) – walk 2.7 km
- **08:05 Arrive in Krakow** – Flight with Finnair (see Getting there and back).
- **09:40 Wawel Castle** – The historic royal castle on Wawel Hill, founded by King Casimir III and rebuilt in Renaissance style. Open 09:30‑17:00. Ticket: see the website. [source](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Krak%C3%B3w/Old_Town)
- **12:00 Mały Śledź** – Low‑budget bar in Kazimierz; try cheap pierogi and bigos. Budget tier: low. [source](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Krak%C3%B3w/Kazimierz)
- **13:15 Wawel Cathedral** – Principal coronation church with Gothic, Renaissance and Baroque elements; houses royal tombs and the Sigismund Bell. Open 09:00‑16:00. ticket €5.61. [source](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Krak%C3%B3w/Old_Town)
- **14:30 Archaeological Museum of Kraków** – Pre‑historic to early‑modern artefacts from the Vistula basin. Open 10:00‑16:00. ticket €2.69. [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Archaeological_Museum_of_Krak%C3%B3w)
- **16:15 Dragon's Den** – Limestone cavern beneath Wawel Hill linked to the legend of the Wawel Dragon; entry costs 9 zł. No opening hours listed. [source](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Krak%C3%B3w/Old_Town)
- **18:30 Kuchnia u Doroty** – Home‑cooked Polish dishes; warm dinner after rain. Mid‑budget. Open 10:00‑21:00. [source](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Krak%C3%B3w/Kazimierz)

### Day 2 – Tue 2027-05-18: Market Square area
**Weather:** light rain, 8‑14 °C (rainy) – walk 3.8 km
- **10:45 Barbican** – Massive 15th‑century fortified gateway, now a museum of medieval defence. Open 10:30‑18:00 (Nov‑Mar closed). [source](https://www.openstreetmap.org/relation/13562708)
- **12:00 Polakowski** – Budget Polish cafeteria; try pierogi and bigos. Budget tier: low. Open 09:00‑22:00. [source](http://www.polakowski.com.pl)
- **13:45 St. Mary's Basilica** – Brick Gothic church on the Main Market Square, famous for its twin towers and 15th‑century wooden altarpiece. Open 12:00‑18:00. ticket €2.24. [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Basilica_of_the_Assumption_of_the_Blessed_Virgin_Mary,_Krak%C3%B3w)
- **16:00 National Museum – Nineteenth Century Polish Art Gallery at the Sukiennice** – Renaissance Cloth Hall housing 19th‑century Polish paintings (Matejko, etc.). Open Tue‑Sat 10:00‑18:00. [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Museum_in_Krak%C3%B3w)
- **18:30 Hamsa** – Middle‑Eastern spot with warm atmosphere; good for dinner after rain. Mid‑budget. Open Mon‑Fri 10:00‑23:00, Sat‑Sun 09:00‑23:00. [source](http://hamsa.pl/)

### Day 3 – Wed 2027-05-19: Kazimierz & southern districts
**Weather:** light drizzle, 7‑19 °C (not rainy) – walk 2.7 km
- **09:30 Oskar Schindler's Enamel Factory** – Museum in the former factory, preserving industrial heritage and WWII rescue story. Open Tue‑Sun 09:00‑19:00. [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oskar_Schindler%27s_Enamel_Factory)
- **12:00 Judah Food Market** – Street‑food market (11 trucks) offering pierogi, zapiekanki and other snacks. Low‑budget. [source](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Krak%C3%B3w/Kazimierz)
- **13:15 Galicia Jewish Museum** – Photographic exhibitions documenting Jewish life in historic Galicia. Open 10:00‑18:00. [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Galicia_Jewish_Museum)
- **15:00 Krakus Mound** – Prehistoric tumulus traditionally attributed to the legendary founder King Krakus; offers city views. Outdoor, free entry. [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Krakus%20Mound)
- **15:25 Return to hotel / prepare for departure** – Relax before heading to the airport; need to leave by 16:25.
- **18:55 Depart from Krakow** – Flight with Finnair (see Getting there and back).

## Food to try
- **borscht** – Red beet soup, spicy and slightly sour, served hot and often poured over dumplings or with a fried pâté roll. [source](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Poland#Eat)
- **pierogi** – Unleavened stuffed buns, filled with cottage cheese and onion, meat, or forest fruits, commonly served alongside soup. [source](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Poland#Eat)
- **bigos** – A hearty "hunter's stew" of various meats and vegetables on a base of pickled cabbage, thick and flavorful. [source](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Poland#Eat)
- **cabbage roll** – Large cabbage leaves stuffed with a mix of grains and meats, steamed or boiled and served with white or tomato sauce. [source](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Poland#Eat)
- **sauerkraut** – Fermented cabbage, a common side accompaniment to many Polish dishes. [source](https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Q154166)
