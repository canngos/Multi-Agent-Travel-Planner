## Day by day
*Days in this plan start at 07:00 and end by 22:00, at a balanced pace. For another rhythm, tell the concierge, e.g. "we wake up early, start at 07:00", "we stay out late" or "a more relaxed pace, please".*

### Day 1 – Sat 2027-05-15: Old Riga
**Weather:** partly cloudy, 8‑20 °C • **Walk:** 1.2 km
- **10:00 Art Museum "Riga Bourse"** – Historic stock‑exchange building now housing varied collections. Open 10:00‑18:00 (Tu‑Th, Sa‑Su) / 10:00‑20:00 (Fr). Ticket price: see the website. [source](https://www.openstreetmap.org/relation/9248240)
- **11:45 St. Peter's Church** – Baroque spire with city views; tower lift ticket €3. Open 10:00‑18:00 (Mo‑Th, Su) / 10:00‑22:00 (Fr, Sa). [source](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Riga/Vecr%C4%ABga)
- **12:45 DOM** – Contemporary Latvian dishes; try the rye‑bread with cheese. Open 12:00‑23:00. [source](https://www.openstreetmap.org/node/3172202159)
- **13:30 The Large Guild** – 14th‑century guild hall, impressive façade. [source](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Riga/Vecr%C4%ABga)
- **15:30 Museum of the History of Riga and Navigation** – Maritime and urban history exhibits. May‑Sep 10:00‑17:00; Oct‑Apr 11:00‑17:00. [source](https://www.openstreetmap.org/node/13721805929)
- **17:30 13 krēsli** – Casual bar, cheap drinks, relaxed dinner. [source](https://www.openstreetmap.org/node/445460362)
- **21:40 Depart from Riga** – Finnair flight, see Getting there and back. [details](https://kiwi.com/u/zzpy5y4gby)

### Day 2 – Fri 2027-05-14: Old Riga
**Weather:** light drizzle, 5‑16 °C • **Walk:** 3.2 km
- **08:00 Riga Central Market** – Large market in former Zeppelin hangars, great for exploring local stalls. Free entry. Open 07:30‑18:00 (Mo‑Sa). [source](https://www.arboursabroad.com/riga-central-market)
- **10:00 Latvian National Museum of Art** – Large collection of Latvian art, including Art Nouvelle. Ticket €6. Open 10:00‑20:00 (Fr) / 10:00‑18:00 (Tu‑Th) / 10:00‑17:00 (Sa‑Su). [source](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/87971760)
- **11:30 Burger Corner** – Quick, cheap burger – ideal for a light lunch. [source](https://www.openstreetmap.org/node/6617343796)
- **15:00 Riga Art Nouveau Centre** – Exhibits on Riga's famous Art Nouvelle; ticket €7. Open 10:00‑18:00 (Tu‑Su). [source](https://www.openstreetmap.org/node/1160803251)
- **19:00 KwakInn** – Belgian‑style beer bar; relaxed drink. Open 12:00‑01:00 (Su‑Th) / 12:00‑02:00 (Fr, Sa). [source](https://www.openstreetmap.org/node/7882140663)

### Day 3 – Thu 2027-05-13: Old Riga
**Weather:** light rain, 7‑11 °C • **Walk:** 1.1 km
- **08:10 Arrive in Riga** – Flight with Finnair. [details](https://kiwi.com/u/4hgyfuup8r)
- **10:00 House of the Blackheads** – Renaissance guild house, entrance fee €7. Open 10:00‑17:00 (Su‑Th) / 10:00‑16:00 (Fr‑Sa). [source](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Riga/Vecr%C4%ABga)
- **12:30 Key to Riga** – Try the cabbage rolls or cold borscht. Open 12:00‑22:00 (Mo‑Th, Su) / 12:00‑23:00 (Fr‑Sa). [source](https://www.openstreetmap.org/node/445460372)
- **14:00 Museum of Decorative Arts and Design** – Ceramics, textiles, etc.; ticket €10. Open 10:00‑17:00 (closed Mondays). [source](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Riga/Vecr%C4%ABga)
- **16:30 Melnais ķiploks** – Inexpensive rye‑bread snacks, perfect after the museum. Open 12:00‑23:00. [source](https://www.openstreetmap.org/node/10192025381)
- **18:10 Spot Kafé** – Late‑night bar; affordable drinks before heading to the airport. Open 10:00‑03:00 (Su‑Th) / 10:00‑05:00 (Fr‑Sa). [source](https://www.openstreetmap.org/node/3092908017)
- **19:10 Travel to airport** – Leave hotel for airport to catch the 21:40 flight.

## Food to try
- **Sklandrausis** – Traditional Latvian open pastry filled with sweetened rye dough and carrot filling. [source](https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Q2087110)
- **Alexandertorte** – Rich Latvian almond and chocolate layered cake traditionally served on Latvian Independence Day. [source](https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Q4720543)
- **rupjmaize** – Hearty Latvian rye bread, a staple often eaten with butter, cheese or smoked fish. [source](https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Q7380416)
- **cold borscht** – Chilled beet soup made with kefir, often served with diced vegetables and fresh herbs. [source](https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Q70241)
- **cabbage roll** – Savory cabbage leaves stuffed with a mixture of minced meat, rice and spices, common in Latvian home cooking. [source](https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Q6499669)
