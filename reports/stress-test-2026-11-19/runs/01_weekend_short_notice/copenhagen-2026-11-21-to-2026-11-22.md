## Food to try
- **smørrebrød** – Open‑faced rye bread sandwich topped with a variety of ingredients, a staple of Danish cuisine. – [source](https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Q275621)
- **frikadeller** – Pan‑fried Danish meatballs usually made from pork or a pork‑beef mix, served hot with potatoes or gravy. – [source](https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Q251265)
- **æbleskiver** – Round Danish pancakes traditionally dusted with powdered sugar and served with jam or berries. – [source](https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Q5395567)
- **rødgrød** – Sweet red‑berry pudding made from strawberries and redcurrants, often served with cream. – [source](https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Q493749)
- **Danish** – Laminate sweet pastry, often filled with custard, fruit or almond paste. – [source](https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Q1140186)
