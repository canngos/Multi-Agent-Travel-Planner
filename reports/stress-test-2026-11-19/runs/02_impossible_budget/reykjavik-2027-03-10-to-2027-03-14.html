Food to try

  • harðfiskur – Dried fish pieces eaten as a snack with butter (also good with coleslaw). source
  • skyr – A yoghurt‑like cheese available in flavored and unflavored varieties, low in fat and high in protein. source
  • hangikjöt – Smoked lamb, a classic Icelandic meat. source
  • svið – Singed sheep's head, traditionally served as part of Þorramatur. source
  • Rúgbrauð – “Thunder bread,” a sweet rye bread baked underground using geothermal heat. source
  • hákarl – Putrefied shark cubes, a notorious delicacy served during the Þorri season. source
  • Slátur – Traditional Icelandic blood sausage and liver sausage made from sheep offal. source
