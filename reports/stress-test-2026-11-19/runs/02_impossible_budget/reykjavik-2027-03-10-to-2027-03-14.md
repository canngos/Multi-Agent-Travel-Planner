### Day 1 – Wed 2027-03-10: Reykjavik
*Weather*: snow – **walk** ≈ 1.8 km
- **09:15 Arrive in Reykjavik** – Flight with Finnair; see Getting there and back. (source: [Kiwi](https://kiwi.com/u/8guzkx4jyr))
- **10:30 Hallgrímskirkja** – Iconic Lutheran church, 74.5 m tall, completed in 1986; a national symbol. Open 09:00‑20:00. ticket price: see the website. [source](https://www.hallgrimskirkja.is)
- **13:00 Bæjarins beztu pylsur** – Classic hot‑dog stand. Recommended: try the hot‑dog with ketchup and remoulade. **Low‑budget**. [source](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Reykjav%C3%ADk)
- **14:30 Harpa** – Modern concert hall with glass façade inspired by basalt. Open daily 10:00‑00:00. ticket price: see the website. [source](https://www.harpa.is/)
- **18:30 Hamborgarabúllan** – Casual burger café, good value. **Low‑budget**. [source](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Reykjav%C3%ADk)

### Day 2 – Thu 2027-03-11: Reykjavik
*Weather*: light snow – **walk** ≈ 1.4 km
- **10:00 The Icelandic Phallological Museum** – World’s largest collection of animal penises, over 300 specimens. Open 10:00‑19:00. ticket price: see the website. [source](https://www.phallus.is/)
- **12:30 The Icelandic Bar** – Mid‑budget Icelandic cuisine; try the lamb shank and skyr dessert. **Mid‑budget**. [source](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Reykjav%C3%ADk)
- **14:00 The Culture House** – Exhibition space hosting rotating shows; historic building from 1906. Open 10:00‑17:00. ticket price: see the website. [source](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/94560552)
- **16:30 National Gallery of Iceland** – Art museum founded 1884, showcasing 20th‑ and 21st‑century Icelandic art. Open 10:00‑17:00. ticket price: see the website. [source](https://www.listasafn.is/)
- **19:00 Þrír frakkar** – Seafood restaurant; order the plokkfiskur lunch special and the skyr cake. **Mid‑budget**. [source](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Reykjav%C3%ADk)

## Food to try
- **harðfiskur** – Dried fish pieces eaten as a snack with butter (also good with coleslaw). [source](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Iceland#Eat)
- **skyr** – A yoghurt‑like cheese available in flavored and unflavored varieties, low in fat and high in protein. [source](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Iceland#Eat)
- **hangikjöt** – Smoked lamb, a classic Icelandic meat. [source](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Iceland#Eat)
- **svið** – Singed sheep's head, traditionally served as part of Þorramatur. [source](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Iceland#Eat)
- **Rúgbrauð** – “Thunder bread,” a sweet rye bread baked underground using geothermal heat. [source](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Iceland#Eat)
- **hákarl** – Putrefied shark cubes, a notorious delicacy served during the Þorri season. [source](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Iceland#Eat)
- **Slátur** – Traditional Icelandic blood sausage and liver sausage made from sheep offal. [source](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Iceland#Eat)
