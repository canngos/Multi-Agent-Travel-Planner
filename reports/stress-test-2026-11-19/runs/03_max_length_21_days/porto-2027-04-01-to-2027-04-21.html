Food to try

  • Caldo verde – The Minho speciality, caldo verde, is a soup made from kale, potatoes and spiced smoked sausage. – source
  • Bacalhau à Brás – Bacalhau, the revered salt cod, is prepared in many ways, notably as Bacalhau à Brás with shredded cod, onions and potatoes. – source
  • Sardinhas assadas – Grilled sardines, a beloved fish dish that features heavily in Portuguese cuisine, especially in summer. – source
  • Pastel de nata – Portuguese egg‑tart pastry dusted with cinnamon. – source
  • Francesinha – Hearty Portuguese sandwich stacked with cured meats, covered in melted cheese and a rich tomato‑beer sauce. – source
