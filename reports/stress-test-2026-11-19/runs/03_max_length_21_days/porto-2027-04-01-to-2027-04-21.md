## Food to try
- **Caldo verde** – The Minho speciality, caldo verde, is a soup made from kale, potatoes and spiced smoked sausage. – [source](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Portugal#Eat)
- **Bacalhau à Brás** – Bacalhau, the revered salt cod, is prepared in many ways, notably as Bacalhau à Brás with shredded cod, onions and potatoes. – [source](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Portugal#Eat)
- **Sardinhas assadas** – Grilled sardines, a beloved fish dish that features heavily in Portuguese cuisine, especially in summer. – [source](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Portugal#Eat)
- **Pastel de nata** – Portuguese egg‑tart pastry dusted with cinnamon. – [source](https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Q1421401)
- **Francesinha** – Hearty Portuguese sandwich stacked with cured meats, covered in melted cheese and a rich tomato‑beer sauce. – [source](https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Q732)
