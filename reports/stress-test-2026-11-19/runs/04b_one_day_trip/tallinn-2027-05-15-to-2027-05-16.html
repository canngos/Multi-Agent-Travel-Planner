Day 1 – Sat 2027‑05‑15: Old Town (central cluster)

Weather: overcast, 8‑18 °C • Walking: ~1.7 km

  • 12:45 Arrive in Tallinn – Ferry with Tallink Grupp AS (see “Getting there and back”).
  • 13:30 Loca – Fast‑food joint offering pizza, falafel, hummus, soup and beer; prices €3–8. Budget tier: low. source
  • 14:15 Toompea Castle – Medieval fortress founded in the 9th‑10th century, expanded by the Danes (13th‑15th c.). Today it houses the Estonian Parliament (Riigikogu). Open 10:00–16:00. Ticket: see the website. source
  • 15:30 St. Nicholas Church (Niguliste kirik) – Founded c. 1230‑1275, restored after WWII bomb damage; now hosts the “Dance of Death” exhibit. Open Wed‑Sun 10:00‑17:00. ticket €3.5. source
  • 18:30 Scotland Yard – English‑style pub with hearty grilled dishes and weekday lunch specials. Budget tier: low. Open Fri‑Sat 12:00‑02:00. source

Food to try

  • Verivorst – Estonia's national dish, a pig's blood sausage similar to black pudding, usually served with lingonberry jam. source
  • Mustamakkara – A Finnish‑style pig's blood sausage, a common winter dish also enjoyed in Estonia. source
  • Mulgikapsad – A cabbage‑based stew (sauerkraut) traditionally served with meat and potatoes. source
  • Kartulisalat – Traditional Estonian potato salad often mixed with peas, carrots and a sour‑cream dressing. source
  • Kohuke – A chocolate‑coated, flavoured curd snack widely sold in Estonian supermarkets. source
  • Kiluvõileib – Open‑faced sprat sandwich, a popular Estonian snack. source
