### Day 1 – Sat 2027‑05‑15: Old Town (central cluster)
**Weather:** overcast, 8‑18 °C • **Walking:** ~1.7 km

- **12:45 Arrive in Tallinn** – Ferry with Tallink Grupp AS (see “Getting there and back”).
- **13:30 Loca** – Fast‑food joint offering pizza, falafel, hummus, soup and beer; prices €3–8. *Budget tier:* low. [source](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Tallinn)
- **14:15 Toompea Castle** – Medieval fortress founded in the 9th‑10th century, expanded by the Danes (13th‑15th c.). Today it houses the Estonian Parliament (Riigikogu). Open 10:00–16:00. Ticket: *see the website*. [source](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Tallinn)
- **15:30 St. Nicholas Church (Niguliste kirik)** – Founded c. 1230‑1275, restored after WWII bomb damage; now hosts the “Dance of Death” exhibit. Open Wed‑Sun 10:00‑17:00. **ticket €3.5**. [source](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Tallinn)
- **18:30 Scotland Yard** – English‑style pub with hearty grilled dishes and weekday lunch specials. *Budget tier:* low. Open Fri‑Sat 12:00‑02:00. [source](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Tallinn)

## Food to try
- **Verivorst** – Estonia's national dish, a pig's blood sausage similar to black pudding, usually served with lingonberry jam. [source](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Estonia#Eat)
- **Mustamakkara** – A Finnish‑style pig's blood sausage, a common winter dish also enjoyed in Estonia. [source](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Estonia#Eat)
- **Mulgikapsad** – A cabbage‑based stew (sauerkraut) traditionally served with meat and potatoes. [source](https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Q16411925)
- **Kartulisalat** – Traditional Estonian potato salad often mixed with peas, carrots and a sour‑cream dressing. [source](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Estonia#Eat)
- **Kohuke** – A chocolate‑coated, flavoured curd snack widely sold in Estonian supermarkets. [source](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Estonia#Eat)
- **Kiluvõileib** – Open‑faced sprat sandwich, a popular Estonian snack. [source](https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Q12366986)
