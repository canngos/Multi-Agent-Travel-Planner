- **21:20 Arrive in Tokyo** – Same flight as above. – [details](https://kiwi.com/u/37krgqs6tp)

### Day 3 – Wed 2027‑04‑07: Shinjuku (4.3 km walk)
Weather: light drizzle, 9‑18 °C. 
- **10:00 Suga Shrine** – Quiet Shinto shrine featured in the film *Your Name*. Open 10:00‑17:00. Free entry. – [source](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Tokyo/Shinjuku)
- **11:30 Fire Museum (東京消防博物館)** – Three‑floor exhibit on firefighting history, interactive gear try‑on. Open 09:30‑17:00. Free entry. – [source](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Tokyo/Shinjuku)
- **12:30 Lunch at Torafugu‑Tei** – High‑budget fugu (blowfish) set. Recommended to try the torafugu sashimi. – [source](https://www.torafugu.co.jp/en/shinjuku_honten/)
- **15:00 Shinjuku Gyoen National Garden** – Large garden with Japanese, English and French landscapes; tea house and greenhouse. Open 09:00‑16:30 (extended seasonally). Ticket price: see the website. – [source](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Tokyo/Shinjuku)
- **18:30 Dinner at Nabezo** – Mid‑budget all‑you‑can‑eat shabu‑shabu. – [source](https://nabe-zo.com/en)

### Day 4 – Thu 2027‑04‑08: Asakusa (0.2 km walk)
Weather: mainly clear, 7‑17 °C. 
- **10:00 Kaminarimon (Thunder Gate)** – Iconic outer gate of Senso‑ji with giant red lantern. – [source](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Tokyo/Asakusa)
- **11:30 Dembō‑in Temple** – Sub‑temple of Senso‑ji with historic garden, famous for cherry‑blossom viewing. – [source](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Tokyo/Asakusa)
- **13:30 Lunch near Asakusa** – Explore the local street‑food stalls (e.g., tempura, takoyaki).
- **19:00 Dinner near Asakusa** – Casual dining in the Kaminarimon area.

### Day 5 – Fri 2027‑04‑09: Marunouchi (1.9 km walk)
Weather: heavy drizzle, 11‑18 °C. 
- **09:30 Meiji Seimei Kan** – Historic 1934 building symbolising Japan’s modernization; Japanese‑architect‑designed. – [source](https://opentripmap.com/en/card/R8048114)
- **12:00 Lunch near Marunouchi** – Quick bite in the business district.
- **13:00 DN Tower 21** – Historic tower that housed General MacArthur’s headquarters; designated historic building. – [source](https://opentripmap.com/en/card/Q3011415)
- **14:30 Idemitsu Museum of Arts** – Collection of Japanese and Asian art, modern and traditional pieces. – [source](https://opentripmap.com/en/card/N5403425184)
- **16:00 Dinner at Sushi Kanesaka** – Two‑Michelin‑star omakase sushi; chef selects seasonal nigiri. High‑budget experience. – [source](http://www.sushi-kanesaka.com/)

### Day 6 – Sat 2027‑04‑10: Chiyoda
Weather: rain, 16‑19 °C (rainy). 
- **10:30 National Museum of Modern Art, Tokyo (MOMAT)** – Japan’s first national museum of modern art, works from Meiji to present. Open Sa‑Th 10:00‑17:00; Fr 10:00‑20:00. Ticket price: see the website. – [source](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/43934324)
- **12:30 Lunch near Chiyoda** – Simple meal close to the museum.
- **19:00 Dinner near Chiyoda** – Relaxed dinner nearby.

### Day 7 – Sun 2027‑04‑11: Shinjuku
Weather: overcast, 16‑25 °C. 
- **02:00 Depart from Tokyo** – Flight with Korean Air, China Southern Airlines. – [details](https://kiwi.com/u/wdn7m8qmzg)

## Food to try
- **sushi** – Portioned prepared vinegared rice topped or rolled with other ingredients. – [source](https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Q46383)
- **ramen** – Japanese noodle soup of Chinese origin. – [source](https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Q234646)
- **tempura** – Battered deep‑fried fish or vegetable. – [source](https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Q328709)
- **okonomiyaki** – Savory Japanese pancake cooked at your table. – [source](https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Q701075)
- **takoyaki** – Ball‑shaped snack with octopus filling. – [source](https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Q905527)
- **miso soup** – Soup flavored with miso paste. – [source](https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Q471866)
