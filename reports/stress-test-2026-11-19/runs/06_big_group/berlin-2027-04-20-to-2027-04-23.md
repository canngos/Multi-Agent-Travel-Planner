### Day 3 – Thu 2027‑04‑22: Mitte
- **Weather:** overcast, 1‑16 °C (no rain)
- **Walking:** 2.9 km
- **09:30 Brandenburg Gate** – Iconic 18th‑century neoclassical monument (built 1788‑1791) that now symbolises German unity. Open 24/7. *ticket price: see the website* [Source](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/518071791)
- **10:30 Reichstag dome** – Glass dome atop the historic Reichstag building; offers panoramic city views and represents reunification. Hours 08:00‑21:45. *ticket price: see the website* [Source](https://www.openstreetmap.org/node/2432753840)
- **13:00 Witty's Currywurst** – Classic Berlin currywurst and fries. Budget tier: low. Order the currywurst with fries. [Source](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Berlin/Mitte)
- **14:30 German Historical Museum** – Shows German history from the Middle Ages to today, housed in the 17th‑century Zeughaus. Hours 10:00‑18:00. Ticket €8 per person. [Source](https://www.openstreetmap.org/node/3203167561)
- **17:00 Neues Museum** – UNESCO World Heritage site on Museum Island, home to Egyptian artifacts and the Papyrus Collection. Hours Tue‑Sun 10:00‑18:00. *ticket price: see the website* [Source](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/18578139)
- **19:30 Ständige Vertretung** – Mid‑budget Rhenish specialties; try the Flammekuchen. Open daily 10:30‑01:00. [Source](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Berlin/Mitte)
