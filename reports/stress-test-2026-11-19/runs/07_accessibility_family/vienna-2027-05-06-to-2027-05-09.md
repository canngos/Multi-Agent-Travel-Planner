## Day by day

*Days in this plan start around 09:30 and end by 22:00, at a balanced pace (the defaults). For another rhythm, tell the concierge, e.g. "we wake up early, start at 07:00", "we stay out late" or "a more relaxed pace, please".*

### Day 1 – Thu 2027‑05‑06: 
**Weather:** light drizzle, 15‑26 °C (typical). No walking distance listed.
- **14:28 Depart from Helsinki** – Bus with FlixBus‑eu. See *Getting there and back*.

### Day 2 – Fri 2027‑05‑07: 
**Weather:** drizzle, 14‑21 °C. 
- **21:44 Arrive in Vienna** – Bus with FlixBus‑eu.
- **22:30 Stadtpark** – Evening stroll in a 65 000 m² municipal park famous for its gold‑plated Johann Strauss II monument and open‑air concerts. *Free entry.* [source](https://opentripmap.com/en/card/W551031461)

### Day 3 – Sat 2027‑05‑08 (Innere Stadt) – Walk 1.4 km
**Weather:** overcast, 11‑21 °C.
- **09:30 Burggarten** – Historic park created in the early 19th century on former fortifications; includes the Butterfly House greenhouse. *Free entry.* [source](https://opentripmap.com/en/card/W8063831)
- **10:15 Albertina** – Museum housing one of the world’s finest graphic art collections (Dürer, Michelangelo, Picasso). Open Thu‑Tue 10:00‑18:00, Wed 10:00‑21:00. Ticket **€11.9**. [source](http://www.albertina.at/)
- **12:30 Lunch at Palmenhaus** – Mid‑range restaurant in a glasshouse; try the Wiener Schnitzel. (mid budget) [source](http://www.palmenhaus.at/)
- **13:45 Imperial Treasury (Schatzkammer)** – Displays the Habsburg crown jewels and regalia in 20 lavish rooms of the Hofburg Palace. Open Mon, Wed‑Sun 09:00‑17:30. Ticket **€6**. [source](http://www.kaiserliche-schatzkammer.at/)
- **15:30 Volksgarten** – Rose garden belonging to the Hofburg complex, laid out in 1821 over former city walls. *Free entry.* [source](https://opentripmap.com/en/card/W8063768)
- **19:00 Dinner at Zum Leupold** – Seasonal local dishes in a relaxed setting. (mid budget) [source](http://www.leupold.at/)

### Day 4 – Sun 2027‑05‑09 (Innere Stadt) – Walk 4.7 km
**Weather:** overcast, 10‑21 °C.
- **09:30 St. Stephen's Cathedral** – Iconic Gothic cathedral, mother church of the Archdiocese of Vienna, cornerstone laid in the 12th century. Open Mo‑Sa 06:00‑22:00; Su 07:00‑22:00. Ticket info: *see the website.* [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/St._Stephen%27s_Cathedral,_Vienna)
- **11:15 House of Music** – Interactive museum of sound and music, covering instruments from pre‑history to modern electronic. Open daily 10:00‑22:00. Ticket info: *see the website.* [source](https://www.hausdermusik.com/)
- **13:30 Lunch at Figlmüller** – Famous for its oversized Wiener Schnitzel; classic Viennese meat experience. (mid budget) [source](http://www.figlmueller.at/en/figlmueller-wollzeile-en.html)
- **14:45 Mozarthaus Vienna** – Mozart’s only surviving Viennese residence (1784‑87), now a museum of his life and work. Open daily 10:00‑19:00. Ticket info: *see the website.* [source](https://www.mozarthausvienna.at/)
- **17:00 Augarten** – Vienna’s oldest Baroque garden (52 ha) with historic artillery pieces and a porcelain museum. *Free entry.* [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Augarten)
- **18:00 Dinner at Zum Leupold** – Repeat of earlier dinner; seasonal dishes. (mid budget) [source](http://www.leupold.at/)
- **23:07 Depart from Vienna** – Bus with FlixBus‑eu. See *Getting there and back*.

## Food to try
- **Wiener Schnitzel** – Thin, breaded and fried veal cutlet, often served with lemon and potatoes or salad. source
- **Tafelspitz** – Boiled beef served with potatoes, horseradish, clear broth and dumplings. source
- **Apfelstrudel** – Layered pastry filled with spiced apples, raisins and crumbly topping. source
- **Kaiserschmarren** – Fluffy shredded pancake served with plum compote and powdered sugar. source
- **Knödel** – Savory or sweet dumplings (bread, potatoes or semolina) served with meat or as dessert. source
