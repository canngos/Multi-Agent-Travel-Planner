## Day by day

*Days in this plan start around 09:30 and end by 22:00, at a balanced pace (the defaults). For another rhythm, tell the concierge, e.g. "we wake up early, start at 07:00", "we stay out late" or "a more relaxed pace, please".*

### Day 1 – Thu 2027‑06‑10: Travel
- **10:06** – Depart from Helsinki by bus (UAB Kautra). See *Getting there and back*.

### Day 2 – Fri 2027‑06‑11: Berlin (≈8 km walk)
- **13:38** – Arrive in Berlin (bus). 
- **13:45** – **Neues Museum** – Egyptian collection and the bust of Nefertiti. Open Tue‑Sun 10:00‑18:00. Ticket: *see the website*. [source](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/18578139)
- **15:30** – **Curry 36** – Classic currywurst, low‑budget. Budget tier: low. [source](http://www.curry36.de)
- **17:30** – **Pergamon Museum** – Currently closed for renovation (off). Historically housed the Pergamon Altar. [source](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/313659704)
- **19:30** – **Asador Steakhouse** – Mid‑range steak and Spanish dishes. Budget tier: mid. [source](http://www.restaurant-asador.de/)

### Day 3 – Sat 2027‑06‑12: Berlin (≈8.5 km walk) – rainy
- **10:30** – **Bode Museum** – Sculptures, Byzantine art and coin collection. Open Sat‑Sun 10:00‑18:00. [source](https://www.openstreetmap.org/relation/4211594)
- **12:30** – **Altes Museum** – Classical antiquities on Museum Island. Open Sat‑Sun 10:00‑18:00. [source](https://www.openstreetmap.org/relation/3619)
- **14:30** – **Hasir** – Turkish kebab, 24 h. Budget tier: mid. [source](http://www.hasir.de)
- **15:45** – **DDR Museum** – Interactive exhibits on everyday life in former East Germany. Open daily 09:00‑21:00. Ticket €13.5. [source](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/175149971)
- **20:30** – **Café Sarotti‑Höfe** – German dishes in former chocolate factory, mid‑budget. Budget tier: mid. [source](http://cafe-sarottihoefe.de/)
- **23:59** – **Berghain / Panorama Bar** – World‑renowned techno club; doors open at 23:59 Saturday. Ticket €27.5. [source](https://www.technoairlines.com/blog/how-to-get-into-berghain)

### Day 4 – Sun 2027‑06‑13: Berlin (≈9 km walk)
- **10:30** – **Humboldt Forum** – Cultural exhibitions in rebuilt Berlin Palace. Open Mo,We‑Su 10:30‑18:30. Ticket €14.0. [source](https://www.humboldtforum.org)
- **12:30** – **Mustafas Gemüse Kebap** – Best‑rated döner kebab. Budget tier: low. [source](http://mustafas.de/)
- **13:30** – **German Historical Museum** – German history from Middle Ages to present. Open daily 10:00‑18:00. Ticket €8.0. [source](https://www.openstreetmap.org/node/3203167561)
- **20:00** – **Burgermeister** – Late‑night burgers, open to 03:00. Budget tier: low. [source](http://burger-meister.de/)

### Day 5 – Mon 2027‑06‑14: Travel
- **08:06** – Depart from Berlin by bus (FlixBus‑eu). See *Getting there and back*.
