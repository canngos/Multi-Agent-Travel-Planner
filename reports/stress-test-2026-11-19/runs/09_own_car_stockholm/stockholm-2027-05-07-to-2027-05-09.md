## Day by day

*Days in this plan start around 09:30 and end by 22:00, at a balanced pace (the defaults). For another rhythm, tell the concierge, e.g. "we wake up early, start at 07:00", "we stay out late" or "a more relaxed pace, please".*

### Day 1 – Sat 2027‑05‑07: Central Stockholm
*Weather:* overcast, 14‑15 °C – no rain
- **20:36 Depart from Turku** – drive to Turku harbour and board the Viking Line car ferry (see Getting there).

### Day 2 – Sun 2027‑05‑08: Djurgården
*Weather:* overcast, 14‑15 °C – no rain
*Walking:* approx. 8.5 km
- **06:55 Arrive in Stockholm** – disembark with your car.
- **10:00 Skansen** – the world’s oldest open‑air museum, featuring historic Swedish buildings and a zoo. Open 10:00‑18:00. Ticket : price – see the website. [source](https://www.skansen.se)
- **12:30 Café at the Swedish Centre for Architecture and Design** – light lunch on‑site. Open Tue 10:00‑22:00; Wed‑Sun 10:00‑18:00. [source](https://www.openstreetmap.org/node/1092471973)
- **14:00 Vasa Museum** – displays the almost fully intact 17th‑century warship Vasa, salvaged after sinking on her maiden voyage in 1628. Open 08:30‑18:00. ticket €19.5. [source](https://www.vasamuseet.se/en/visit/buy-ticket)
- **16:00 Maritime Museum** – chronicles Sweden’s naval history and maritime heritage on Djurgården’s waterfront. Open Tue, Thu‑Sun 10:00‑17:00; Wed 10:00‑20:00. Ticket : price – see the website. [source](https://www.sjohistoriska.se/)
- **18:00 Fotografiska** – contemporary photography museum presenting international works. Open 10:00‑23:00. Ticket : price – see the website. [source](https://www.fotografiska.com/sto/)
- **20:00 Mälarpaviljongen** – self‑service waterfront café, affordable Swedish snacks. [source](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Stockholm/Kungsholmen)

### Day 3 – Mon 2027‑05‑09: Lidingö
*Weather:* overcast, 14‑15 °C – no rain
*Walking:* approx. 2.1 km
- **13:30 Kungsholmen (restaurant)** – mid‑budget restaurant on Kungsholmen, generous portions, mains €175‑260 kr. Ideal for a relaxed lunch. [source](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Stockholm/Kungsholmen)
- **15:00 Swedish Museum of Performing Arts** – museum dedicated to theatre, dance and performance, showcasing Swedish cultural history. Open 11:00‑17:00. Ticket : price – see the website. [source](https://scenkonstmuseet.se/)
- **19:27 Depart from Stockholm** – board the Viking Line car ferry for the return to Turku (see Getting there and back).

## Food to try
- **gravlax** – Nordic dish of raw salmon cured in salt, sugar and dill. [source](https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Q252630)
- **surströmming** – fermented herring with a strong connection to northern Sweden. [source](https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Q778396)
- **cinnamon roll** – sweet pastry. [source](https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Q254979)
- **semla** – traditional Scandinavian sweet roll associated with Shrove Tuesday. [source](https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Q1139319)
- **crispbread** – flat, dry type of bread or cracker. [source](https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Q251981)
- **Janssons frestelse** – Swedish potato casserole. [source](https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Q1197873)
