Day 1 – Wed 2027-06-02: Museum Quarter

Weather: rain, 14‑20 °C • Walking: – km

  • 20:50 Arrive in Amsterdam – Flight with Finnair, Norwegian Air Sweden. See Getting there and back.
  • 21:30 De Blauwe Hollander – Dutch home‑cooked comfort food (herrings, pea soup, hutspot). Mid‑budget, price around €20. http://www.deblauwehollander.nl

Day 2 – Thu 2027-06-03: Canal Ring

Weather: rain, 12‑18 °C • Walking: 2.7 km

  • 10:00 Foam Fotografiemuseum Amsterdam – A photography museum on Keizersgracht offering changing exhibitions of documentary, art and fashion photography. Hours: Th‑Fr 10:00‑21:00. Ticket: ticket price: see the website. https://www.openstreetmap.org/node/250624672
  • 12:30 Magere Brug – The iconic skinny wooden drawbridge over the Amstel, first built in 1691 and rebuilt in 1934; a favourite photo spot for cyclists. Free entry. https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Amsterdam/Canal_District
  • 14:00 Rijksmuseum – The national museum housing around 8,000 objects, including Rembrandt’s Night Watch and Vermeer’s Girl with a Pearl Earring. Hours: 09:00‑17:00 daily. Ticket €26.5. https://www.rijksmuseum.nl/en/visit
  • 17:30 La Margarita – Caribbean plates such as rice, beans, plantains and vegetables. High‑budget, €20‑30 per person. https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Amsterdam/Canal_District

Day 3 – Fri 2027-06-04: Jordaan

Weather: light rain, 13‑18 °C • Walking: 4.5 km

  • 10:00 Museum Het Grachtenhuis – Multimedia exhibition telling the story of Amsterdam’s 17th‑century Canal District and its urban planning. Ticket €12. https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Amsterdam/Canal_District
  • 12:00 Anne Frank House – Museum in the building where Anne Frank wrote her diary; includes the Secret Annex and a permanent exhibition. Hours: 09:00‑22:00. Ticket €16. http://www.annefrank.org/
  • 15:00 Van Gogh Museum – Home to the world‑largest collection of Vincent van Gogh’s paintings and drawings. Hours: Fri 09:00‑22:00. Ticket: ticket price: see the website. https://www.vangoghmuseum.nl/en/visit/tickets-and-ticket-prices
  • 17:30 Damsteeg – Modern fish‑focused restaurant with meat and vegetarian options. Mid‑budget. https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Amsterdam/Canal_District
  • 21:00 Pancakes! Amsterdam – Wide variety of sweet and savoury Dutch pancakes. Low‑budget. http://www.pancakesamsterdam.com/

Day 4 – Sat 2027-06-05: North

Weather: rain, 12‑18 °C • Walking: 1.4 km

  • 10:30 Eye Filmmuseum – Film archive and museum on the north bank of the IJ, offering exhibitions and screenings on film history and technology. Hours: 10:00‑19:00 daily. Ticket: ticket price: see the website. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/EYE_Institut_Filmmuseum
  • 13:00 Kinnaree – Thai restaurant; recommended dishes are the green curry and pad thai. Mid‑budget. http://www.restaurantkinnaree.nl
  • 19:25 Depart from Amsterdam – Flight with Finnair. See Getting there and back.

Food to try

  • Stamppot – A traditional Dutch one‑pot meal of mashed potatoes mixed with vegetables, often served with smoked sausage. https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Netherlands#Eat
  • Hutspot – A variant of stamppot made with carrots, onions and potatoes, classic Dutch comfort food. https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Netherlands#Eat
  • Haring (soused herring) – Raw herring served with chopped onion, often in a bun. https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Netherlands#Eat
  • Pannenkoeken – Large Dutch pancakes, sweet with syrup or savoury with cheese and bacon. https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Netherlands#Eat
  • Stroopwafels – Thin waffle cookies filled with caramel‑like syrup, a classic sweet treat. https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Netherlands#Eat
  • Poffertjes – Small fluffy mini‑pancakes dusted with powdered sugar and butter, popular at street stalls. https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Netherlands#Eat
  • Erwtensoep – Thick split‑pea soup, traditionally eaten in winter, often enriched with pork. https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Netherlands#Eat
