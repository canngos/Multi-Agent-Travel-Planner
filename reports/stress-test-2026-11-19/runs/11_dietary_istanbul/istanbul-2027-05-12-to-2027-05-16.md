### Day 1 – Wed 2027-05-12: Sultanahmet (hotel)
Weather: overcast, 17‑28 °C • Walk: –
- **19:20 Arrive in Istanbul** – Flight with Pegasus (see Getting there and back). [source](https://kiwi.com/u/psukpxhnv9)
- **21:30 Boutique Saint Sophia** – Mid‑range vegan dinner; try the vegan mezze platter. Budget: mid. [source](https://www.moganddogtravels.com/blogs/vegan-istanbul)

### Day 2 – Thu 2027-05-13: Sultanahmet
Weather: light drizzle, 16‑24 °C • Walk: 0.9 km
- **09:30 Blue Mosque** – Built 1609‑1617, famed for its six minarets and Iznik tiles. Hours: Mo‑Su 08:30‑12:15, 14:00‑16:45, 17:45‑18:30. ticket price: see the website. [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blue_Mosque,_Istanbul)
- **11:00 Hagia Sophia** – 6th‑century Byzantine cathedral turned mosque, renowned for its massive dome. Hours: Mo‑Su 09:00‑19:30. ticket price: see the website. [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hagia_Sophia)
- **12:30 Galeyan Restaurant** – Vegan‑friendly Turkish dishes; we recommend the lentil soup and pilaf. Budget: mid. [source](https://veggiesabroad.com/istanbul-vegan-restaurants)
- **14:00 Turkish and Islamic Arts Museum** – Houses Ottoman calligraphy, tiles and decorative arts. Hours: Mo‑Su 09:00‑18:30. ticket price: see the website. [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Turkish_and_Islamic_Arts_Museum)
- **15:30 Arasta Bazaar** – Indoor bazaar in Sultanahmet; great for local crafts and souvenirs. [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bazaar)
- **19:00 Vegansartist** – Mid‑range vegan dinner; try the vegan kebab plate. Budget: mid. [source](https://www.momthemuse.com/ultimate-vegan-guide-to-istanbul)

### Day 3 – Fri 2027-05-14: Sultanahmet
Weather: drizzle, 12‑18 °C (rainy) • Walk: 2.2 km
- **09:30 Grand Bazaar** – One of the world’s largest covered markets, >4 000 shops over 30 700 m². Hours: Mo‑Sa 09:00‑19:00. ticket €0 (free entry). [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grand_Bazaar,_Istanbul)
- **12:00 Çemberlitaş Hammam** – Historic Ottoman bath built 1584 by Mimar Sinan. Hours: Mo‑Su 06:00‑23:30. ticket price: see the website. [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/%C3%87emberlita%C5%9F_Hamam)
- **12:30 Vegan Dükkan Lokanta** – Vegan mantı and sarma are highlights. Budget: – (vegan‑friendly). [source](https://www.veganventuresofficial.com/foodieguides/best-vegan-amp-vegan-friendly-spots-in-istanbul-turkey)
- **14:00 Bayezid II Hamam** – Museum of Turkish bath culture, early‑Ottoman building. Free entry. Hours: Weekdays 09:00‑16:30. [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bayezid_II_Hamam)
- **15:30 Theodosius Cistern** – Ancient underground water reservoir, cool indoor visit. Hours: Mo‑Su 09:00‑18:00. [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Theodosius_Cistern)
- **19:00 Ayramin Hotel Taksim** – Mid‑range vegan dinner; the vegetable pilaf is a solid choice. Budget: mid. [source](https://www.moganddogtravels.com/blogs/vegan-istanbul)

### Day 4 – Sat 2027-05-15: Eminönü / Beyoğlu
Weather: overcast, 10‑19 °C • Walk: 0.9 km
- **09:30 Spice Bazaar** – Historic covered market famous for colorful spices, dried fruits and Turkish delights. Hours: 09:00‑19:00 daily. [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spice_Bazaar)
- **12:00 Community Kitchen** – Vegan lunch; try the lentil stew and grain bowl. Budget: mid. [source](https://thehopefulveganista.com/vegan-restaurants-in-istanbul-turkiye-2)
- **13:30 Istanbul Railway Museum** – Displays historic locomotives and railway memorabilia. Free entry. Hours: Tu‑Su 09:30‑17:00 (Mo,PH off). [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Istanbul_Railway_Museum)
- **15:00 Rüstem Pasha Medrese** – Former medrese built 1551 by Mimar Sinan; indoor historic site. [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/R%C3%BCstem_Pasha_Medrese)
- **19:00 Vegan Istanbul** – Low‑budget vegan dinner; definitely try the vegan baklava. Budget: low. [source](https://thehopefulveganista.com/vegan-restaurants-in-istanbul-turkiye-2)

### Day 5 – Sun 2027-05-16: Sultanahmet
Weather: rain, 14‑25 °C (rainy) • Walk: –
- **09:30 Topkapı Palace** – Former Ottoman imperial residence (1460s‑1853), now museum of royal artifacts. [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Topkap%C4%B1_Palace)
- **12:00 Fener Sweet** – Low‑budget vegan snack; enjoy a vegan pastry. Budget: low. [source](https://www.moganddogtravels.com/blogs/vegan-istanbul)
- **15:35 Depart from Istanbul** – Flight with airBaltic (see Getting there and back). [source](https://kiwi.com/u/239n9592jw)
