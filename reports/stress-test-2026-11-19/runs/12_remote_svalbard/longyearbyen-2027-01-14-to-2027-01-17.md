- **22:45 Northern Lights Viewing Spot** – Dark‑sky spot away from settlement lights. Free entry. Outdoor. [info](https://en.visitsvalbard.com/inspiration/various/10-things-about-the-northern-lights)

## Food to try
- **Fårikål** – Stewed lamb and cabbage, Norway’s national dish. [source](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Norway#Eat)
- **Lutefisk** – Dried fish reconstituted in lye, a traditional specialty. [source](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Norway#Eat)
- **Smalahove** – Sheep’s head, traditionally served around Christmas. [source](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Norway#Eat)
- **Pinnekjøtt** – Dried mutton ribs, a festive Christmas dish. [source](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Norway#Eat)
- **Tørrfisk** – Dried cod, staple of coastal communities. [source](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Norway#Eat)
- **Klippfisk** – Salted cod, used in many Arctic recipes. [source](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Norway#Eat)
