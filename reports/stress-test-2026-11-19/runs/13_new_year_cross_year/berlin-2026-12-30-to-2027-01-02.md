## Day by day
*Days in this plan start around 09:30 and end by 22:00, at a balanced pace (the defaults). For another rhythm, tell the concierge, e.g. "we wake up early, start at 07:00", "we stay out late" or "a more relaxed pace, please".*

### Day 1 – Wed 2026-12-30: Berlin
*Weather:* snow, ‑2‑3 °C • Walk ≈ 1 km
- **16:00 Arrive in Berlin** – Eurowings flight lands at Berlin‑Tegel.
- **17:30 Brandenburg Gate** – Iconic 18th‑century neoclassical triumphal arch, free 24/7. [source](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/518071791)
- **18:30 Reichstag Dome** – Glass dome by Norman Foster, symbol of reunification. Open 08:00‑21:45 (Dec 31 08:00‑13:00). Ticket info: see the website. [source](https://www.openstreetmap.org/node/2432753840)
- **20:30 Die Berliner Republik** – Mid‑range German cuisine; try the pork knuckle. Mid budget. [source](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Berlin/Mitte)

### Day 2 – Thu 2026-12-31: Berlin
*Weather:* snow, ‑4‑3 °C (rainy) • Walk ≈ 5.1 km
- **10:30 Humboldt Forum** – Museum of non‑European art, ticket €14. Open Mo,We‑Su 10:30‑18:30. [source](https://www.openstreetmap.org/node/8413352835)
- **12:15 Witty's Currywurst** – Low‑budget organic currywurst. Low budget. [source](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Berlin/Mitte)
- **13:30 DDR Museum** – Interactive look at East German life, ticket €13.5. Open 09:00‑21:00. [source](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/175149971)
- **15:45 Berlin Cathedral** – Largest Protestant church, free nave entry. Open Mo‑Fr 09:00‑18:00, Sa 09:00‑17:00, Su/PH 12:00‑17:00. [source](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/313670734)
- **19:30 Kopps** – Mid‑range vegan restaurant; try the vegan schnitzel. Mid budget. [source](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Berlin/Mitte)
- **21:30 Brandenburg Gate (NYE fireworks)** – Main gathering point for Berlin’s New Year’s Eve fireworks. Free, 24/7. [source](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/518071791)

### Day 3 – Fri 2027-01-01: Berlin
*Weather:* snow, 1‑3 °C (rainy) • Walk ≈ 2.3 km
- **10:00 German Historical Museum** – History of Germany, ticket €8. Open daily 10:00‑18:00. [source](https://www.openstreetmap.org/node/3203167561)
- **12:15 Chi Sing Restaurant** – Mid‑range Chinese; try the dumplings. Mid budget. [source](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Berlin/Mitte)
- **14:00 Afternoon coffee break** – Relax at a nearby café (free).
- **18:30 Grill Royal** – High‑end grill, great steaks; reservation recommended. High budget. [source](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Berlin/Mitte)
- **22:30 Berghain** – Legendary techno club, entry €25‑30. Open midnight‑late Fri‑Sat. [source](https://www.technoairlines.com/blog/how-to-get-into-berghain)

### Day 4 – Sat 2027-01-02: Berlin
*Weather:* heavy snow, ‑1‑3 °C (rainy) • Walk ≈ 2.3 km
- **09:30 Fountain of the Peoples' Friendship** – Concrete fountain on Alexanderplatz, popular NYE spot. Free. [source](https://opentripmap.com/en/card/W52564405)
- **11:30 Lucky Star** – Mid‑range Chinese hot‑pot, €12.80 per person (min 2). Mid budget. [source](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Berlin/Mitte)
- **14:30 Museum der Stille** – Free contemporary art space, open Sat‑Sun 14:00‑19:00. Free entry. [source](https://www.openstreetmap.org/node/4261575774)
- **19:30 Depart from Berlin** – Finnair flight back to Helsinki.
