## Day by day

*Days in this plan start around 09:30 and end by 22:00, at a balanced pace (the defaults). For another rhythm, tell the concierge, e.g. "we wake up early, start at 07:00", "we stay out late" or "a more relaxed pace, please".*

### Day 1 – Fri 2027-05-21: Monte Carlo
*Weather*: partly cloudy, 15‑26 °C *Walking*: 2.8 km
- **09:10 Arrive in Monaco** – Flight with Finnair (see Getting there).
- **10:45 Taxi to accommodation** – Transfer to the Duplex in a village house.
- **12:30 Le Grill** – Michelin‑style rooftop grill with panoramic Monte‑Carlo views. *Budget tier*: high.  Recommended: Alpine meat plates.  [source](https://www.montecarlosbm.com/en/restaurant-monaco/the-grill)
- **16:30 Monte‑Carlo Casino** – The iconic casino opened in 1863 and forms the heart of Monte‑Carlo’s gambling and cultural life, housing the Opéra de Monte‑Carlo and the ballet. Open 14:00‑04:00. *Ticket*: see the website.  [source](http://www.casinomontecarlo.com)
- **18:30 Yacht Club de Monaco** – Founded in 1953, the club gathers over 1,200 members and its Lord Foster‑designed building hosts the YCM Gallery.  [source](http://www.yacht-club-monaco.mc/en/)
- **20:00 Louis XV** – 3‑star Michelin restaurant by Alain Ducasse. *Budget tier*: high.  Signature sea‑bass with artichokes.  [source](https://www.montecarlosbm.com/en/restaurant-monaco/le-louis-xv-alain-ducasse-hotel-de-paris)

### Day 2 – Sat 2027-05-22: Monte Carlo
*Weather*: clear, 17‑25 °C *Walking*: 3.5 km
- **10:00 Prince's Palace of Monaco** – Built as a Genoese fortress in 1191, the palace is the Prince’s official residence and offers audio‑guided tours with panoramic views. Open 10:00‑18:00. *Ticket €10*.  [source](http://www.palais.mc/)
- **11:30 St. Nicholas Cathedral** – Baroque cathedral where Prince Rainier III was married; quick visit. Open 10:00‑12:30.  [source](null)
- **12:30 Chez Roger** – Classic local snacks: socca (€3) and pissaladière (€2). *Budget tier*: low.  [source](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Monaco)
- **15:30 Musée des Timbres et des Monnaies** – Presents Monaco’s postal and monetary history, with stamps dating back to 1640. Open 09:30‑17:00 (Jul‑Aug 09:30‑18:00). *Ticket €3*.  
- **16:30 The Car Collection of HSH the Prince of Monaco** – Exhibition of vintage and Formula 1 cars from the Prince’s personal collection. Open Sep‑May 10:00‑18:00 (Jun‑Aug 10:00‑19:00).  [source](https://www.mtcc.mc/)
- **19:30 Beefbar** – High‑quality beef cuts in a sleek setting; side portions €8.5. *Budget tier*: mid.  [source](http://beefbar.com/)

### Day 3 – Sun 2027-05-23: Monte Carlo
*Weather*: overcast, 18‑27 °C *Walking*: 2.4 km
- **10:00 Oceanographic Museum of Monaco** – Inaugurated in 1910 by Prince Albert I, the museum showcases marine fauna, historic submarines and was once directed by Jacques‑Yves Cousteau. Open 10:00‑18:00 (varies by season).  [source](null)
- **12:30 L'Express** – Pizza, pasta and the “Farandole Monégasque” sampler plate. *Budget tier*: mid.  [source](https://lexpress-restaurant-monaco.com/)
- **14:30 Jardins de Saint‑Martin** – Terraced gardens on the Rock of Monaco with Mediterranean flora and a bronze statue of Prince Albert I.  [source](https://opentripmap.com/en/card/W88875704)
- **16:30 Café de Paris** – Iconic Monte‑Carlo café ideal for people‑watching. Open 08:00‑02:00.  [source](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Monaco)
- **19:30 Cooks Monaco** – Italian‑style pizzeria with a rotisserie corner; relaxed dinner. *Budget tier*: low.  

### Day 4 – Mon 2027-05-24: Monte Carlo
*Weather*: overcast, 19‑28 °C *Walking*: 0.8 km
- **10:30 Nouveau Musée National – Villa Sauber** – Belle Époque villa hosting contemporary art exhibitions. Open 10:00‑18:00 during exhibitions. *Ticket €6*.  [source](http://www.nmnm.mc/)
- **12:30 Maison des pâtes Saint‑Charles** – Freshly made pasta, gnocchi and ravioli. *Budget tier*: low.  
- **16:30 Lo Sfizio Street** – Casual eatery offering tasty pies; good for a snack. *Budget tier*: low.  
- **20:55 Depart from Monaco** – Flight with Norwegian (see Getting there).

## Food to try
- **Socca** – A salty chickpea flour pancake, often served hot with pepper. – [source](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Monaco#Eat)
- **Pissaladière** – A savory tart topped with caramelised onions, olives and anchovies. – [source](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Monaco#Eat)
- **Barbajuan** – Flaky, warm fritter stuffed with spinach and other ingredients, considered Monaco’s national dish. – [source](https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Q2294743)
- **Bouillabaisse** – A rich fish stew made with various Mediterranean fish, shellfish and saffron. – [source](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Monaco#Eat)
