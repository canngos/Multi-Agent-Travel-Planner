Food to try

  • Ålandspannkaka – Traditional Åland pancake made from semolina porridge, flavored with cardamom or vanilla, served with stewed plums or raspberry jam and whipped cream. source
  • Smoked flounder with potato salad – Smoked flounder served alongside a classic potato salad, a typical fish dish on the islands. source
  • Svartbröd – Dense, sweet black rye bread flavored with treacle, traditionally used on long sea voyages. source
  • Kastelholm cheese – Historic cheese from the ÅCA dairy, often enjoyed with svartbröd, tracing its roots back to the 16th century. source
  • Taffel Crisps – Local potato crisps produced in Åland from island‑grown potatoes, sold in the factory outlet in Saltvik. source
