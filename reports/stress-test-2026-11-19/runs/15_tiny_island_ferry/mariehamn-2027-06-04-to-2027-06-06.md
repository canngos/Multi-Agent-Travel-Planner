### Day 1 – Fri 2027-06-04: Mariehamn centre
*Weather*: drizzle, 14‑18 °C – **0.5 km walk**.
- **16:00 Arrive in Mariehamn** – Flight with Finnair. [source](https://kiwi.com/u/purcufg9m9)
- **16:30 Bagarstugan Cafe och Vin** – Try the traditional **Ålandspannkaka** (≈ €6.5). Low‑budget snack. [source](https://bagarstugan.ax/)

### Day 2 – Sat 2027-06-05: Mariehamn centre
*Weather*: light rain, 12‑16 °C – **2.2 km walk**.
- **10:00 Pommern (museum ship)** – Four‑masted barque built in Glasgow (1903) now a museum ship; ticket €15. [source](https://www.pommern.ax/)
- **11:45 Åland Maritime Museum** – Exhibits ship‑building artefacts, captain’s saloon of Herzogin Cecilie; ticket €16.5. [source](https://sjofartsmuseum.ax)
- **13:00 Draken Kebab Hamn** – Low‑budget kebab lunch. [source](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Mariehamn)
- **14:30 Åland Museum and Åland Art Museum** – History from prehistoric to modern times; ticket €8. [source](https://www.museum.ax/museer-sevardheter/alands-kulturhistoriska-museum)
- **15:30 Mariehamn Museum** – Model of 1920s Mariehamn, free entry. [source](https://www.mariehamnsmuseet.hembygd.fi/)
- **18:30 Pizza Diablo** – Mid‑range pizza, popular local spot. [source](https://diablo.ax/)

## Food to try
- **Ålandspannkaka** – Traditional Åland pancake made from semolina porridge, flavored with cardamom or vanilla, served with stewed plums or raspberry jam and whipped cream. [source](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/%C3%85land#Eat)
- **Smoked flounder with potato salad** – Smoked flounder served alongside a classic potato salad, a typical fish dish on the islands. [source](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/%C3%85land#Eat)
- **Svartbröd** – Dense, sweet black rye bread flavored with treacle, traditionally used on long sea voyages. [source](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/%C3%85land#Eat)
- **Kastelholm cheese** – Historic cheese from the ÅCA dairy, often enjoyed with svartbröd, tracing its roots back to the 16th century. [source](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/%C3%85land#Eat)
- **Taffel Crisps** – Local potato crisps produced in Åland from island‑grown potatoes, sold in the factory outlet in Saltvik. [source](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/%C3%85land#Eat)
