## Day by day
*Days in this plan start around 09:30 and end by 22:00, at a balanced pace (the defaults). For another rhythm, tell the concierge, e.g. "we wake up early, start at 07:00", "we stay out late" or "a more relaxed pace, please".*

### Day 1 – Thu 2027-06-03: la Dreta de l'Eixample
*Weather:* overcast, 20‑24 °C &nbsp;• Walking ≈ 7 km
- **09:10 Arrive in Barcelona** – Flight arrival (see Getting there).
- **11:15 Palau de la Música Catalana** – UNESCO‑listed Modernista concert hall designed by Lluís Domènech i Montaner (built 1905‑1908). Open 09:00‑15:30. *ticket price: see the website* – [source](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/377216661)
- **13:00 Can Xurrades** – Mid‑range Catalan dishes; try the **Ous Estrellats** (scrambled eggs with potato). Mid‑budget – [source](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Barcelona/Gr%C3%A0cia)
- **14:45 Cathedral of Santa Eulàlia** – Gothic cathedral spanning centuries, housing the city’s patron saint shrine. Open 09:30‑18:30 (Mon‑Fri). *ticket price: see the website* – [source](http://www.catedralbcn.org/)
- **16:30 Museu d'Història de Barcelona** – Shows Roman ruins beneath the Gothic Quarter, tracing Barcelona from its origins. Open 10:00‑19:00 (Mo‑Sa). *ticket price: see the website* – [source](https://www.barcelona.cat/museuhistoria/)
- **20:00 La Rosa del Desierto** – Moroccan tapas; try the lamb tagine. Mid‑budget – [source](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Barcelona/Gr%C3%A0cia)

### Day 2 – Fri 2027-06-04: la Dreta de l'Eixample
*Weather:* rain, 16‑24 °C &nbsp;• Walking ≈ 3.6 km
- **10:00 Basilica of Santa Maria del Mar** – Iconic Catalan Gothic basilica (1329‑1383) with soaring interior and thin columns. Open 10:00‑20:30. *ticket price: see the website* – [source](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Barcelona/Ciutat_Vella)
- **13:30 Sol i Lluna** – Cozy Mediterranean spot; good for a relaxed lunch. Mid‑budget – [source](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Barcelona/Gr%C3%A0cia)
- **18:00 Botafumeiro** – High‑end seafood; try the grilled fish platter. High‑budget – [source](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Barcelona/Gr%C3%A0cia)
- **20:30 Metro tip** – Use metro line 3 from Santa Eulàlia to Barceloneta to shorten walking.

### Day 3 – Sat 2027-06-05: la Dreta de l'Eixample
*Weather:* light drizzle, 16‑21 °C &nbsp;• Walking ≈ 5.1 km
- **10:00 Palau Güell** – Early Gaudí mansion showing his first modernista experiments; rooftop chimneys are iconic. Open 10:00‑20:00 (Apr‑Oct). *ticket €12* – [source](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/166565579)
- **13:30 Frankfurt's Casa Valles** – Cheap burgers & hot dogs; perfect for a quick lunch. Low‑budget – [source](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Barcelona/Gr%C3%A0cia)
- **15:30 Palau Robert** – Historic exhibition building, formerly a private aristocratic residence; free entry. Open 09:00‑20:00. *free entry* – [source](https://palaurobert.gencat.cat/)
- **16:00 Casa Vicens** – Gaudí’s first major work, an orientalist‑style modernist house (1883‑1885). Open 10:00‑20:00 daily. Free entry – [source](https://casavicens.org/)
- **20:00 Karakala** – Lebanese tapas; try the mixed mezze. Mid‑budget – [source](http://www.karakala.com/)

### Day 4 – Sun 2027-06-06: la Dreta de l'Eixample
*Weather:* overcast, 17‑24 °C
- **09:00 Casa Batlló** – Gaudí masterpiece with undulating façade and colourful mosaics. Open 08:30‑22:30 (last admission 21:30). *ticket €35* – [source](https://www.casabatllo.es/en/online-tickets)
- **10:45 Depart from Barcelona** – Flight departure (see Getting there).

## Food to try
- **paella** – rice dish from the Valencian Community, Spain – [source](https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Q212121)
- **gazpacho** – cold soup from Spain and Portugal – [source](https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Q202677)
- **churro** – fried‑dough pastry – [source](https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Q727605)
- **aioli** – Provençal sauce made of garlic, olive oil, usually egg yolks, and seasonings – [source](https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Q283231)
- **chorizo** – pork sausage originating and typical of the Iberian Peninsula, seasoned with spices and dried – [source](https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Q23374)
- **Spanish omelette** – egg, potato and onion dish – [source](https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Q281751)
- **marzipan** – confection consisting primarily of sugar or honey and almond meal (ground almonds) – [source](https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Q106252)
