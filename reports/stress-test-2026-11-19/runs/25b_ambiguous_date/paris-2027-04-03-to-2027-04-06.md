## Day by day
*Days in this plan start around 09:30 and end by 22:00, at a balanced pace (the defaults). For another rhythm, tell the concierge, e.g. "we wake up early, start at 07:00", "we stay out late" or "a more relaxed pace, please".*

### Day 1 – Sat 2027-04-03: Drancy
*Weather:* light drizzle, 13‑3 °C • Walk: 3.3 km
- **09:40 Arrive in Paris** – Flight landed at CDG. See *Getting there and back*.
- **10:30 Conciergerie** – Originally part of the royal palace, the Conciergerie was a medieval courthouse and later a Revolutionary prison where Marie‑Antoinette was held before execution. Open 09:30‑18:00. Ticket info: see website. [Source](https://www.paris-conciergerie.fr)
- **13:00 Notre‑Dam Treasury** – The Treasury of Notre‑Dame displays liturgical objects, reliquaries and treasures recovered from the cathedral’s archaeological excavations, reflecting centuries of French religious art. Open 09:30‑18:30 (weekends slightly later). Ticket info: see website. [Source](https://www.notredamedeparis.fr/visiter/visiter-le-tresor)
- **13:30 Lunch – La Robe et le Palais** – Mid‑budget bistro; try the quiche. Budget tier: mid. [Source](http://larobeetlepalais.fr/en)
- **16:30 Musée national du Moyen Âge** – Housed in a former royal palace, the National Museum of the Middle Ages showcases medieval art, including the famous "Lady and the Unicorn" tapestries. Ticket info: see website. [Source](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Paris)
- **18:30 Dinner – L'Express Bar** – Low‑budget spot for a quick dinner. Budget tier: low. [Source](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Paris/1st_arrondissement)

### Day 2 – Sun 2027-04-04: Drancy
*Weather:* light drizzle, 17‑11 °C • Walk: 1.1 km
- **09:45 Louvre Museum** – National art museum housed in the historic Louvre Palace, originally a medieval fortress turned royal residence. See the Mona Lisa. Hours 09:00‑18:00 (Wed/Fri 09:00‑21:00). **ticket €22**. [Source](https://www.louvre.fr/en/visit/hours-admission/tickets-and-prices)
- **13:00 Lunch – Café Marly** – Mid‑budget café with a view of the Louvre Pyramid; great for a croissant and coffee. Budget tier: high. [Source](http://cafe-marly.com/en/)
- **14:30 Musée des Arts Décoratifs** – The Museum of Decorative Arts displays eight centuries of French design, from furniture and glassware to fashion and graphic arts, within a wing of the Louvre Palace. Ticket info: see website. [Source](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Paris)
- **18:30 Dinner – Beef Club & Co** – Mid‑budget steakhouse, open all evening. Budget tier: mid. [Source](https://beefclubandco.com/)

### Day 3 – Mon 2027-04-05: Drancy
*Weather:* drizzle, 15‑7 °C • Walk: 3.8 km
- **10:00 Musée du Luxembourg** – Founded in 1750, the Musée du Luxembourg was France’s first museum of contemporary art and now hosts temporary exhibitions of modern and historic works within the former palace orangery. Hours 10:00‑19:00 (Mon/Fri 10:00‑21:30). Ticket info: see website. [Source](https://museeduluxembourg.fr/)
- **13:00 Lunch – Lemoni Café** – Mid‑budget café, good for a croissant and lunch. Budget tier: mid. [Source](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Paris/1st_arrondissement)
- **15:00 Musée de l'Orangerie** – Located in the Tuileries Garden, the Orangerie houses Monet’s eight large Water Lilies murals and an important collection of Impressionist and Post‑Impressionist paintings. Ticket info: see website. [Source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mus%C3%A9e_de_l%27Orangerie)
- **19:00 Dinner – Pizza Nelly.** – Low‑budget pizzeria. Budget tier: low. [Source](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Paris/1st_arrondissement)
