## Day by day

*Days in this plan start around 09:30 and end by 22:00, at a balanced pace (the defaults). For another rhythm, tell the concierge, e.g. "we wake up early, start at 07:00", "we stay out late" or "a more relaxed pace, please".*

### Day 1 – Thu 2027‑06‑10: Pechersk Lavra district
*Weather:* light drizzle • Walking: 6.7 km
- **11:40 Arrive in Kyiv** – Flight with Finnair & Wizz Air Malta. See *Getting there and back*.
- **13:30 Kyiv Pechersk Lavra** – Founded in 1077, the oldest cave monastery in Eastern Europe, UNESCO World Heritage. Open summer 08:00‑19:00. Ticket **€5.5**. [source](https://visitkyiv.com/sights-and-landmarks/kyiv-pechersk-lavra)
- **15:45 National Museum of the History of Ukraine in the Second World War** – “Motherland” monument and indoor exhibitions about WWII. Open Mo‑Fr 10:00‑19:00; Sa‑Su 10:00‑20:00. Ticket price: see the website. [source](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/158427185)
- **17:30 Two Geese – Pechersk** – Low‑budget cafeteria; try the borshch. Budget tier: low. [source](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Kyiv)
- **19:00 Mystetskyi Arsenal** – Contemporary art housed in a former 18th‑century arsenal. Open Thu‑Sun 11:00‑20:00. Ticket price: see the website. [source](https://artarsenal.in.ua/)
- **20:45 Korchma Budmo** – Low‑budget Ukrainian kitchen, good for dinner. Budget tier: low. Open daily 09:00‑23:00. [source](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Kyiv)

### Day 2 – Fri 2027‑06‑11: Old Kyiv (Upper Town)
*Weather:* light drizzle • Walking: 1.9 km
- **09:30 Saint Sophia Cathedral** – 11th‑century UNESCO World Heritage site, famous frescoes. Open 09:00‑16:00. Ticket price: see the website. [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saint%20Sophia%20Cathedral%2C%20Kyiv)
- **12:30 Zavertailo** – Mid‑budget café; recommended croissants. Budget tier: mid. [source](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Kyiv)
- **13:30 House of the Metropolitan** – 18th‑century palace, now museum of Kyiv’s Orthodox hierarchy. Ticket price: see the website. [source](https://uk.wikipedia.org/wiki/%D0%91%D1%83%D0%B4%D0%B8%D0%BD%D0%BE%D0%BA%20%D0%BC%D0%B8%D1%82%D1%80%D0%BE%D0%BF%D0%BE%D0%BB%D0%B8%D1%82%D0%B0)
- **15:30 St. Michael's Golden‑Domed Monastery** – Rebuilt 1997‑98, gold domes dominate Kyiv’s skyline; offers city views from its park. Budget tier: low (outdoor). [source](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Kyiv)
- **19:00 Puzata Khata** – Low‑budget cafeteria serving classic Ukrainian dishes. Budget tier: low. [source](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Kyiv)

### Day 3 – Sat 2027‑06‑12: Central Kyiv
*Weather:* rain (rainy) • Walking: 1.0 km
- **09:30 Zustrich** – Mid‑budget café known for tasty borshch. Budget tier: mid. [source](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Kyiv)
- **11:00 October Palace** – Historic cultural centre, former Soviet exhibition hall, now hosts contemporary art and performances. Open daily 11:00‑19:00. Ticket price: see the website. [source](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/62805186)

## Food to try
- **borshch** – A beet soup typically garnished with sour cream. – [source](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Ukraine#Eat)
- **varenyky** – Dumplings filled with berries, vegetables, fruits or mushrooms. – [source](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Ukraine#Eat)
- **deruny** – Crispy potato pancakes. – [source](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Ukraine#Eat)
- **kholodets** – A molded meat gelatin dish served cold. – [source](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Ukraine#Eat)
- **holubtsi** – Cabbage rolls stuffed with meat and rice. – [source](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Ukraine#Eat)
- **medovyk** – Honey cake, a traditional Ukrainian dessert. – [source](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Ukraine#Eat)
- **napoleon** – Layered pastry cake popular in Ukraine. – [source](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Ukraine#Eat)
- **mlyntsi** – Thin Ukrainian pancakes, often served with sweet or savory fillings. – [source](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Ukraine#Eat)
