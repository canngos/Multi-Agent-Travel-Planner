Food to try

  • Paella – Rice dish from the Valencian Community, traditionally made with meat (chicken/rabbit) or seafood. source
  • Arròs a banda – Rice cooked in fish stock and served with alioli, a classic Valencian coastal specialty. source
  • Arròs negre – Black rice flavored with squid ink, squid or cuttlefish, peppers and paprika. source
  • Fideuà – Paella‑like dish using short noodles and fish, invented in nearby Gandía. source
  • All i pebre – Hearty stew of eel from the Albufera lagoon, seasoned with garlic and paprika. source
