### Day 2 – Fri 2027-06-04: Museu de Belles Arts, Almodí, Catedral, Torres de Serranos, Torres de Quart
*Weather:* partly cloudy, 20.1‑30.9 °C • Walking: 3.7 km
- **10:15 Museu de Belles Arts de València** – Free entry; founded 1913, it houses ~2,000 works from the 14th‑17th centuries (Velázquez, El Greco, Goya). Hours 10:00‑20:00. [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Museu_de_Belles_Arts_de_Val%C3%A8ncia)
- **11:45 Torres de Serranos** – Medieval gate towers offering panoramic city views. *Ticket price: see the website*. Hours 10:00‑18:00 (Mon‑Sat). [source](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/9281502)
- **12:30 Central Bar** – tapas bar (mid‑budget). Try the daily‑changing tapas, especially the tortilla de patatas. Hours 06:30‑15:30. [source](http://www.centralbar.es/en/)
- **13:45 Almodí (Almudín)** – Contemporary art in a restored 14th‑century granary. Ticket **€2**. Hours 09:30‑14:00 & 15:00‑19:00 (Mon‑Sat). [source](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Valencia)
- **15:30 Catedral de Santa María de Valencia** – Gothic‑Romanesque‑Moorish cathedral; ticket **€9**. Hours 10:00‑18:30 (Apr‑Oct). [source](https://catedraldevalencia.es/)
- **17:00 Torres de Quart** – Surviving medieval gate with historic battle markings. Ticket **€2**. Hours 09:30‑19:00 (Mon‑Sat). [source](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Valencia)
- **20:30 El Poblet Restaurante** – Michelin‑starred chef Quique Dacosta’s modern Valencian dishes. Mid‑budget; mains ~€21. Hours 20:30‑22:30 (Mon). [source](http://elpobletrestaurante.com/home)

### Day 3 – Sat 2027-06-05: Lonja de la Seda, Central Market, Museu Nacional de Ceràmica
*Weather:* drizzle, 19.7‑23.2 °C • Walking: 2.1 km
- **09:30 Central Market** – Art Nouveau hall to browse fresh produce and local delicacies. Hours 07:30‑15:00. [source](https://www.mercadocentralvalencia.es)
- **10:45 Lonja de la Seda** – UNESCO‑listed Gothic silk exchange, indoor historic gem. *Ticket price: see the website*. Hours 10:00‑19:00 (Mon‑Sat). [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lonja_de_la_Seda)
- **12:30 Tasca Ángel** – Popular tapas bar (low‑budget). Try the grilled sardines. Hours 10:30‑15:00 & 19:30‑23:30. [source](https://www.facebook.com/Tasca-Angel-163047987042853/)
- **16:00 Museu Nacional de Ceràmica y Artes Suntuarias González Martí** – Historic ceramics museum in a Baroque palace. Ticket **€3**. Hours 10:00‑14:00 & 16:00‑20:00. [source](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Valencia)
- **18:00 Bar Ricardo** – Traditional seafood tapas (mid‑budget). Recommended patatas bravas. Hours 08:00‑00:00 (Tue‑Sat). [source](http://barricardo.com/english.html)

### Day 4 – Sun 2027-06-06: City of Arts and Sciences, L'Oceanogràfic
*Weather:* overcast, 18.9‑25.0 °C • Walking: 0.3 km
- **10:00 City of Arts and Sciences** – Futuristic cultural complex designed by Santiago Calatrava & Félix Candela; includes science museum, opera house, etc. *Ticket price: see the website*. Hours 10:00‑15:00 (Sun). [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/City_of_Arts_and_Sciences)
- **12:30 L'Oceanogràfic** – Europe’s largest oceanarium with dolphinarium and shark tunnel. *Ticket price: see the website*. Open daily, hours vary. [source](https://www.oceanografic.org/en/)
- **18:00 Depart from Valencia** – Flight with Lufthansa (see Getting there and back). [source](https://kiwi.com/u/pwccmh874c)

## Food to try
- **Paella** – Rice dish from the Valencian Community, traditionally made with meat (chicken/rabbit) or seafood. [source](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Valencia)
- **Arròs a banda** – Rice cooked in fish stock and served with alioli, a classic Valencian coastal specialty. [source](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Valencia)
- **Arròs negre** – Black rice flavored with squid ink, squid or cuttlefish, peppers and paprika. [source](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Valencia)
- **Fideuà** – Paella‑like dish using short noodles and fish, invented in nearby Gandía. [source](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Valencia)
- **All i pebre** – Hearty stew of eel from the Albufera lagoon, seasoned with garlic and paprika. [source](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Valencia)
