Food to try

  • Borscht – Spicy red beet soup, often served hot with dumplings or a fried pâté roll. source
  • Pierogi – Stuffed dumplings with fillings such as cottage cheese and onion, meat, or forest fruits. source
  • Bigos – Hunter's stew of various meats and vegetables on a pickled cabbage base, thick and hearty. source
  • Zapiekanka – Toasted baguette half topped with mushrooms, cheese and sauces, sold at the Okrąglak market. source
  • Obwarzanek – Doughy ring halfway between a bagel and a pretzel, sold everywhere for about 3 zł. source
  • Kotlet schabowy – Breaded pork chop, similar to a German/Austrian schnitzel. source
  • Żurek – Sour rye soup served with traditional Polish sausage and a hard‑boiled egg. source
  • Gołąbki – Cabbage rolls stuffed with grains and meat, served with a white or tomato sauce. source
