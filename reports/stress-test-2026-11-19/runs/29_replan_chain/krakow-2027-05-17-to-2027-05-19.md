## Day 1 – Mon 2027‑05‑17: Old Town
*Weather*: heavy drizzle, 8‑11 °C (rainy). Walk about 4.4 km.
- **08:05 Arrive in Krakow** – Flight landing. See *Getting there and back*.
- **09:35 Wawel Cathedral** – The nearly 1 000‑year‑old cathedral was the coronation site of Polish monarchs, displaying Romanesque to Baroque elements. Open hours not listed. Ticket: “ticket price: see the website”. [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wawel_Cathedral)
- **11:15 Rynek Underground** – Museum beneath the Main Market Square showing archaeological finds that reveal medieval life in Kraków. Open 10:00‑20:00 (Tue 10:00‑16:00, etc.). Ticket: “ticket price: see the website”. [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rynek_Underground)
- **13:00 Mały Śledź** – Traditional bar serving bigos, herring and, crucially, pierogi. Budget tier: low. Recommend trying the pierogi with mushroom‑onion filling. [source](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Krak%C3%B3w/Kazimierz)
- **13:45 Town Hall Tower** – Historic tower in the Main Market Square housing a museum on municipal history. Open 11:00‑18:00 (Mon 11:00‑15:00). Ticket: “ticket price: see the website”. [source](https://muzeumkrakowa.pl/oddzialy/wieza-ratuszowa)
- **14:30 Wawel Castle** – The royal castle on Wawel Hill showcases medieval, Renaissance and Baroque architecture; it is Poland’s most visited art museum. Open 09:30‑17:00 (Mon 10:00‑16:00). Ticket: “ticket price: see the website”. [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wawel_Castle)
- **18:30 Hamsa** – Mediterranean‑style dinner (Israeli/Syrian/Moroccan). Mid‑budget. Good spot to unwind after a day of sights. [source](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Krak%C3%B3w/Kazimierz)

## Day 2 – Tue 2027‑05‑18: Old Town
*Weather*: rain, 8‑13 °C (rainy). Walk about 6.9 km.
- **10:00 National Museum in Kraków** – Poland’s largest museum, founded 1879, with 21 departments covering art from antiquity to modern times. Open 10:00‑18:00 (Tue‑Sun). Ticket: “ticket price: see the website”. [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Museum_in_Krak%C3%B3w)
- **12:30 Poranki – breakfast, coffee & cake** – Hearty breakfast with generous portions; try a traditional Polish pastry. Mid‑budget. [source](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Krak%C3%B3w/Kazimierz)
- **13:30 St. Mary's Basilica** – Brick‑Gothic 14th‑century church famed for its wooden altarpiece by Veit Stoss and the hourly Hejnał mariacki trumpet call. Open 11:30‑18:00 (Mon‑Sat). Ticket: “ticket price: see the website”. [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Basilica_of_the_Assumption_of_the_Blessed_Virgin_Mary,_Krak%C3%B3w)
- **14:30 Museum of Pharmacy** – Exhibits historic pharmacy equipment, bottles and laboratory gear, tracing the profession in Poland. Open 12:00‑18:30 (Tue) and 09:30‑15:00 (Wed‑Sat). Ticket: “ticket price: see the website”. [source](http://muzeum.farmacja.uj.edu.pl)
- **16:30 Oskar Schindler's Enamel Factory** – Museum in the former enamel factory documenting Kraków’s WWII history and Schindler’s rescue efforts. Open 09:00‑19:00 (Tue‑Sun). Ticket: “ticket price: see the website”. [source](https://muzeumkrakowa.pl/oddzialy/fabryka-emalia-oskara-schindlera)
- **19:00 Judah Food Market** – Street‑food market with several stalls offering pierogi, zapiekanki and other quick bites. Low‑budget; definitely try the pierogi here. [source](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Krak%C3%B3w/Kazimierz)

## Food to try
- **Borscht** – Spicy red beet soup, often served hot with dumplings or a fried pâté roll. [source](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Poland#Eat)
- **Pierogi** – Stuffed dumplings with fillings such as cottage cheese and onion, meat, or forest fruits. [source](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Krak%C3%B3w)
- **Bigos** – Hunter's stew of various meats and vegetables on a pickled cabbage base, thick and hearty. [source](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Poland#Eat)
- **Zapiekanka** – Toasted baguette half topped with mushrooms, cheese and sauces, sold at the Okrąglak market. [source](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Krak%C3%B3w)
- **Obwarzanek** – Doughy ring halfway between a bagel and a pretzel, sold everywhere for about 3 zł. [source](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Krak%C3%B3w)
- **Kotlet schabowy** – Breaded pork chop, similar to a German/Austrian schnitzel. [source](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Poland#Eat)
- **Żurek** – Sour rye soup served with traditional Polish sausage and a hard‑boiled egg. [source](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Poland#Eat)
- **Gołąbki** – Cabbage rolls stuffed with grains and meat, served with a white or tomato sauce. [source](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Poland#Eat)
