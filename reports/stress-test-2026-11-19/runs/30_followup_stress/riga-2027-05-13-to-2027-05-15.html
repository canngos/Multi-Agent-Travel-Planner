Food to try

  • Cottage cheese (biezpiens) – A staple dairy product, often served with honey or fresh fruit. source
  • Kotlete – Meat patties made from minced pork, commonly served with potatoes or grain sides. source
  • Skābēti kāposti – Fermented cabbage, a typical side dish accompanying many Latvian meals. source
  • Griķi – Buckwheat groats, widely used as a side or base for soups. source
  • Kūpināts siers – Smoked cheese, often enjoyed with rye bread or as part of a cold platter. source
  • Gaileņu mērce – Herb sauce made with dill and other herbs, used to flavor fish or potatoes. source
