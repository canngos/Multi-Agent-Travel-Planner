## Day by day
*Days in this plan start at 07:00 and end by 22:00, at a balanced pace. For another rhythm, tell the concierge, e.g. "we wake up early, start at 07:00", "we stay out late" or "a more relaxed pace, please".*

### Day 1 – Thu 2027-05-13: Central Riga (art‑nouveau focus)
*Weather:* light rain, 6‑11 °C – **rainy**. Walking ~1.3 km.
- **10:10 Arrive in Riga** – airBaltic flight lands at Riga International Airport.
- **12:00 Riga Art Nouveau Centre** – The centre documents Riga’s rich Art Nouveau heritage, which makes up about one‑third of the city’s central buildings and was built during the 1904‑1914 economic boom. Hours: Tu‑Su 10:00‑18:00. ticket price: see the website. [source](https://www.openstreetmap.org/node/1160803251)
- **13:30 Latvian National Museum of Art** – Holds Latvia’s most important art collection, originating from the 1869 Riga City Art Gallery, displaying works from the 18th century to today. Hours: Tu‑Th 10:00‑18:00; Fr 10:00‑20:00; Sa‑Su 10:00‑17:00. ticket €6. [source](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/87971760)
- **15:30 Melnais ķiploks** – Try the smoked cheese **kūpināts siers** with rye bread. Open 12:00‑23:00. [source](https://www.openstreetmap.org/node/6289393920)
- **18:30 Key to Riga** – Regional dishes; a good spot for a relaxed dinner. Open Mo‑Th,Su 12:00‑22:00; Fr‑Sa 12:00‑23:00. [source](https://www.openstreetmap.org/node/445460372)

### Day 2 – Fri 2027-05-14: Old Town & Market
*Weather:* light drizzle, 5‑16 °C – **not rainy**. Walking ~1.3 km.
- **07:30 Riga Central Market** – One of Europe’s largest market complexes and a UNESCO World Heritage site, housed in repurposed German Zeppelin hangars with a mix of neoclassical and Art Deco architecture. Free entry. Pavilions 07:30‑18:00; Area 07:00‑18:00. [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Riga_Central_Market)
- **10:30 House of the Blackheads** – Historic guild building (1334) rebuilt after WWII, now a museum of Riga’s trading past. Hours: Su‑Th 10:00‑17:00; Fr‑Sa 10:00‑16:00. ticket €8. [source](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/87495471)
- **12:00 Neiburgs** – Enjoy **kotlete** (meat patties) for lunch. Open Mo 11:00‑15:00; Tu‑Sa 11:00‑23:00. [source](https://www.openstreetmap.org/node/3172202148)
- **13:30 St. Peter's Church** – Riga’s oldest church (1209) with a tower offering city views; interior features Baroque décor. Hours: Mo‑Th,Su 10:00‑18:00; Fr‑Sa 10:00‑22:00. ticket €3. [source](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/45069779)
- **15:00 Museum of Decorative Arts and Design** – Located in the former St. George’s Church (1204); displays historic ceramics and contemporary Baltic design. Hours: Tu,Th‑Su 11:00‑17:00; We 11:00‑19:00. ticket €10. [source](https://www.openstreetmap.org/node/4813948103)
- **18:00 DOM** – Try the local cabbage dish **skābēti kāposti**. Open Mo‑Su 12:00‑23:00. [source](https://www.openstreetmap.org/node/3172202159)

### Day 3 – Sat 2027-05-15: South‑west Old Town
*Weather:* partly cloudy, 8‑20 °C – **not rainy**. Walking ~0.5 km.
- **09:00 Triangula Bastion** – Modern terrace built around an excavated fragment of Riga’s historic fortifications, offering panoramic city views. Free entry. Open 09:00‑22:00. [source](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Riga/Vecr%C4%ABga)
- **10:00 Art Museum Riga Bourse** – Housed in the historic Riga Stock Exchange building (est. 1920), it holds collections from ancient Egypt to contemporary Asian art. Hours: Tu‑Th,Sa‑Su 10:00‑18:00; Fr 10:00‑20:00. ticket price: see the website. [source](https://www.openstreetmap.org/relation/9248240)
- **11:30 Museum of the History of Riga and Navigation** – Part of the Dom Cathedral ensemble; traces Riga’s maritime and urban development since 1773. Hours: May‑Sep 10:00‑17:00; Oct‑Apr We‑Su 11:00‑17:00. ticket price: see the website. [source](https://www.openstreetmap.org/node/13721805929)
- **13:00 B Bārs Restorāns** – Casual lunch; try the local buckwheat dish **griķi**. [source](https://www.openstreetmap.org/node/452610878)
- **19:40 Depart from Riga** – airBaltic flight back to Helsinki.

## Food to try
- **Cottage cheese (biezpiens)** – A staple dairy product, often served with honey or fresh fruit. [source](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Latvia#Eat)
- **Kotlete** – Meat patties made from minced pork, commonly served with potatoes or grain sides. [source](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Latvia#Eat)
- **Skābēti kāposti** – Fermented cabbage, a typical side dish accompanying many Latvian meals. [source](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Latvia#Eat)
- **Griķi** – Buckwheat groats, widely used as a side or base for soups. [source](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Latvia#Eat)
- **Kūpināts siers** – Smoked cheese, often enjoyed with rye bread or as part of a cold platter. [source](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Latvia#Eat)
- **Gaileņu mērce** – Herb sauce made with dill and other herbs, used to flavor fish or potatoes. [source](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Latvia#Eat)
